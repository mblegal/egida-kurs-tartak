## Introduction

Friday, 23.04.2026, 15:30. Andrij is finishing his shift at band saw 2. Shift log ZMI-2026-048-02 is signed, package SO-FSC-002 is closed (12 beams sawn, 3 remain for Monday), and the 4W form from Thursday's escalation of package SO-FSC-003 is in the crew leader's folder. Andrij hangs his helmet and goggles on the rack and walks over to Marek. Marek: 'Andrij, good work this week. Forklift operator Krzysztof says he has understood your hand signals since the second package. Piotr Czerwiński saw the escalation form, well written. The shift log is legible, and you tell the 4 downtime types apart.' A pause. 'Monday morning you have the Module 2 final test. Friday afternoon is a good moment for a quick review. We'll sit down for 20 minutes, walk through the four areas, then two practical cases. I want you to go home with a clear picture: what you have under control, where there are still gaps. Monday before the test there is no time for learning, only for a calm refresher. This lesson is your compass.'

This lesson is a **summary of the 31 previous lessons of Module 2** organised around the **four competence areas of an operator in the crew**: (1) **material knowledge** from week 2, (2) **machine operation under supervision** from week 3, (3) **operational documentation** from week 4 Block 1, (4) **human relations and self-regulation** from week 4 Block 2. You get a **bullet list** for a quick scan before the test plus **two integration cases** in which several competences from different weeks combine into a single decision. The point is for you to see that Monday's test does not ask 'remember parameter X', it asks 'operator, what do you do in situation Y, in which 5 competences come into play at once'.

## Objectives

After this lesson:

1. You can list the **4 M2 operator competence areas** and know which week each comes from: area 1 (W2, l1-l8), area 2 (W3, l1-l8), area 3 (W4 Block 1, l1-l3), area 4 (W4 Block 2, l4-l6). W1 is a bridge, not a new area.
2. You know the **solid numerical test parameters**: band tension 2200-2400 PSI, guide rollers 3-4 mm, shift log min. 8 fields, sawing order min. 10 fields, 4W format, 3 forklift-operator gestures, 6 fatigue signals, 5 escalation levels, 4 downtime types T/M/O/P, 3 urgency categories, test threshold 70%.
3. You know **what distinguishes an operator in the crew from an M1 helper**: 5 competences (machine under supervision, signing off the sawing order, shift log, 4W escalation, self-regulation). Together they form the profile of a person to whom the crew leader entrusts a machine for 8 hours with supervision limited to a walkaround every 2 hours.
4. You can **apply 5 competences simultaneously** in a typical situation: FSC export sawing order + forklift operator + raw-material defect in the 6th hour of the shift, leading to 4W escalation + quarantine + shift log + rotation.
5. You know **how to prepare mentally for the test**: sleep 7-8 hours, eat breakfast, arrive 15 minutes early, read each question **twice**, for open-ended questions use the 4W format, do not leave a blank field.

### Area 1 - Material knowledge (week 2)

**What you know after W2:**

1. **You recognise 4 species**: Scots pine (light, resinous, 0.45 kg/dm³), Norway spruce (lighter, less resin, even growth rings), pedunculate oak (dark, porous, 0.70 kg/dm³), European beech (pinkish, homogeneous). **l1-l2 W2**.
2. **You determine 3 moisture levels** (PN-EN 13183): fresh 30-35%, semi-dry 18-22%, dry 12-15% structural / 6-10% joinery. You operate the Gann Hydromette Compact (10 mm probe insertion, reading after 3 s). **l3 W2**.
3. **You identify 8 defect types**: sound/loose knots, surface/end cracks (A=1/4 width, B=1/3, C=1/2) / internal, grain twist (>5° disqualifies A), blue stain (disqualifies joinery), split pith. **l4 W2**.
4. **You distinguish sortiments**: sawn timber (15-80 mm), structural beam (100x200, 150x200, 200x200), floorboard (25x150, 30x200), batten, parquet strip. **l5 W2**.
5. **You classify A/B/C/D** (EGIDA system) and map to PN-EN (A approx. S0, B approx. S1-S2). The **weakest-criterion rule**: one B-grade defect downgrades the board to B. **l6 W2**.
6. **You recognise the saws**: WoodMizer LT70 band saw (blade 40x1x4240 mm stellite, life 200 cuts spruce / 140 cuts oak), Storti circular saw, Paul Y-40M multi-rip saw (**a junior does NOT operate it**). **l7 W2**.
7. **You know the blade parameters**: tension 2200-2400 PSI, set 0.4 mm, tooth height 8 mm, pitch 22 mm pine / 28 mm oak. Guide rollers 3-4 mm. **l7-l8 W2**.

::: tip
**How to study for the test.** Area 1 is memory-based (tables, numbers, names): draw a table in your notebook with 4 species x 5 features plus 3 moisture levels plus 8 defects x 4 classes, copy it out three times by hand. Area 2 is procedural: write the 6-step band-saw procedure from memory and compare with your notes. Area 3 is documentary: fill in blank forms (sawing order + shift log + 4W) with your own data. Area 4 is decision-based: make a list of 15 situations ('I see a crack', 'my hands are shaking'), and for each one write down the decision. The cases in the test always map onto area 4.
:::

### Area 2 - Machine operation under supervision (week 3)

**What you know after W3:**

1. **You start up the band saw** in 6 steps: (a) guards plus riving knife 2-10 mm from the tooth, (b) visual band check, (c) upper guides 10 mm above the beam plus side guides 0.5 mm, (d) 10 s idle-run test plus listening, (e) running-hours entry, (f) 'ready' announcement to the crew leader. **l1 W3**.
2. **You adjust the tension** every 3 h (2200-2400 PSI), and the guides every 6 h. Tooth set every 200 cuts pine / 140 oak. Dullness = 3 A rise on the ammeter = band replacement. **l2 W3**.
3. **You cut under supervision**: pine 18-20 mm/s, spruce 16-18, oak 14-16, beech 12-14. Ammeter green 12-16 A, yellow 16-19 warning, red >=20 STOP. Rule: strange sound + yellow + wave pattern = STOP + escalation. **l3 W3**.
4. **You operate the circular saw** (Storti DDM-200, 2 blades 350 mm): riving knife 2-10 mm, guard <=5 mm, short push stick <300 mm plus narrow push stick <150 mm, position on the side never on the discharge line. A junior does NOT operate the Paul Y-40M multi-rip saw. **l4 W3**.
5. **You operate the edger and the 4-sided planer/moulder** Weinig Powermat 700: 6 rollers x 2000 N = **12,000 N** (crushes a hand in 0.1 s), push stick 500 mm, the hand never closer than 500 mm to the first roller. Planing 'against the grain' = chips at 20-30 m/s. **l5 W3**.
6. **You sort by 4 criteria**: thickness ±1 mm, length tolerance 10-50 mm, species, class A/B/C/D (weakest-criterion rule). Label: 2 species letters + 1 class letter + 6 date digits + 3 batch digits (SO-A-260420-012). Stack <=2 m, 1 m spacing. **l6 W3**.
7. **You load the kiln** under the master's direction: stickers 20-25 mm every 600-800 mm, vertical columns not offset, gaps 150/100/300 mm. Mantra: '**I load, I close, I report - I do NOT program cycles**'. **l7 W3**.
8. **You measure after drying and reclassify**: 4 positions A/B/C/D (PN-EN 13183-2) min. 500 mm from the end, spread <2%. Reclassification = a new label with the '**r**' suffix (SO-B-260420-012-r). Teaching rule: '**class is not fixed in time**'. **l8 W3**.

### Area 3 - Operational documentation (week 4 Block 1)

**What you know after W4 Block 1:**

1. **You read and sign off the sawing order** in 5 steps: read the whole thing twice, underline 3 critical parameters (species + dimension + priority), ask questions in the 'I see X, I understand Y' pattern, sign both copies, file the yellow one in the folder. 4 non-conformity types require escalation. **l1 W4**.
2. **You keep the shift log**: 8 main fields (number, date, operator, machine, running hours, m³ in, m³ out, efficiency ratio) + a table of the 4 downtime types **T/M/O/P** (Technical/Material/Organizational/Personnel) + a band table + a 3-part fault field (**what/when/what I did**, no diagnosis). A two-way flow: white copy to crew leader to dispatcher, green copy to relief operator. **l2 W4**.
3. **You recognise 3 standards systems**: **PN-EN 1611-1** (visual softwood S0-S4), **PN-EN 14081-1** (strength-graded C14-C50 softwood plus D18-D70 hardwood, MicroTec Goldeneye 702 scanner), **FSC + PEFC** (parallel supply chains, FSC-C012345 for EGIDA, mixing = loss of certificate = 150,000-300,000 PLN). 3 junior mistakes: 'it's probably certified', confusing S0-S4 with C14-C50, substituting FSC for PEFC (IKEA accepts FSC only). Mantra: **certification without proof = no certification**. **l3 W4**.

### Area 4 - Human relations and self-regulation (week 4 Block 2)

**What you know after W4 Block 2:**

1. **You work with the forklift operator**: 3 gestures (PN-ISO 7010) - STOP (palm above the head), BACK (arm bent, motion toward yourself), READY (palms crossed in an X). Manoeuvre zone 3 m (braking distance 2.1-2.4 m + buffer). **Do NOT step between the machine and the load** (PIP statistic: 48% of accidents = a standing operator hit). **l4 W4**.
2. **You escalate in 4W**: **What** (symptom factually) / **Where** (machine + beam) / **When** (±5 min) / **What I did** (usually STOP). 30-45 seconds. **3 urgency categories**: IMMEDIATELY (fire/accident - phone call + presence), END OF SHIFT (doubt about a batch - shift log + verbal), IN WRITING (a suggestion - form, response within 30 days). **5 levels**: operator to crew leader to shift master to production manager to director. Skip one level + a transparency SMS. **Describe symptoms, not diagnose**. **l5 W4**.
3. **You manage your own rhythm**: **6 fatigue signals** with self-diagnosis tests (hand tremor - paper test, squinting - label at 2 m, slowed reactions, counting errors - count down from 100 by 7, dry throat, stiff neck - rotation 70-80° vs 50-60°). **3 signals at once = mandatory micropause**. **5 exercises** every 90 min (neck rotation 8+8, wrists 10+10, shoulders 10+10, forward bend 15+15, 20-20-20 rule). CIOP-PIB curve 2019-2023: 7th hour **+150%**, 8th hour **+250%**. EGIDA rhythm: 2 breaks + 4 micropauses = 57 min non-sawing in 8 h. **Reporting 'I cannot cope'** in 4W is a competence, not weakness. **l6 W4**.

::: warning
**Five operator-helper differences.** M1 helper: (a) passes material, (b) carries out verbal orders, (c) does not keep a shift log, (d) runs over with 'something happened', (e) 'pushes through'. M2 operator: (a) operates a machine under supervision, (b) signs off the sawing order, (c) keeps an 8-field shift log + T/M/O/P, (d) escalates in 4W with urgency categories and 5 levels, (e) recognises 3 fatigue signals and asks for a micropause. **The operator takes responsibility for the process, the helper for the moment**. The test checks whether you can already behave like an operator.
:::

### Integration case 1 - Monday morning, FSC oak export order

**Situation.** Monday 6:45, band saw 2. Marek hands you sawing order DP-2026-050: client Bouwhout van Doorn B.V. Breda, pedunculate oak 50x200x3000 mm C24, 80 pcs, FSC 100% FSC-C012345, priority A (by 15:00). At 7:10 Krzysztof Zagrodny brings package SO-DĄB-FSC-210420-004 (1820 kg) on a Toyota 8FG25. At 13:15 (6th hour) in beam 42 you notice **an internal crack 12x4 mm with a rusty deposit**. At the same time you feel: a tremor in your right hand, squinting, stiff neck. Marek is in a management meeting until 14:00.

**Integration of 5 competences from 4 areas:**

1. **Area 3**: you sign off the sawing order (3 critical parameters: pedunculate oak / 50x200x3000 C24 / priority A), you ask 'I see FSC-C012345, I understand the package comes from the FSC yard, is that right?', Marek confirms, you sign both copies.
2. **Area 4 (forklift operator)**: at reception you stand in the 3 m zone, use STOP/BACK/READY gestures, and check the FSC label in the FSC yard - **it is there**.
3. **Area 2**: feed 14-16 mm/s oak, ammeter 12-16 A, oak-blade pitch 28 mm, tension 2300 PSI, guides 3-4 mm.
4. **Area 1**: you recognise internal crack + rusty deposit = **C24 disqualified** (PN-EN 14081-1 requires full internal integrity); the rusty deposit = a defect present in the living tree 2-3 years ago.
5. **Area 4 (escalation)**: Marek is absent. Category **END OF SHIFT** (machine safe, the order continues with the remaining 79 beams). Phone call to Piotr Czerwiński (**one-level skip**) + SMS to Marek. 4W report: 'What - internal crack 12x4 mm + rusty deposit in beam 42 of package SO-DĄB-FSC-004, C24 disqualified. Where - band saw 2. When - 13:15. What I did - STOP on this beam, quarantine with a red label, continuing from beam 43, notified through Piotr because Marek is in a meeting'.
6. **Area 4 (self-regulation)**: **3 signals at once** = mandatory 5-min micropause (5 exercises + 20-20-20 rule). Shift-log entry: '13:15 crack beam 42 escalation Piotr, 13:16 three signals 5-min micropause, 13:21 return, beams 43-60 feed reduced'.

**Result**: 60 beams out of 79 by 15:00, beam 42 in quarantine, shift log with 2 decision entries. The test checks whether you can **describe this situation in 4W and list the 5 competences**.

### Integration case 2 - Friday end of shift, 3 signals, sorting, shift log

**Situation.** Friday 14:15 (7th hour of the shift, curve +150%). You are finishing package SO-SW-A-230420-008, and on the yard there are 12 spruce boards 45x150x3000 for classification. You feel: a tremor in both hands (not only the right), a counting error (notes 11:30 vs clock 11:20), dry throat despite 2 l of water. Marek is at the crew shift log.

**Integration of 4 competences from 3 areas:**

1. **Area 4 (self-regulation)**: **3 signals = a mandatory micropause or a 4W report**. Not 'I push through for 45 minutes' (helper's mistake). You go to Marek.
2. **Area 4 (4W)**: 'What - three signals: tremor in both hands, counting error, dry throat despite 2 l of water. Where - the yard, package SO-SW-A-008, 12 boards. When - 14:15. What I did - stopped sorting, I am asking for rotation or a 10 min micropause. Class A sorting requires sharp vision; with my current vision I will disqualify a good A board as B'. Marek: **10 min micropause + rotation to the shift log**, sorting for Sofía on Tuesday.
3. **Area 3 (shift log)**: you fill in ZMI-2026-048-05 - 8 fields, downtime table (T 0, M 15 keys were missing, O 0, **P 10 micropause**), band table, fault field empty. Notes field: '14:15 three signals, 10-min micropause + rotation, Marek decision. Sorting SO-SW-A-008 Tuesday morning'.
4. **Area 1**: you check that you are not leaving an unresolved classification - weakest-criterion rule: better to sort in the morning with rested eyes. Notebook A6: 'Tuesday 6:30 SO-SW-A-008 sort 12 boards, fresh eyes'.

**Result**: 15:00 shift finished, shift log complete, micropause documented, sorting rescheduled. No accident, no wrong classification, no 'I pushed through'. The test checks whether you can **tell "I push through" from "I request rotation"** and record it in the shift log.

### Solid test parameters

These must be **exactly in your memory**, not 'more or less':

- Band tension **2200-2400 PSI**; guide rollers **3-4 mm**.
- Saw feed: pine **18-20** / oak **14-16** mm/s. Ammeter green **12-16 A**, red **>=20 A STOP**.

## Key terms

Po polsku / In English / En español / Українською:

- **Operator w brygadzie / Operator in the crew / Operador de brigada / Оператор у бригаді** - an M2 worker with competences in operating a machine under supervision, reading sawing orders, keeping a shift log, 4W escalation and self-regulation; crew-leader supervision limited to a walkaround every 2 hours; differs from the M1 helper by five competences.
- **Cztery obszary kompetencji M2 / Four M2 competence areas / Cuatro áreas de competencia M2 / Чотири сфери компетенції М2** - material knowledge (W2), machine operation under supervision (W3), operational documentation (W4 Block 1), human relations and self-regulation (W4 Block 2); the final test asks about the integration of all four.
- **Case integracyjny / Integration case / Caso de integración / Інтеграційний кейс** - a scenario combining 4-6 competences from different weeks of the course into a single operator decision situation; the standard for open-ended questions of the M2 test.
- **Wzorzec decyzyjny M2 / M2 decision pattern / Patrón de decisión M2 / Шаблон рішення М2** - observation, problem classification, 4W communication, shift-log entry, continuing with a solution; the core of the operator's competence, repeated across all cases.
- **Pięć różnic operator-pomocnik / Five operator-helper differences / Cinco diferencias operador-ayudante / П'ять відмінностей оператор-помічник** - (1) machine under supervision vs passing material, (2) signing off the sawing order vs verbal orders, (3) shift log vs the crew leader's memory, (4) 4W escalation vs running over with 'something happened', (5) micropause vs 'I push through'.
- **Parametr pewny przed testem / Solid test parameter / Parámetro seguro para el examen / Певний параметр перед тестом** - a number or procedure you know exactly (not 'more or less'): 2200-2400 PSI, 3-4 mm, 8 shift-log fields, 10 sawing-order fields, 4W, 3 gestures, 6 signals, 5 levels, 4 downtime types, 3 urgency categories, 70% threshold.
- **Strategia testowa M2 / M2 test strategy / Estrategia del examen M2 / Тестова стратегія М2** - sleep 7-8 h, eat breakfast, arrive 15 min early, read each question twice, for open-ended questions write in the 4W format, when uncertain write what you know in the direction of the question (do not leave a blank field).

## Link to practice

At the training plant you will get three exercises. **First: a 20-question quiz across the 4 areas** (5 questions per area), closed-form A/B/C/D, 30 minutes, threshold 14/20 (70%). Below the threshold = repeat the weakest area. **Second: a case simulation** - the trainer hands you a card with a situation (e.g. 'Monday morning, PEFC spruce C24 sawing order, the forklift operator delivers the package, at 10:30 you find blue stain'), you answer in writing in 4W and list 4-6 competences; the trainer grades on 5 criteria (recognition, urgency category, 4W format, record, order); threshold 4/5. **Third: a review of last week's shift log** - the trainer asks about 3 entries, 'why did you write it that way?'; grades compliance with the procedure (3-part format, T/M/O/P, symptom description); threshold 3/3.

## Trainer notes

This lesson is a **meta-lesson**: it does not teach a new competence, it organises the knowledge from the previous 31 lessons. For migrants it is crucial, because without an explicit map they perceive the test as 'a set of random questions', whereas with the map they see it as 'a check on four areas, each of which I know from specific lessons'.

Three difficulties:

1. **Stretching the recap into theory**. Learners try to 'clarify' parameters from W2/W3 that they did not understand. The trainer enforces discipline: 'this is a recap, not a lesson; fill in gaps from W3 l4 with the W3 l4 material over the weekend, not now'. Learners with gaps are referred to the materials, not to improvised explanations.
2. **Feeling overwhelmed by volume**. 24-32 bullets + 2 cases = a sense of 'I will not remember this'. The trainer shows that **the list is a review**, not fresh material; each bullet refers back to a specific lesson. Over the weekend the learner verifies 'OK / gap' and fills the gaps from the materials.
3. **Neglecting the cases**. Migrant learners focus on the list (the memory area) and skip the cases. The trainer emphasises: **the cases are the heart of the M2 test**, 30-40% of the score. Area 4 of decision competences is responsible for promotion from helper to operator.

Closing exercise (20 min): **a test-day simulation**. The trainer hands out a fictitious 'invitation to Monday 8:00 test', and learners answer 5 questions in turn: 'what time do you go to sleep on Sunday?', 'what for breakfast?', 'how do you get to the room?', 'what if you do not know the answer to question 15?', 'what if time is running out and you still have 5 questions?'. Pass = 5/5 in line with the test strategy. Fail = repeat on Monday morning, 30 min before the test.
