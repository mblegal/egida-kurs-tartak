## Introduction

This is the **final lesson of M3 and of the entire EGIDA sawmill course**. Over the previous 95 lessons you have learned: in M1 the hazards, material, processes and helper organisation (November 2025), in M2 the bandsaw, sorting, drying and the team of an operator under supervision (March 2026), in M3 the responsibility, systems, processes and organisation of an independent operator (May-June 2026). In l7 you carried out a 9-month retrospective (8 rules + 3 meta-rules + 5 competencies + 3 cases + voices of 6 people). Today **you verify whether you can apply this in new situations**.

The M3 final test covers **all three modules in a 20/30/50 proportion** (20% M1, 30% M2, 50% M3) and consists of **3 parts**, totalling **108 points**, duration **120 minutes**, passing threshold **70% = 76 points**:

1. **Multiple-choice questions** (30 questions x 2 pts = **60 pts**, **60 min**). Coverage: M1 (6 questions) + M2 (9 questions) + M3 (15 questions). Multiple choice, 4 answers, one correct. No consultations, independent work, on paper.
2. **Decision scenarios** (3 scenarios x 10 pts = **30 pts**, **30 min**). One scenario from each module (M1 OHS + M2 bandsaw/material + M3 coordination/complaint). You describe **what you do and why**, scoring criteria: safety (3 pts) + sequence of actions (3 pts) + reference to a rule (2 pts) + realism (2 pts).
3. **Practical tasks** (6 tasks x 3 pts = **18 pts**, **30 min**). Mixed tasks: filling EGIDA forms (KDP-001 + KAO-001), citing legal articles (KC + KP + statutes), calculations (yield, margin, career ROI), fault recognition via 3 senses, escalation structure, legal citation of defective tolerance.

**Whoever passes (76+ pts)** receives the **EGIDA Sawmill Course Completion Certificate – Module 3 Independent Operator** (10-year retention in personnel files), is admitted to the **practical exam** (independent 8-hour shift on P3 LT70 under external observation by the EGIDA commission + foreman + internal auditor, scheduled +2 weeks after the theoretical test), and to **solo export orders** (first batch of FSC oak to the Netherlands Bouwhout van Doorn, 12 m³, priority A).

**Whoever does not pass** repeats selected M3 lessons (Marek's decision after analysing gaps from the test), takes the make-up test in **3 weeks**, and in the meantime works as an **extended M2** (independent under M3 supervision on LT30, but without LT70 and without supervisory tasks).

This lesson contains the **student test** (questions without answers) and the **answer key + scoring criteria + practical exam scenario** (in a separate Phase 4 artefact for the instructor). The student receives only the „Test" section + a short mental instruction from l7.

## Objectives

After this lesson:

- **You have passed the M3 test** with at least 70% (76/108 pts).
- You know **in which areas you have gaps** (if you did not pass – which lessons to return to; if you did – where to deepen through practice).
- You understand that **passing the test is not the end of development**. Passing = admission to the practical exam and solo orders. Full M3 maturity grows over 6-12 months of post-course practice.
- You understand the **career structure after the course**: 3 specialisation paths (UDT+SEP, BTM drying internship, UPP studies), with financial and time dimensions, letting you plan the next 2-5 years.
- You are prepared for the **practical exam** (independent shift) in +14 days, and you know what to expect.

## Content

### Student instructions

**Part 1 – multiple-choice questions (60 min, 30 questions x 2 pts = 60 pts)**:
- Read each question **twice** before marking.
- Mark **one** answer (A, B, C, D).
- If you do not know, **do not leave it blank** – mark the most likely answer. Blank = 0 pts automatically.
- If you correct your answer, **cross out the old one clearly** and mark the new one.
- Manage your time: 60 minutes / 30 questions = 2 minutes per question on average.

**Part 2 – decision scenarios (30 min, 3 scenarios x 10 pts = 30 pts)**:
- The instructor hands out a sheet with three scenarios.
- You answer in writing: **what you do and why**, minimum 4 sentences, maximum 10.
- Scoring criteria: (a) **immediate safety** (3 pts) + (b) **correct sequence of actions** (3 pts) + (c) **reference to a rule / course lesson** (2 pts) + (d) **realism and specificity** (2 pts).
- Use the **4W** scheme: What I did / Where / When / What's next.

**Part 3 – practical tasks (30 min, 6 tasks x 3 pts = 18 pts)**:
- Mixed tasks: filling forms (blank sheets KDP-001, KAO-001), calculations (yield, margin, ROI), citing legal articles, sensory recognition (verbal description from material), 5-rung escalation structure.
- Each task 3 pts: 3/3 (fully correct), 2/3 (minor error), 1/3 (correct direction with substantial gaps), 0/3 (incorrect or blank).

**Integrity rule**: the test is **individual**. Copying, cheat sheets, phones, consulting a colleague: **0 pts for that part** + disciplinary conversation with the production manager.

## Key terms

- **test końcowy M3** (PL) / **M3 final test** (EN) / **examen final de M3** (ES) / **підсумковий тест M3** (UK): course completion test, 108 pts, threshold 70% = 76 pts
- **proporcja 20/30/50** (PL) / **20/30/50 proportion** (EN) / **proporción 20/30/50** (ES) / **пропорція 20/30/50** (UK): distribution of questions in the test – 20% M1, 30% M2, 50% M3
- **egzamin praktyczny** (PL) / **practical exam** (EN) / **examen práctico** (ES) / **практичний іспит** (UK): independent 8-hour shift on LT70 under commission observation, +14 days from the theoretical test
- **scenariusz decyzyjny** (PL) / **decision scenario** (EN) / **escenario de decisión** (ES) / **сценарій прийняття рішень** (UK): part 2 of the test, 3 scenarios x 10 pts, 4-8 sentences in writing
- **zadanie praktyczne** (PL) / **practical task** (EN) / **tarea práctica** (ES) / **практичне завдання** (UK): part 3 of the test, 6 tasks x 3 pts, filling forms + calculations + citations
- **reguła uczciwości** (PL) / **integrity rule** (EN) / **regla de integridad** (ES) / **правило чесності** (UK): ban on cheating, consequence 0 pts + disciplinary conversation
- **Akt Ukończenia Kursu** (PL) / **Course Completion Certificate** (EN) / **Acta de Finalización del Curso** (ES) / **Акт завершення курсу** (UK): document issued after passing the test and the practical exam, 10-year retention in personnel files
- **M2 rozszerzony** (PL) / **extended M2** (EN) / **M2 extendido** (ES) / **розширений M2** (UK): status of an operator who did not pass the M3 test – independent work on LT30, without LT70, without supervisory tasks
- **solo zlecenie eksportowe** (PL) / **solo export order** (EN) / **pedido de exportación solo** (ES) / **індивідуальне експортне замовлення** (UK): first full order of an M3 operator after the practical exam, usually FSC priority A for a foreign client
- **EGIDA-RPW-001** (PL) / **EGIDA-RPW-001** (EN) / **EGIDA-RPW-001** (ES) / **EGIDA-RPW-001** (UK): Accident Report, EGIDA form for incidents with injury
