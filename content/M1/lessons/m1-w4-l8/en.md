## Introduction

This is **the last lesson of M1**. In the previous 29 you learned the hazards, the material, the processes and the organization. In l7 you condensed the knowledge into 12 great principles. Today you **check whether you remember**.

The test has **3 parts** and lasts 120 minutes:

1. **Closed-ended questions** (30 questions × 2 pts = 60 pts, 60 min). Multiple choice, 4 answers, 1 correct. No consultation, individual, on paper. Coverage: **T1 OHS (8), T2 Material (7), T3 Processes (8), T4 Organization (7)**.
2. **Decision scenarios** (3 scenarios × 10 pts = 30 pts, 30 min). The trainer reads a situation, you **describe what you do and why**. Written or oral answer (trainer's decision).
3. **Practical tasks** (6 tasks × varying points = 20 pts, 30 min). Recognizing gestures, pronouncing commands, formulating a 5W1H report, selecting PPE, marking log defects, calculating a transport limit.

**Total 110 pts**. Pass threshold: **77 pts (70%)**. Whoever passes moves to M2. Whoever fails repeats selected M1 lessons and takes a retake test in 2 weeks.

The test **is not a threat**. It is information: „am I ready to step onto a real sawmill floor?". If the test detects gaps, better fix them now than have the missing fact end in an accident on a real floor.

This lesson is **the material for the test** and at the same time **the material of the test**. The trainee receives this file; the trainer gets in a separate Phase 4 artefact the **answer key** and the assessment criteria for scenarios and practical tasks.

## Objectives

After this lesson:

- **You passed the M1 test** with at least 70% (77/110 pts).
- You know **in which areas you have gaps** (if you did not pass, which lessons to go back to).
- You understand that **passing the test ≠ the end of learning**. A pass is the minimum to step onto the floor. Real competence grows through months of practice.
- You understand that **after M1 come M2 and M3**. M1 = helper. M2 = specific workstations (operator, stacker, storekeeper). M3 = practice + certification.

## Content

### Trainee instructions

**Part 1 – closed-ended questions (60 min, 30 questions × 2 pts = 60 pts)**:
- Read the question.
- Mark **one** answer (A, B, C, D).
- If you do not know, **do not guess at random** – mark „don't know" (0 pts, but information for the trainer on where you have a gap).
- Do not leave blanks. Marking is required.
- If you correct an answer, **strike out the old one clearly** and mark the new one.

**Part 2 – decision scenarios (30 min, 3 scenarios × 10 pts = 30 pts)**:
- The trainer reads the situation aloud (or you have it on paper).
- You answer: **what you do and why**. Minimum 3-4 sentences, maximum 10.
- Assessment criteria: (a) immediate safety (3 pts), (b) correct sequence of actions (3 pts), (c) reference to an M1 principle / lesson (2 pts), (d) realism and specificity (2 pts).

**Part 3 – practical tasks (30 min, 6 tasks = 20 pts)**:
- The trainer shows material (a gesture, pictogram, log picture) or gives a command („pronounce the command", „formulate a report").
- You answer physically (perform the gesture, pronounce the command) or in writing (5W1H form).
- Each task has its own **scoring rubric**, described below.

**Honesty rule**: the test is **individual**. Copying from a colleague, using a principle card, phone: **0 pts** for that part + disciplinary talk. The purpose of the test is **your development**, not a pass „on paper". A migrant who cheats and steps onto the floor without knowledge is in hospital in a month.

### Part 3: 6 practical tasks

**Task 1 (3 pts) – gesture recognition**

The trainer shows 5 gestures one by one (from photos or live). You name each: STOP, PODAJ, CZEKAJ, ODSUŃ SIĘ, COFNIJ. **Scoring**: 0.5 pts per correct name + 0.5 pts for recognizing all 5 in <30 sec. Maximum 3 pts.

**Task 2 (4 pts) – command pronunciation**

The trainer gives you 5 situations in writing (e.g. „you see a stack about to fall on a colleague"). For each you shout the **correct Polish command** loudly, clearly, with appropriate intonation. Situations: (a) stack falling → UWAGA!, (b) operator about to switch on a saw with a loose blade → STOP!, (c) colleague in a forklift zone → ODSUŃ SIĘ!, (d) hand cart rolling by itself → ZABLOKUJ!, (e) a jammed log, the operator says „let go!" → PUŚĆ! or for the reverse motion TRZYMAJ!. **Scoring**: 0.8 pts per command (accuracy + loudness + intonation). Maximum 4 pts.

**Task 3 (4 pts) – 5W1H report formulation**

The trainer reads you a situation: „At 11:30, while feeding an oak board to the saw, the board slipped from the operator's grip and flew 20 cm past your head. Nobody was hurt, but it was a close near-miss. Witnesses were operator Andrzej Nowak and foreman Paweł Kowalski, who was passing by at that moment". **Your task**: write a minor-incident-log report using the **5W1H** formula. **Scoring**: 0.5 pts for each of the 6 components (what/when/where/who/why/how) + 1 pt for quality (short, factual, Polish, signed). Maximum 4 pts.

**Task 4 (3 pts) – PPE selection**

The trainer shows the scenario: „Today you will help with cutting an oak log on the band saw. After the cut, manually carry boards 3 metres to the stack". You name **all** PPE you put on in **correct order** of use: (a) helmet, (b) S3 boots, (c) safety goggles, (d) hearing protection (ear muffs), (e) FFP3 mask (because oak), (f) **gloves** – and here is the trap: gloves are **removed during the cutting** (rotating machine) and **put on again** for manual transport. **Scoring**: 0.5 pts per PPE item + 1 pt for correctly resolving the glove trap. Maximum 3 pts.

**Task 5 (3 pts) – log defect marking**

The trainer shows you a drawing / photograph of a log with 3 visible defects (e.g. lengthwise crack, blue stain, large sound knots). You: (a) name each defect, (b) decide if the log is suitable for construction / pallets / burning, (c) propose a colour marking (red = rejection, yellow = caution, green = OK). **Scoring**: 1 pt for each of 3 defects correctly identified + 0 or 1 pt for correct marking decision. Maximum 3 pts.

**Task 6 (3 pts) – transport limit calculation**

The trainer gives you a situation: „You need to carry a pine beam 3 m × 10 cm × 10 cm (dry pine density ~450 kg/m³). You are a man. Can you alone? If yes, can you repeat for an hour? If not, what solution?". You: (a) calculate the volume (3 × 0.1 × 0.1 = 0.03 m³), (b) the mass (0.03 × 450 = 13.5 kg), (c) compare with the limit M 30 kg at one time / 8 000 kg per shift, (d) conclusion: OK at one time; with many repetitions per shift you check the total mass. **Scoring**: 1 pt for mass calculation + 1 pt for comparison with the limit + 1 pt for the correct practical decision. Maximum 3 pts.

**Part 2 – decision scenarios (assessment criteria)**:

**Scenario 1 (Marek's accident)**:
- (a) immediate safety (3 pts): call 112 / do not move Marek if spine suspected / compress bleeding with sterile dressing from the workstation first-aid kit.
- (b) correct sequence (3 pts): 112 → call for help (shout / send Vitaliy for the foreman) → first aid → secure the zone (so nobody else falls in).
- (c) reference to M1 principles (2 pts): Labour Code art. 234 (duty to give help), art. 211 pt 6 (notification), escalation pyramid (112 for a serious case + foreman in parallel), 4 documents (log entry after the event).
- (d) realism (2 pts): do not move Marek, do not remove the board (it may be blocking bleeding), do not run off alone, stay until the ambulance arrives.

**Scenario 2 (foreman and forklift)**:
- (a) safety (3 pts): you refuse, you do not drive without UDT.
- (b) sequence (3 pts): calmly explain → propose an alternative (hand cart, another authorized person) → if the foreman insists, log it in the minor-incident log.
- (c) reference to M1 (2 pts): Labour Code art. 210 § 1 (right to refuse), art. 210 § 5 (ban on punishment), Regulation of the Minister of Economy 2000 (forklifts require UDT WJO class II).
- (d) realism (2 pts): use Polish words („Foreman, I have no UDT authorization...", „I am logging it..."), do not fight emotionally, preserve the relationship.

**Scenario 3 (sound near-miss)**:
- (a) safety (3 pts): signal the operator (STOP gesture + eye contact), not words (he cannot hear).
- (b) sequence (3 pts): STOP gesture → operator switches off → brief assessment (operator + you) → call the foreman → 5W1H entry in the log.
- (c) reference to M1 (2 pts): principle 5 (2-sec decision), principle 9 (gestures), principle 11 (escalation), principle 12 (Heinrich 1:30:300:3000, unusual sound = near-miss).
- (d) realism (2 pts): do not ignore „old machines", do not let Oksana's words silence you, use the hypothesis „I think it is a bearing", let the foreman verify.

**Part 3 – practical tasks (scoring notes)**:
- **Task 1 (gestures)**: see „Recognition of 5 gestures". 0.5 pts × 5 + 0.5 pts for pace (<30 sec). Accuracy criterion: correct name; language variants accepted (PODAJ = DAJ = DAWAJ).
- **Task 2 (commands)**: 0.8 pts × 5. Assessed: accuracy (correct command for situation), loudness (audible from 5 m), intonation (sharp, short, not sing-song).
- **Task 3 (5W1H)**: 6 × 0.5 pts + 1 pt quality. Required: what (board slipped from grip and flew 20 cm past head), when (11:30), where (saw workstation, feeding), who (operator Andrzej Nowak, foreman Paweł Kowalski), why (hypothesis: dry grip, wet board, loose grip), how (close near-miss, 20 cm, no contact). Quality: conciseness, signature, date.
- **Task 4 (PPE)**: 6 × 0.5 pts = 3 pts, of which 1 pt for the glove trap. The order from helmet to mask is not critical, but removing gloves at the machine + putting them back for transport is critical.
- **Task 5 (log defects)**: 3 × 1 pt for defects, of which 1 general pt for marking. Defects depend on the image: e.g. lengthwise crack (yellow, careful cut), blue stain (yellow, pallets not construction), large sound knots (yellow, secondary construction).
- **Task 6 (calculation)**: 1 pt mass 13.5 kg, 1 pt comparison to 30 kg (OK one-off), 1 pt awareness of the 8 000 kg daily limit = 593 beams (above that use cart).

### After the test – feedback

The trainer returns the tests **within 24 h**. Each trainee gets:
- The total score.
- The list of **wrong** questions with lesson numbers to revisit.
- Scenario criteria where points were lost.
- Practical-task hints if something went poorly.

**Who scored ≥77 pts (70%)**: moves to M2.
**Who scored <77 pts**: **revision** of selected lessons + a retake test in 2 weeks. The revision is done with the trainer individually or in a small group of 2-4 (depending on how many did not pass).

The EGIDA foundation keeps statistics: **on average 75% of trainees pass on the first try**. Failing is a **norm for migrants**, not a reason for shame. The retake usually goes better because the trainee knows where the gaps are.

::: info
**Why the 70% threshold, not 50% and not 90%**. **50%** is too low – a trainee at 50% may not know half of the principles, which in a sawmill means an accident within a month. **90%** is too high – first month of learning in a foreign language with fully translated terminology; unrealistic for a migrant. **70%** is the compromise: you have most principles in your head, gaps are localized (not systemic) and can be filled by practice + individual foreman support. The EGIDA foundation uses the same threshold in the MAG welding course and the carpentry course.
:::

## Notatki dla trenera

**Pedagogical goal**: **reliably assess** M1 mastery + **give the trainee feedback** on gaps. The test is not „life or death" – it is **diagnostics**. After the test the trainee knows what he knows and does not know and has a realistic plan for continued learning (revision of specific lessons, M2, practice).

**120-minute rhythm** (as the trainer running the test):

- **0–5 min**: instructions. Announce the structure (3 parts, 60+30+30 min), scoring (60+30+20 = 110), threshold (77). Self-reliance rules. Hand out materials.
- **5–65 min**: **Part 1 – closed-ended questions** (60 min). Trainees work alone. The trainer walks around the room, answers only technical questions („I do not understand word X"), not substantive ones. After 60 min collects the sheets.
- **65–95 min**: **Part 2 – decision scenarios** (30 min). The trainer reads scenario 1 slowly, loudly, twice. The trainees have 10 min to write (or answer orally, depending on the group). Same for scenarios 2 and 3.
- **95–120 min**: **Part 3 – practical tasks** (25 min). The trainer leads the 6 tasks one by one with the group. For task 1 (gestures) trainees line up in a semicircle, the trainer shows a gesture, each names it in turn. For task 2 (commands) similarly. For tasks 3-6 hand out paper and let them answer individually.

**Within 24 hours after the test**: the trainer grades (~15-20 min per test), prepares an individual report (list of wrong questions + scenario comments + practical-task hints). Returned to trainees the next day.

**Common trainer mistake (1)**: **a too lenient grading**. The trainee marked B instead of A – the trainer „forgives" because „he essentially understands". No. **70% is the minimum**. If the trainee got 11 out of 30 wrong (63%), that is a **reason for revision**, not acceptance. A real sawmill floor will not „forgive" missing knowledge.

**Common trainer mistake (2)**: **grading scenarios subjectively**. The trainee writes „I would help the colleague and call the foreman" – the trainer gives 10 pts because „mostly correct". But missing: 112, correct sequence, reference to art. 234, realism. **Correction**: use the 4-criterion rubric (safety + sequence + reference + realism), 2-3 pts each. Sum. Avoid „mostly".

**Practical recommendation**: print **A4 sheets with the questions** (trainees mark in pencil), separately **scenario forms** (lines to write on), separately **trainer's grading sheet** (30 questions + 3 scenarios + 6 tasks + points column + comment space). Keep grading sheets **for 5 years** (Minister of Labour Regulation of 10.12.2018 on documentation).

**Link to M2**: „Module 2 – specific sawmill workstations". The first M2 lesson (m2-w1-l1): „Why we choose a workstation" (self-assessment + map of 8 stations + career paths). After M1 the trainee chooses which M2 path to focus on – saw operator (6-month path to UDT), storekeeper (fast track, 2-3 months), stacker (low entry barrier, physically hard), sorting-line operator (automation), quality control (for careful observers). The choice is not final; it can be changed mid-M2 if the path does not fit.
