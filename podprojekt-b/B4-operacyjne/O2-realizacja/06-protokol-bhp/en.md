# PROTOCOL OF OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY BRIEFING

**Individual copy of the Course Participant** taking part in the practical Course "Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill), organised free of charge by Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation) for the benefit of foreign nationals lawfully residing on the territory of the Republic of Poland.

---

## Introductory information

This protocol certifies that an occupational health and safety briefing has been delivered, the content and form of which complies with the requirements of the Rozporządzenie Ministra Gospodarki i Pracy z dnia 27 lipca 2004 r. w sprawie szkolenia w dziedzinie bezpieczeństwa i higieny pracy (Regulation of the Minister of Economy and Labour of 27 July 2004 on training in the field of occupational health and safety). The protocol is drawn up in two counterparts: one remains in the Course records kept by the Foundation, the other is delivered to the Course Participant. Both counterparts are identical as to their content.

The briefing is delivered by an OHS (Occupational Health and Safety) instructor holding the qualifications required by law. Participation in the briefing is a mandatory condition for admitting the Course Participant to the practical part of the Course held at the sawmill plant.

## Reference to the project

The Course "Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill) was created as a result of the potential developed in the framework of the project "Budowa fundamentów wsparcia cudzoziemców na rynku pracy w województwie opolskim" (Building the foundations of support for foreign nationals on the labour market in the Opolskie voivodeship), implemented by Stowarzyszenie SMART (lead partner) in cooperation with Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (partner) in the years 2026-2027 with funding from the European Social Fund Plus. The Course itself is not financed from the project funds and does not constitute a project activity; it is an independent statutory undertaking of the Foundation, conducted free of charge for the benefit of foreign nationals.
