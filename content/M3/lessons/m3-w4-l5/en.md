## Shift handover, theory

### Legal basis of operator responsibility

An M3 operator on a solo shift is legally responsible for the machine, production quality, and the station's OH&S. The Labour Code specifies two pillars of this responsibility:

> **Art. 100 § 1 Kodeksu pracy.** Pracownik jest obowiązany wykonywać pracę sumiennie i starannie oraz stosować się do poleceń przełożonych, które dotyczą pracy, jeżeli nie są one sprzeczne z przepisami prawa lub umową o pracę.

Explanatory translation: The employee is obliged to perform work conscientiously and carefully and to follow the supervisors' instructions concerning work, unless they are contrary to provisions of law or to the employment contract.

*(ELI Dz.U. 1974 nr 24 poz. 141, tekst jednolity, stan na 2026-04-22)*

> **Art. 114 Kodeksu pracy.** Pracownik, który wskutek niewykonania lub nienależytego wykonania obowiązków pracowniczych ze swej winy wyrządził pracodawcy szkodę, ponosi odpowiedzialność materialną według zasad określonych w przepisach niniejszego rozdziału.

Explanatory translation: An employee who, through failure to perform or improper performance of employee duties by his fault, has caused damage to the employer, bears material (financial) liability according to the rules set out in the provisions of this chapter.

> **Art. 115 Kodeksu pracy.** Pracownik ponosi odpowiedzialność za szkodę w granicach rzeczywistej straty poniesionej przez pracodawcę i tylko za normalne następstwa działania lub zaniechania, z którego wynikła szkoda.

Explanatory translation: The employee bears liability for the damage within the limits of the actual loss suffered by the employer and only for the normal consequences of the action or omission from which the damage arose.

Art. 100 § 1 demands "conscientiousness and care" – **an incomplete shift handover is a breach of care**. Art. 114 ties this to material liability: if negligence causes damage (e.g. 2100 zł of service from the March incident), the employee covers the actual loss. Art. 115 limits this liability to "normal consequences" – the wheel runout that Damian ignored was a normal consequence of not showing it to M3 in the morning, so Damian was **materially** liable, but the penalty was reduced to an admonition through the employer's pragmatism (first offence, not a bad man, a well-performing operator).

**Conclusion**: the PZ-001 form is not bureaucracy for its own sake. It is **a legal instrument that protects the operator**. If the operator writes "I heard a slight vibration at 4:00, to be observed" and the next shift ignores it – the fault lies with the next operator. If he writes "no faults" and in reality there was a vibration – the fault lies with the outgoing operator, leading to an admonition, and with material damage to covering the costs.

### Five sections of the EGIDA-PZ-001 form

**Section 1: Machine status**. All moving elements and critical parameters: service seal, blade (run time), tension, guides, bearings, extractor, hydraulics, electrical cabinet, lighting. Each parameter written with concrete numbers (not "OK", but "2280 PSI", "540 min", "3.0/3.3 mm"). If any one is out of norm – entry in this section, red marker, outgoing signature with a note.

**Section 2: Current order**. ZLE number, species, state of completion (logs milled, boards ready, planned finish). **If the final order does not remain for the next shift, one writes "order closed, new shift starts from ZLE-Y"**. If it remains half-done, precisely: how many logs milled, how many boards in the store, how many left to cut.

**Section 3: Reported faults**. **Mandatory in writing since March 2026** (reg.wew.05/2024 § 9 sec. 3 – after Damian's incident). If none – write "none". If something – KKS-001 card number (Service Correction Registry), status ("waiting for maintenance / waiting for service / closed"), priority ("critical / medium / low"). **An empty field = a formal defect of PZ-001, Marek's supervisory signature will not be added**.

**Section 4: Open OH&S points from morning briefing**. Topics from the 5-minute briefing, particular species-related hazards (oak/beech splinters, pine greasy blade, birch soft), mandatory PPE, incidents during the shift (falling board, blade jam, voltage surge). **Information about the first-aid kit, the key, first aid** – so that the next operator knows where to look in case of an accident.

**Section 5: Technical and personal notes**. Two areas: **technical** (batch specifics, parameters per species, planned maintenance, calibrations) and **personal** (helper – who is on, who is not, emergency contact phone, cultural note if the incoming operator is new).

Section 5 is the most extensive and the least formal. It is "the dictionary inside the incoming operator's head" – everything that does not fit in sections 1-4, but will help the next shift understand the context.

### Supervisory signature of the foreman

**The third signature** on PZ-001 – the foreman's. It is not required at every handover (two signatures, outgoing and incoming, are enough for validity), but in **two situations Marek signs additionally**:
1. **First solo shift of the incoming operator** (like Bohdan today – first night solo on P3). Marek's signature = acceptance of the briefing, full responsibility for the further course.
2. **Incident or fault in section 3 marked as "critical"** – the foreman must see the PZ and confirm that he knows about the matter.

Marek's signature under Bohdan's PZ this evening means that Marek **takes over the coordination responsibility** for the course of the night shift. If Bohdan makes a mistake, Marek will not be able to say "I did not know who was on P3" – he knew, he approved, he supervised.

### The overtime question after the handover

The outgoing operator officially ends the shift at the hour set in the schedule (15:00 day shift). If the on-the-job briefing requires a longer time than a normal handover (e.g. first solo shift of the substitute), the question of **overtime** arises:

> **Art. 151 § 1 Kodeksu pracy.** Praca wykonywana ponad obowiązujące pracownika normy czasu pracy, a także praca wykonywana ponad przedłużony dobowy wymiar czasu pracy (...), stanowi pracę w godzinach nadliczbowych. (...)

Explanatory translation: Work performed beyond the working time norms applicable to the employee, as well as work performed beyond the extended daily working time dimension (...), constitutes work in overtime hours. (...)

EGIDA regulation § 8 sec. 2 clarifies: **15 minutes of exceeding the working time norm is minor unpaid work** (accepted by the employee within the duty of care, art. 100 § 1 KP). From **the 16th minute onwards** – overtime with a 50% premium (in line with art. 151¹ § 1 point 2 KP).

Mykoła today finished at 15:00, briefing until 14:57. He did not exceed even a minute of overtime. But if the briefing were to last until 15:20, it would be **20 minutes**: 15 minutes unpaid + 5 minutes of overtime × rate 50 zł/hour × 1.5 = **6.25 zł gross**. If until 15:45 – 30 minutes of overtime = 25 zł gross.

**Pedagogy**: the outgoing operator, who knows that the substitute is new, **prepares the briefing in a compact way** – does not ramble, but also does not leave gaps. Mykoła today fit within 27 minutes with a full briefing, because earlier he had prepared the PZ-001 form (he filled in sections 1-2 on the fly during the shift, not at the end). **Planning saves overtime** without compromising completeness.

## Key terms

- **Przekazanie zmiany** (PL) / **Shift handover** (EN) / **Entrega de turno** (ES) / **Передача зміни** (UK) – a formal procedure of handing over the station and responsibility to the substitute, with the 5-section EGIDA-PZ-001 form.
- **Zdający zmianę** (PL) / **Outgoing operator** (EN) / **Operador saliente** (ES) / **Оператор, що здає зміну** (UK) – the operator ending the shift, responsible for the completeness of the PZ-001 document.
- **Przyjmujący zmianę** (PL) / **Incoming operator** (EN) / **Operador entrante** (ES) / **Оператор, що приймає зміну** (UK) – the operator starting the shift, verifying the consistency of the status with the outgoing operator's report.
- **Formularz EGIDA-PZ-001** (PL) / **EGIDA-PZ-001 form** (EN) / **Formulario EGIDA-PZ-001** (ES) / **Формуляр EGIDA-PZ-001** (UK) – A5 double-sided, 5 sections, introduced 04.2026.
- **Sekcja 1 stan maszyny** (PL) / **Section 1 machine status** (EN) / **Sección 1 estado de la máquina** (ES) / **Секція 1 стан машини** (UK) – seal, blade, tension, guides, bearings, extractor, hydraulics, electrical cabinet, lighting.
- **Sekcja 3 zgłoszone usterki** (PL) / **Section 3 reported faults** (EN) / **Sección 3 averías reportadas** (ES) / **Секція 3 заявлені несправності** (UK) – mandatory in writing since 03.2026, empty field = formal defect of the document.
- **reg.wew.05/2024 § 9 ust. 3** (PL) / **Internal regulation 05/2024 § 9.3** (EN) / **Reglamento interno 05/2024 § 9.3** (ES) / **Внутрішній регламент 05/2024 § 9.3** (UK) – incomplete handover = offence: admonition, reprimand, 10% deduction, contract termination.
- **Art. 100 § 1 KP** (PL) / **Labour Code art. 100 § 1** (EN) / **Código de Trabajo art. 100 § 1** (ES) / **Кодекс праці ст. 100 § 1** (UK) – employee's duty of conscientiousness and care.
- **Art. 114 KP** (PL) / **Labour Code art. 114** (EN) / **Código de Trabajo art. 114** (ES) / **Кодекс праці ст. 114** (UK) – employee's material liability for damage caused to the employer.
- **Art. 151 § 1 KP** (PL) / **Labour Code art. 151 § 1** (EN) / **Código de Trabajo art. 151 § 1** (ES) / **Кодекс праці ст. 151 § 1** (UK) – definition of work in overtime hours.
- **Drobny nadliczbowy 15 min** (PL) / **Minor overtime 15 min** (EN) / **Horas extras menores 15 min** (ES) / **Дрібна понаднормова праця 15 хв** (UK) – unpaid exceeding of the norm up to 15 minutes, EGIDA regulation § 8 sec. 2.
- **LOTO** (PL) / **LOTO (Lockout/Tagout)** (EN) / **LOTO (Bloqueo/Etiquetado)** (ES) / **LOTO (Блокування/Маркування)** (UK) – procedure of securing an energy source with a named padlock before intervening in a machine.
- **KKS-001** (PL) / **KKS-001 (Correction Card)** (EN) / **KKS-001 (Tarjeta de Corrección)** (ES) / **KKS-001 (Картка корекції)** (UK) – Service Correction Registry, number of a reported fault entry.
- **Bohdan Szewczenko** (PL) / **Bohdan Szewczenko** (EN) / **Bohdan Szewczenko** (ES) / **Богдан Шевченко** (UK) – M3 operator at EGIDA since 05.2026, Ukrainian from Lviv, P3 afternoon shift.
- **Drugi podpis przyjmującego** (PL) / **Incoming operator's countersignature** (EN) / **Segunda firma del entrante** (ES) / **Другий підпис приймаючого** (UK) – condition of formal validity of PZ-001, without which the document is only a note.
- **Komunikacja kulturowa w instruktażu** (PL) / **Cross-cultural communication in briefing** (EN) / **Comunicación intercultural en la instrucción** (ES) / **Міжкультурна комунікація в інструктажі** (UK) – simple sentences, physical demonstration, encouragement of questions, tolerance of multilingualism.
