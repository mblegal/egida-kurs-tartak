## II. Nature of the graduate apprenticeship

1. **Statutory character**: the apprenticeship is undertaken on the basis of a **written graduate apprenticeship agreement** (Article 5(1) of the Polish Act of 17 July 2009 on graduate apprenticeships [pol. *Ustawa z dnia 17 lipca 2009 r. o praktykach absolwenckich*]), concluded between the Trainee and the Entrepreneur indicated in section III as the host entity. This Certificate does not replace that agreement.

2. **Purpose of the apprenticeship** (Article 1(2) of the Act of 17 July 2009): to facilitate the Trainee's acquisition of experience and practical skills necessary to perform work in the sawmill, timber, joinery and furniture industries.

3. **Compliance with statutory conditions** (Article 2(1) of the Act of 17 July 2009): on the date of commencement of the apprenticeship, the Trainee has completed at least a school corresponding to a Polish lower secondary school (gimnazjum) or to an eight-year primary school (with regard to Article 2(2) of the Act in conjunction with Article 93 of the Act on the Education System, where the school certificate has been obtained abroad), and **has not reached 30 years of age**.

4. **Unpaid nature**: the apprenticeship is undertaken **free of charge** (Article 3(1) of the Act of 17 July 2009). The Trainee receives no monetary benefit nor any other remuneration on account of undertaking the apprenticeship. This provision is consistent with the principle of unremunerated participation in the Course expressed in section VI(1) of the Course Agreement.

5. **Exclusion of particularly hazardous work** (Article 5(3) of the Act of 17 July 2009): the apprenticeship does not include particularly hazardous work within the meaning of the regulations issued on the basis of Article 237 of the Labour Code.

6. **Statutory limit on duration** (Article 5(4) of the Act of 17 July 2009): the total duration of the apprenticeship, together with any subsequent agreements with the same host entity, **does not exceed 3 months**.

7. **Applicable labour law provisions** (Article 4 of the Act of 17 July 2009): labour law provisions do not apply to the apprenticeship, with the exception of the provisions of the Labour Code on equal treatment (Article 18 of the Labour Code), on standards of working time (Article 129 § 1 of the Labour Code), on breaks (Article 131 § 1 of the Labour Code), on daily rest (Article 132 § 1 of the Labour Code), on weekly rest (Article 133 § 1 of the Labour Code), on breaks due to the type of work (Article 134 of the Labour Code), and on the prohibition of overtime in respect of specified groups (Article 151 of the Labour Code).

## IV. Type of work performed within the apprenticeship

Within the framework of the apprenticeship, the Trainee acquires experience and gains practical skills in the performance of sawmill, timber, joinery and related tasks, to an extent corresponding to the level of advancement of the Course, on three progressive competence levels:

| Course module | Profile of work within the apprenticeship | Volume |
|---------------|-------------------------------------------|--------|
| **M1** | auxiliary work at the sawmill: recognition of wood raw material, marking and moving of material, basic handling and warehousing operations, internalisation of the OHS regime of the plant | 4 weeks / 72 didactic hours |
| **M2** | work as an operator under supervision: operation of basic sawmill machines (band saws, circular saws, planers) under the supervision of a competent person, control of cutting parameters, keeping of workshop documentation, cooperation within a production team | 4 weeks / 112 didactic hours |
| **M3** | work as an independent operator: independent operation of sawmill machines throughout the full production cycle, planning and optimisation of the cutting process, quality control and material sorting, diagnostics of simple malfunctions, supervision of auxiliary workers | 4 weeks / 132 didactic hours |

**Total volume of the apprenticeship**: 12 weeks / 316 didactic hours within the framework of the entire Practical Course „Praca w tartaku".

This Certificate confirms exclusively the **fact of undertaking** the apprenticeship as at the date of its issuance. The current stage of the Trainee's apprenticeship as at that date:

## VI. Occupational health and safety of the apprenticeship

1. In accordance with Article 6 of the Act of 17 July 2009, the Entrepreneur, as the host entity, ensures safe and hygienic conditions for the Trainee's performance of the apprenticeship, on terms analogous to those applicable to its own employees, including **personal protective equipment (PPE)** appropriate to the type of work and the hazards present at the sawmill plant.

2. The Entrepreneur conducts a **workstation OHS instruction** for the Trainee before allowing the Trainee to perform tasks at the sawmill plant and exercises supervision over the work safety of the Trainee, on the terms set out in section V(2)(d) of the Course Agreement.

3. The apprenticeship **does not include particularly hazardous work** within the meaning of the regulations issued on the basis of Article 237 of the Labour Code (Article 5(3) of the Act of 17 July 2009).

## VII. Purpose of the Certificate

This Certificate:

a) confirms the **fact of undertaking** by the Trainee a graduate apprenticeship with the Entrepreneur during the period indicated in section V, within the framework of the Foundation's referral,

b) constitutes a document of a declarative character, suitable for presentation to competent authorities (in particular to the authorities controlling the legality of residence of foreigners, tax control authorities, labour offices and economic control authorities) for confirmation of the status of the Trainee,

c) **does not replace** the certificate of **completion** of the graduate apprenticeship referred to in Article 7(1) of the Act of 17 July 2009; the certificate of completion of the apprenticeship, containing a description of the type of work performed and of the skills acquired by the Trainee, is issued upon completion of the apprenticeship at the request of the Trainee as a separate document,

d) **does not replace** the graduate apprenticeship agreement, the certificate of attendance of the Practical Course (B1 #2 of the Course documents package), the modular certificates, or the certificate of completion of the Course.
