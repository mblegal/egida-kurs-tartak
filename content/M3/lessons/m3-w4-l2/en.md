### 10:20, technical basis: PN-EN 336

Pani Ania:

*"The standard **PN-EN 336:2013 'Structural timber, Sizes, permitted deviations'** is **binding throughout the European Union**. It regulates dimensional tolerances of structural timber (C16, C24, C30, C35). For class C24 and dimensions 175 × 38 mm the tolerance is:

| Nominal dimension | PN-EN 336 tolerance | Acceptable range |
|---|---|---|
| width 175 mm | ±2 mm | **173 – 177 mm** |
| thickness 38 mm | ±2 mm | **36 – 40 mm** |
| length 4050 mm | ±5 mm | **4045 – 4055 mm** |

**Your rafters have**:
- widths of 174.1 and 174.3 mm → **within the range 173-177**
- thicknesses of 37.5 and 37.6 mm → **within the range 36-40**

**There is no dimensional defect in the sense of the technical standard.** The rafter is compliant with PN-EN 336."*

Jacek looks at the table. He frowns. He does not take offence. He thinks.

*"Pani Ania, so **174 mm is 'accepted' for a declared 175**?"*

*"Yes. **Tolerance is part of the dimension.** Wood is not metal: Scots pine shrinks on drying by 3-5% in width, 2-3% in thickness. PN-EN 336 takes this into account and adopts ±2 mm **as a normal state** for fresh or kiln-dried (to 18%) structural timber."*

Marek adds:

**Explanatory translation** (for reference):

**Traducción explicativa**:

Pani Ania continues:

> **Art. 556¹ § 1 Kodeksu cywilnego**

> 1. nie ma właściwości, które rzecz tego rodzaju powinna mieć ze względu na cel w umowie oznaczony albo wynikający z okoliczności lub przeznaczenia;
> 2. nie ma właściwości, o których istnieniu sprzedawca zapewnił kupującemu, w tym przedstawiając próbę lub wzór;
> 3. nie nadaje się do celu, o którym kupujący poinformował sprzedawcę przy zawarciu umowy, a sprzedawca nie zgłosił zastrzeżenia co do takiego jej przeznaczenia;
> 4. została kupującemu wydana w stanie niezupełnym.
>
> [KC, ELI DU/1964/93/poz. 16, tekst jednolity, stan na 2026-04-22]

**Explanatory translation**:
*A defect consists of the sold item's non-compliance with the contract. **Traducción explicativa**:
*El defecto consiste en la no conformidad de la cosa vendida con el contrato. **Traducción explicativa**:

## Theory: 6 typical bases of timber complaints, register, prevention

### 6 typical bases of complaints

Based on EGIDA's complaints register 2020-2026 (on average 8-12 complaints/year, of which 70% unjustified, 20% partly justified, 10% fully justified) and the database of the Polish Wood Industry Association:

| Basis | Frequency | Typical mechanism | Documentary prevention |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Dimension outside tolerance** | 35% (most frequent) | Customer measures with an inaccurate caliper, compares with nominal without tolerance | KEP-001: caliper measurement (calibrated) every 5th board, PN-EN 336 tolerance stated on WZ |
| **2. Moisture outside class** | 20% | Customer buys as "dried to 15%", measures with an uncalibrated moisture meter, finds 18% | KEP-001: moisture at 5 points per PN-EN 13183-2, moisture meter calibrated every 12 months |
| **3. Species mismatch** | 15% | Pedunculate oak vs. sessile oak, Scots pine vs. mountain pine, silver birch vs. downy birch | KEP-001: Latin species name, bark sample, origin Forest District |
| **4. Post-drying cracks** | 10% | Wet timber suddenly dries at the customer's shop (dry climate 35% RH), cracks | WZ: moisture at collection, customer storage instructions |
| **5. Strength class different from declared** | 8% | Customer tested in a laboratory, found C20 instead of C24 | KEP-001 + Pani Ania's grading with a full record; ITB audit every 2 years |
| **6. Undeclared visual defect** | 12% | Loose knot, blue stain, mould on the log end | KEP-001 defects marked in pencil; class B/C declared on WZ |

### EGIDA complaints register (reklamacje-2026.xlsx)

1. **Report number** (auto-incremented, format REK-YYYY-NNN)

**Once a quarter**: statistical analysis of the register (Excel charts), the EGIDA director assesses whether the complaint trend rises, falls, or changes structure. **For 2025**: 11 complaints, 8 unjustified (72%), 2 partly justified (18%, price reduction 3-5%), 1 fully justified (one pine batch with 24% moisture sold as 18%, replacement of 0.8 m³ = 2800 zł net; error of Mr. Henryk in the drying chamber, point-wise measurement unrecognised, from then on mandatory 5 points per PN-EN 13183-2).

### Documentary prevention: how to prevent complaints through KEP-001

**5 prevention principles**:

1. **Measurement in the presence of a witness** (helper, second operator, grader), working notebook **signed by both**.
2. **Instruments calibrated within term**: moisture meter every 12 months, caliper every 24 months, scale every 12 months; certificates in the metrological archive.
3. **Measurement during work, not retrospectively**, date of filling KEP-001 = date of measurement.
4. **Defects marked in pencil on the boards**, the customer sees on collection what is class B and why; no surprise later.
5. **WZ contains full data**: moisture, class, defects, technical standard, FSC origin.

**Additional safeguard**: **documentary photographs of the batch** before shipment (3 photos: stack from above, stack from the side, first 3 boards in plan). Photographs in the EGIDA CRM archive tagged with ZLE-XXX. In case of a complaint, **the photograph from 16.06 before shipment** is evidence that the defect did not arise at our site, but e.g. in transport.

### Conduct of the M3 operator during a complaint visit

**Principle 1: the M3 operator is a technical fact, not a commercial decision.**

At a complaint visit the M3 operator is responsible for:
- **showing KEP-001 with operational measurements** (what was shown during grading)
- **showing the measurement method** (instruments, PN-EN, measurement points)
- **answering the customer's technical question** ("why 174.1, not 175?")
- **maintaining calm and courtesy**

The M3 operator **does NOT answer for**:
- commercial decisions ("shall we refund money?")
- legal decisions ("shall we go to court?")
- offers of discounts, rebates, goodwill gestures (those are for Marek/director)
- blaming the grader or foreman (that is internal, not in front of the customer)

**Principle 2: the M3 operator is the first point of contact for the fact, but not for the conflict.**

If the customer is **aggressive, shouts, threatens court**, the M3 operator has the right and duty to:
- **not enter polemics**
- **call the foreman** ("Mr. Kowalski, please"; Marek must be present within 5 minutes)
- **leave for the hall** if the customer is physically dangerous (EGIDA rules § 14 para. 4)

**Principle 3: the M3 operator says only what they can prove.**

Acceptable: "I measured the batch on 16.06, the values are in KEP-001, I'll show you."
Unacceptable: "It couldn't be wrong, I always do it right."

**Pedagogy**: Rustam today asked **one professional question at the end** ("how did you measure on site?"). That is **a throw of M3-operator maturity**: interest in the customer's measurement method rather than defence of one's own work. Jacek appreciated it more than the earlier tables.

### Long-term relationship vs. one-off order

**EGIDA prefers framework-contract customers** (Dom-Bud, DOM-STOL, MebelPro, 8-15 steady customers, 70% of annual turnover). A complaint from a **steady customer** is treated **differently** than from a one-off customer:

| Aspect | Steady customer (framework) | One-off customer |
|---|---|---|
| **Initial reaction** | face-to-face meeting within 48h | phone call + email |
| **Goodwill gesture** | 2-5% discount even if unjustified | only if justified |
| **Operational priority** | interruption of Rustam's work for the visit | Marek handles without involving the operator |
| **Documentation** | as above, full, with meeting record | basic, email in the register |
| **Escalation to director** | only on a dispute over 10 000+ zł | director informed every time |

**Jacek was a steady customer**, hence the face-to-face visit, the presence of 4 EGIDA employees (Marek, Pani Ania, Rustam, Wahan), the goodwill 2% gesture. For a one-off customer Marek would have handled it by phone alone.

### The question that usually comes up on the course

**"Why don't we plane rafters to the nominal 175 × 38 without tolerance?"**

Answer: planed 175 × 38 would require a raw dimension of **at least 179 × 42** (to plane off 2 mm per face × 2 faces = 4 mm in each dimension). This means:
- larger logs for sawing (not 38 cm diameter, but 42 cm), lower availability in Masuria
- +15% sawdust (planing waste), income from briquettes insufficient to cover it
- thickness planer + four-sided planer = 450 000 zł investment + 3 additional workers
- planing time: 2 min/pc × 52 pcs = 104 min + planer setup

Altogether **+150 zł/m³** net to the price. In the competitive rafter market (Masuria has 14 sawmills within a 50 km radius), **+150 zł/m³ is a customer leaving for Mazur Drew in Ełk**.

**Conclusion**: the PN-EN 336 ±2 mm tolerance is **economically necessary**. All sawmills competing with EGIDA sell rafters with this tolerance. A customer who does not know this is a customer for **education**, not for reproach.
