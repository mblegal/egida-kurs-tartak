# Sawmill glossary for the course participant, short version 50 words

This glossary contains **50 of the most important terms** used in a Polish sawmill, in four languages: Polish (PL), English (EN), Spanish (ES) and Ukrainian (UK).

**How to use it**: carry it in your work pocket (printed A6, laminated). When you do not understand a word from the trainer or the foreman, open the glossary, find the Polish word or ask someone to point it out with a finger; next to it you will find the translation in your language.

**Full version** (200 terms, divided into the 4 M1 areas: occupational safety, material, processes, organisation) is in preparation and will be made available to participants before the start of M2.
