# DECLARATION OF ELIGIBILITY FOR PARTICIPATION IN THE COURSE

**Practical course "Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill)**: a vocational course for foreign nationals legally residing in the territory of the Republic of Poland, organised free of charge by Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation).

---

## Preliminary information for the Candidate

This declaration shall be submitted by a candidate who has filed a Recruitment Form and is applying for admission to the Course. The declaration serves to confirm that the Candidate satisfies the conditions of participation set out in Section IV of the Course Regulations, and in particular that the Candidate:

a) is a foreign national within the meaning of Article 3 point 2 of the ustawa o cudzoziemcach (Act on Foreign Nationals) and resides in the territory of the Republic of Poland legally,

b) is of legal age (an adult),

c) has no medical contraindications to performing physical work in the conditions of a sawmill,

d) has familiarised himself or herself with the nature and scope of the Course, including the fact that the Course is provided to the Candidate free of charge and does not constitute a guarantee of subsequent employment,

e) confirms that the information provided by him or her in the Recruitment Form and in this declaration is true and complete to the best of his or her knowledge.

The declaration shall be submitted in written form, bearing the handwritten signature of the Candidate, in one copy, which the Candidate shall deliver to the Foundation together with the Recruitment Form or no later than on the day of signing the Course Agreement.

## Part F. Declaration as to the truthfulness and completeness of the information provided

The Candidate declares that all information provided by him or her in the Recruitment Form and in this declaration is true and complete to the best of his or her knowledge, and that he or she has not concealed any circumstance which could have a material impact on the Foundation's assessment of his or her candidacy.

The Candidate acknowledges that:

a) the Foundation may verify the truthfulness of the declarations submitted by the Candidate to the extent permitted by law, in particular by inspecting the residence and identity documents submitted, in the manner and on the terms set out in the GDPR clause constituting a separate document;

b) the submission of an untrue declaration may result in refusal of admission of the Candidate to the Course, withdrawal by the Foundation from the Course Agreement, or removal of the Candidate from the list of participants, and in cases provided for under generally applicable law may give rise to civil or criminal liability;

c) the Foundation shall not be liable for the consequences of participation in the Course resulting from information concealed by the Candidate concerning his or her state of health, legal situation, or other circumstances having a material impact on safe participation in the Course.

## Note on the project

The Course "Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill) was created as a result of the potential developed under the project "Budowa fundamentów wsparcia cudzoziemców na rynku pracy w województwie opolskim" (Building foundations for supporting foreigners in the labour market in the Opole Voivodeship), implemented by Stowarzyszenie SMART (lead partner) in cooperation with Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (partner) in the years 2026-2027 from the European Social Fund Plus. The Course itself is not financed from the project's funds and does not constitute a project activity; it is an independent statutory undertaking of the Foundation, conducted free of charge for the benefit of foreign nationals.
