## Content

### The five daily tasks in detail

**Task 1. Lubrication of guides, rollers, bearings.**

**NLGI No. 2 Lithium Grease** is the industrial standard for rolling bearings working at moderate temperatures (-20 °C to +120 °C). Chemical composition: a mineral or synthetic base oil plus a lithium thickener (lithium soap, 8-12% by mass) plus anti-wear additives (phosphates, sulphides). Wood-Mizer recommends Amoco Rycon #2 Synthetic (no. P07253), with identical Polish equivalents: **Lotos Greasen LiX Plus 2** (Grupa Lotos, 35-50 zł per 400 g cartridge, compliant with P07253), **Orlen Litosan Plus 2** (Orlen Oil, similar price, made in Jedlicze). Do not mix with other bases (e.g. calcium grease, polymeric) because the thickeners can coagulate and clog the bearing.

Tool: a **hand grease gun** with a cartridge, manual (lever pump) or pneumatic (compressed air). Wood-Mizer recommends the manual P07252. Output: 1 pump is about 1 gram of grease, a typical bearing needs 2-4 grams per greasing. A 400 g cartridge means **100-200 point greasings**, which is 5-10 full rounds of the 20 LT70 points, i.e. 4-8 weeks of work.

Greasing-point procedure:

1. Wipe the nipple with a clean cotton cloth (not a paper towel, which leaves fibres). Sawdust and dust must stay out of the bearing.
2. Place the gun perpendicular, press it against the nipple. An automatic gun clicks when a tight connection is made.
3. Press the lever 1-2 times (1 pump = 1 gram).
4. Move the gun away; the old grease pushed out by the new emerges on the other side of the bearing (in a typical rolling bearing). Wipe with a cloth.
5. If a crusted, black, thickened grease comes out of the nipple, it is a sign that the bearing is running too hot or has been over-greased. Note in KKS-001.

**Over-greasing** is a common beginner's error. If you squeeze in 3-4-5 pumps instead of 1-2, the grease pushed past the bearing collects in the pocket above it, thickens with sawdust and dust, forms an abrasive paste which on the next shift re-enters the bearing and destroys the race. **Result**: a bearing designed for 5000 engine hours fails at 1500, replacement cost 300-600 zł plus a 4-8 hour stoppage. The "1-2 pumps" discipline means real savings of 300-600 zł per bearing per year.

**Task 2. Checking oil levels.**

The LT70 has **four fluid reservoirs** (plus the fuel-filter water separator in the diesel version). For the EGIDA electric version we skip point D (engine):

| Reservoir | Fluid | Capacity | Change interval | WM catalogue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Up/Down gearbox | Mobil SHC 634 | 230 ml | 500 h / 1 year | L04869-4 |
| B. Feed gearbox | Mobil SHC 634 | 230 ml | 500 h / 1 year | L04869-4 |
| C. Hydrostatic system | Shell TTF-SB | 5.7 l | 1000 h / 2 years | 040642 |
| D. Diesel engine | SAE 15W-40 | 7.6 l | 250 h | n/a |

Daily: only a **visual level check** (level between MIN and MAX, ~30 seconds per reservoir). Full change **by engine hours**, not daily.

**Oil-change procedure** (Up/Down gearbox as an example):

1. STOP, LOTO.
2. Warm up the gearbox (the oil flows more easily), run the machine at idle for 5 minutes, then STOP.
3. Place a container under the drain plug (typically 500 ml, capacity 230 ml plus a margin).
4. Undo the drain plug (17 mm wrench), drain the oil. Wait until dripping stops (another ~20 ml is recovered after 2-3 minutes).
5. Refit the drain plug (10-12 Nm, not too tight, metal seal).
6. Undo the filler plug, pour in the new oil slowly (funnel, 200 ml plus dipstick check, top up to MAX).
7. Refit the filler plug.
8. Remove LOTO, run at low rpm for 5 minutes, STOP, check level (it will drop ~10 ml as the channel fills, top up to MAX).
9. Entry in KKS-001: "19.05 Up/Down oil change, Mobil SHC 634 200 ml fill, counter 1847 h. R. Nazarov."

Total time: **25 minutes for one person**. Frequency: every 500 h or once a year (whichever comes first). For a typical EGIDA schedule (two-shift operation, 16 h/day times 250 days/year = 4000 h/year): **gearbox oils ~8 times a year, hydrostat ~4 times a year**.

**Task 3. Cleaning sawdust and dust.**

Sawdust is **not aesthetic but mainly functional**. Build-ups on the machine:

- Enter the bearings (see task 1).
- Block position sensors (top limit switch, door interlock).
- Increase fire risk (PIP guide "Safe work in a sawmill", chapter 7.3: "fine wood dust combined with a spark is a fire-explosion hazard, ATEX zone 22 in the central extraction area").
- Confuse diagnostics (you cannot see structural cracks under a layer of dust).

Do not use compressed air where bearings are (it blows dust inside, a paradoxical effect); use a **brush plus an industrial vacuum**. Compressed air is OK for open surfaces (guards, the floor, the panel once the keyboard is lifted out).

::: info
**Wood dust as an explosion hazard.** In the central extraction area (filter bag plus extraction ducts) the concentration of fine wood dust can exceed **the lower explosive limit of 30-60 g/m³**. A spark from an electrical switch, a motor brush or static electricity is enough for ignition. That is why the central extraction area in a sawmill is classified as **ATEX 22** (an explosive atmosphere occurring occasionally). The electrical installation must be Ex (spark-safe), and the operator may not enter the zone without a special procedure. Local extraction (the LT70 outriggers) is not ATEX. Details in M4.
:::

**Task 4. Replacing blade guide blocks.**

Blade guide blocks (also called guide rollers) are **wearable elements**. Their role: they hold the band vertical in the gap between the band wheels, without lateral bending. The band constantly rubs against the blocks, so the blocks wear out. Typical material: **UHMW polyethylene** (ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene, low friction, abrasion-resistant); alternatively phosphor bronze (more durable but more expensive, less kind to the band because band filings collect in the block).

Signs for replacement:

- Block thickness has dropped from the original **12 mm to ~8 mm** (measure with a caliper, accessible after removing the guard).
- Deep lateral scratches (band mark >0.5 mm).
- Visible lateral marks on the band (the block does not hold evenly).
- Engine-hour counter: **every 150-250 h** depending on wood hardness (oak 150, pine 250, mixed 200).

The replacement procedure is described above in the lesson (the Rustam + Wahan scene, 30 minutes). Key point: **never without LOTO**; without releasing the band tension the blocks carry a load of 2000-3000 N and a finger can be lost during replacement. Safety first.

**Task 5. Checking signalling and safety switches.**

The PIP OHS guide for sawmills, chapter 5.2, states: "Before starting work the sawmill operator is obliged to check the condition of the emergency switches, position sensors and signalling". At EGIDA the standing standard is the five check points described in the scene above (main E-STOP, side E-STOP, door interlock, top limit switch, LED signalling).

**Full test** (once a week, not daily): start the machine at low rpm, open the door-interlock guard, check that the motor stops in <0.5 s. **Short test** (every day): only a visual check of the levers, guards and buttons while dry, without starting the motor. Both versions are recorded in KKS-001 differently (full on Monday morning, short every day).

### Operator vs maintenance vs external service boundary

The competence boundary is **one of the most important OHS principles** in the machine industry. It follows from three levels of law:

**Level 1. Art. 211 of the Labour Code** (employee duties, literal IURA citation, consolidated text as of 2026-04-21):

> **Art. 211.** Przestrzeganie przepisów i zasad bezpieczeństwa i higieny pracy jest podstawowym obowiązkiem pracownika. W szczególności pracownik jest obowiązany:
>
> 1) znać przepisy i zasady bezpieczeństwa i higieny pracy, brać udział w szkoleniu i instruktażu z tego zakresu oraz poddawać się wymaganiom egzaminom sprawdzającym;
> 2) wykonywać pracę w sposób zgodny z przepisami i zasadami bezpieczeństwa i higieny pracy oraz stosować się do wydawanych w tym zakresie poleceń i wskazówek przełożonych;
> 3) dbać o należyty stan maszyn, urządzeń, narzędzi i sprzętu oraz o porządek i ład w miejscu pracy;
> (...)
> 6) niezwłocznie zawiadomić przełożonego o zauważonym w zakładzie pracy wypadku albo zagrożeniu życia lub zdrowia ludzkiego oraz ostrzec współpracowników, a także inne osoby znajdujące się w rejonie zagrożenia, o grożącym im niebezpieczeństwie;
> 7) współdziałać z pracodawcą i przełożonymi w wypełnianiu obowiązków dotyczących bezpieczeństwa i higieny pracy.

**Explanatory translation:** Art. 211 Polish Labour Code obliges every employee to comply with OHS rules. Specifically, the employee must: (1) know the rules and attend training, (2) work in compliance with OHS and follow superiors' instructions, (3) care for the proper condition of machines, tools and equipment and keep the workplace tidy, (4) use collective and personal protective equipment as intended; (...) (6) promptly report any accident or life/health hazard to the supervisor and warn co-workers and anyone in the danger zone; (7) cooperate with the employer and superiors in OHS duties.

Key for our lesson: **point 3** (care for the proper condition of machines, lubrication, checking, cleaning) and **point 6** (prompt notification of a hazard, the leak scene, the phone call to Marek).

**Level 2. Art. 237 § 1 of the Labour Code** (literal IURA citation):

> **Art. 237.** § 1. Nie wolno dopuścić pracownika do pracy, do której wykonywania nie posiada on wymaganych kwalifikacji lub potrzebnych umiejętności, a także dostatecznej znajomości przepisów oraz zasad bezpieczeństwa i higieny pracy.

**Explanatory translation:** Art. 237 § 1 Polish Labour Code prohibits admitting an employee to work for which they do not hold the required qualifications or necessary skills, or sufficient knowledge of OHS rules and principles.

Meaning: **the employer cannot order Rustam to replace a hydraulic cylinder seal**, because Rustam has not completed the manufacturer's Wood-Mizer hydraulic-repair training. The ban on admission binds the employer (Marek, plant manager Krzysztof Nowak), not the employee themselves, but an employee who sees they are being given an instruction outside their qualifications has the right (and duty) to refuse (Art. 210 of the Labour Code, the right to abstain from work that endangers health).

**Level 3. § 41 of the MPiPS Regulation of 26.09.1997 on general OHS provisions** (literal IURA citation):

> **§ 41.** 1. Pracodawca jest obowiązany udostępnić pracownikom, do stałego korzystania, aktualne instrukcje bezpieczeństwa i higieny pracy dotyczące:
>
> 1) stosowanych w zakładzie procesów technologicznych oraz wykonywania prac związanych z zagrożeniami wypadkowymi lub zagrożeniami zdrowia pracowników;
> 2) obsług maszyn i innych urządzeń technicznych;
> 3) postępowania z materiałami szkodliwymi dla zdrowia i niebezpiecznymi;
> 4) udzielania pierwszej pomocy.
>
> 2. Instrukcje, o których mowa w ust. 1, powinny w sposób zrozumiały dla pracowników wskazywać czynności, które należy wykonać przed rozpoczęciem danej pracy, zasady i sposoby bezpiecznego wykonywania pracy, czynności do wykonania po jej zakończeniu oraz zasady postępowania w sytuacjach awaryjnych stwarzających zagrożenie dla życia lub zdrowia pracowników.

**Explanatory translation:** § 41 of the Polish general OHS regulation obliges the employer to provide employees, for permanent use, with current OHS instructions covering (1) the technological processes used in the plant and the performance of tasks linked to accident or health hazards; (2) the operation of machines and other technical devices; (3) handling hazardous and health-damaging materials; (4) first aid. Sec. 2: these instructions must, in a manner understandable to employees, indicate the actions to be performed before starting the given task, the principles and methods of safe performance, the actions after its completion and the rules for emergency situations posing a threat to the life or health of employees.

This is **the legal basis for the existence of the P3 operator workstation instructions** and the **KKS-001** card. KKS-001 is in practice a "Polish version of the Wood-Mizer Maintenance Log" (which the manufacturer attaches to the English-language LT70 DTR), adapted to the requirements of § 41 sec. 2 (before work, during, after, emergency).

**Level 4. § 40 sec. 2 of the general OHS regulation** (the supervisor stops work, literal IURA citation):

> **§ 40.** (...) 2. W razie stwierdzenia bezpośredniego zagrożenia dla życia lub zdrowia pracowników, osoba kierująca pracownikami jest obowiązana do niezwłocznego wstrzymania prac i podjęcia działań w celu usunięcia tego zagrożenia.

**Explanatory translation:** § 40 sec. 2 of the Polish general OHS regulation: when a direct hazard to the life or health of employees is found, the person managing employees is obliged to stop work immediately and to take action to remove the hazard.

Marek Kowalski, at 7:41 a.m. when he assessed the leak, fulfilled this duty: without delay (within 7 minutes of the report) he confirmed the work stoppage on P3, initiated the escalation to Wood-Mizer Polska. He did not wait, did not tinker, did not try to repair, that is on the side of the service with authorisations.

### Planned inspections

Three levels of inspection on the EGIDA LT70:

**Inspection A (quarterly)**: operator + foreman, 2 hours, full sawmill scope. Every 500 engine hours or once a quarter (whichever comes first). Schedule: first Tuesday of the month (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct). Scope:

- Lubrication of all 20 points (full, not just a check).
- Band-tension check plus guide check.
- Verification of all 5 safety switches (full test).
- Measurement of operating temperatures (the foreman's thermal camera, max 85 °C on bearings).
- Wiring condition (visual, without disassembly).
- Blade guide-block replacement if worn.

Cost: **~0 zł external** (operator and foreman labour; materials: grease 40 zł, oil 200-300 zł, blocks 85 zł, total ~400 zł). Entry in KKS-001, appendix "Quarterly inspection Q1/Q2 2026".

**Inspection B (annual general)**: Wood-Mizer Polska service, 6-8 hours, general scope. Once a year, usually March/April (low sawmill season). Scope:

- Everything from quarterly inspection A.
- Electrical measurements: protective-earth effectiveness, insulation resistance, RCD (every 2 years per PIP; in the off-years only a visual check by Wood-Mizer).
- Head-bearing replacement if showing wear.
- Band-wheel reconditioning (if needed, every 2-4 years).
- Hydraulic inspection (cylinders, pumps, valves), seal replacement per DTR.
- Structural inspection (cracks, beam corrosion), welding if necessary.
- Control-panel software update (if available).

Cost: **2500-4500 zł net** for a 2021 LT70 (depending on the scope of replaced parts). EGIDA has an annual budget of 5 thousand zł gross per machine (margin for unforeseen parts).

**Inspection C (interventional)**: Wood-Mizer service on call, response time 2-5 working days, cost depending on the problem. Rustam's oil leak today is an example of a C-type inspection.
