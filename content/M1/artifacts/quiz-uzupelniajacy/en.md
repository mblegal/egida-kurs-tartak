# M1 supplementary final quiz

## Purpose

This quiz is a **complement to the l8 test**. It contains **15 harder questions** that are **more integrated**, combining knowledge from several M1 lessons at once. Used in two modes:

1. **Self-assessment mode**: the trainee solves it after the l8 test (if passed), to check deeper understanding. No impact on passing.
2. **Retake mode**: a trainee who did not pass l8 solves both (l8 + quiz) in the retake test. Combined threshold **70% of 110+30 = 98 pts out of 140**.

## Instructions

- **15 questions × 2 pts = 30 pts**.
- Each question has 4 answers (A/B/C/D), **one correct**.
- Time: **30 minutes**.
- No consultation, individual, on paper.
- If you do not know, mark „don't know" (0 pts, info for the trainer).

## Questions

**1. A trainee arrives at the workstation. He sees hydraulic oil spilled on the floor. The operator has not shown up yet. What does he do IN ORDER in the first 60 seconds?**

A) He cleans the oil himself so the operator does not have to worry.
B) He puts up a „caution – slippery floor" sign, does not enter the zone, goes to fetch the foreman.
C) He switches on the machine so production time is not lost.
D) He calls 112 right away.

**2. A helper sees a colleague using a forklift he should NOT use (no UDT authorization). The colleague says: „don't talk, the foreman let me, just for a moment". The helper:**

A) Does not get involved, it is the foreman's and the colleague's business.
B) Logs it in the minor-incident log with reference to the Regulation of the Minister of Economy 2000 and Labour Code art. 210; the foreman will find out formally.
C) Yells at the colleague to get off the forklift.
D) Writes an anonymous tip-off to the State Labour Inspectorate (PIP).

**3. An operator and a helper are working at a band saw. The band snapped, flew sideways, nobody was hurt. The reporting sequence:**

A) First PIP, then the foreman, then the accident team.
B) First the foreman (level 1), then the shift manager at the shift change, then maintenance, plus an entry in the failure log within 24 h.
C) An entry is enough – this was not an accident, nobody was hurt.
D) Only 112, this was a life-threatening incident.

**4. On the log yard in winter, temperature -5°C, logs icy. The helper is to carry a pine beam (3 m × 5 cm × 5 cm, ~3.4 kg). Can he do it alone?**

A) Yes, mass <30 kg, conditions OK.
B) Yes as far as mass is concerned, but in these conditions he MUST check the surface + wear anti-slip S3 boots, otherwise he refuses (Labour Code art. 210).
C) No, winter is an automatic ban on yard work.
D) No, mass must be verified by the foreman.

**5. A group of helpers is learning the STOP gesture. The trainer shows: hand above the head, palm open. A Ukrainian asks: „with us the stop sign is two crossed arms, can I use that?". The correct answer:**

A) Yes, gestures are universal, everyone understands.
B) No, because two crossed arms in ISO 7010 and Polish sawmill tradition mean „GENERAL ALARM", not „stop at this operator"; one standard is used.
C) Yes, but only with the Ukrainian operator.
D) It does not matter, the main thing is the hand above the head.

**6. The foreman comments: „you migrants always want to refuse work, Labour Code art. 210 is not vacation time". How does the helper respond?**

A) Apologizes and goes to work.
B) Calmly: „foreman, I use the right to refuse only when I see a direct hazard (art. 210 § 1); today I saw X, so I refused. I am logging it". Art. 210 § 5 protects me from punishment.
C) Shouts „don't insult migrants!".
D) Hands in notice.

**7. Wood species classified as carcinogenic (IARC class 1) in dust are:**

A) Pine and spruce.
B) Oak and beech.
C) All deciduous species.
D) Only exotic tropical species.

**8. The trainee senses an unusual „burnt rubber" smell near the planer. The foreman is not there, the operator takes no notice. The trainee:**

A) Does not interfere – the operator knows what he is doing.
B) Approaches the operator, STOP gesture + asks „do you smell this?". If the operator downplays it, entry in the minor-incident log + call to the shift manager.
C) Switches off the planer himself with the emergency stop.
D) Runs out of the hall.

**9. „I help but I do not operate" is principle 1 of M1. The exception to this principle (when the helper MAY touch the machine) is:**

A) When the foreman is absent and the cycle must be finished.
B) Only when he has station-specific training + authorization for the given machine (workstation card) – e.g. a hand cart or a cordless drill; rotating machines never.
C) On the first day of work, to learn.
D) There is no exception – he never touches the machine.

**10. On Friday morning a group of trainees sits at breakfast before class. One says: „this training is silly, on the floor we do it differently anyway, the foreman in my sawmill does not care about this". A trainee who is about to start work in another sawmill tomorrow should:**

A) Agree – „indeed, why are we even learning this".
B) Think it through: the Heinrich statistic + Labour Code art. 210 + his own example prove that foremen breaking the rules lead to accidents; the course gives the FOUNDATION, and the trainee verifies every workstation himself (not accepting „we always do it this way").
C) Report the complainer to the trainer.
D) Quit the sawmill job.

**11. The permissible mass M 30/50 kg, W 12/20 kg applies to:**

A) Only Poland.
B) One-off lifting (M 30 / W 12) and lifting as a pair (M 50 / W 20) per Regulation of the Minister of Labour of 18.05.2017; daily limits also apply (M 8 000 / W 4 000 kg/shift), assessment required.
C) Only heavy materials above 15 kg.
D) Only women (W 12/20).

**12. Wood dust in the hall starts to accumulate – visible streaks in the air, the extractor cannot keep up. Risks (choose the most complete answer):**

A) Only allergic, if someone has an allergy.
B) Mechanical (obstructed vision), carcinogenic IARC 1 (oak/beech), explosive (spark + concentrated dust = explosion in the duct), fire (accumulated dust ignites); priority: switch off machines + ventilation + report.
C) Only aesthetic – the hall looks dirty.
D) None, dust is a normal state in a sawmill.

**13. The minor-incident log entry uses the 5W1H formula: what/when/where/who/why/how. The correct entry for the near-miss from l6:**

A) „Something happened in the morning at the saw, nothing bad came out of it".
B) „14.04.2026, 10:45, workstation PT-02 hall A zone 3, an oak log slipped from the operator's grip and flew 15 cm past my left temple, witnesses: operator Marek Nowak and foreman Paweł Kamiński, cause hypothesis: wet log + weak operator grip, action: operator switched off the machine, we replaced the grip with a reinforced one". Signature: Jan Kowalski.
C) „I almost had an accident, I think the foreman should do something".
D) „Today was bad, equipment not working, someone should take care of it".

**14. The trainee finishes M1 and starts his first day of work in the sawmill. What does he NOT sign without reading?**

A) He will sign nothing on principle.
B) Four workstation documents (workstation card, daily attendance log, authorization for a specific tool, minor-incident log when he himself fills it in); if he does not understand anything, he asks the foreman or requests a translation (EGIDA has EN/ES/UK templates).
C) Only the employment contract.
D) Anything can be signed, because the foreman is legally responsible anyway.

**15. The Heinrich statistic 1:30:300:3000 means:**

A) Daily 1 lesson = 30 min of exercises and 300 words to learn.
B) For 1 serious / fatal accident there are 30 minor accidents, 300 near-misses and 3000 unsafe behaviours; it justifies the reporting culture („do not ignore trifles – they lead to catastrophes").
C) EGIDA employs 1 trainer per 30 trainees.
D) It is the number of people in the group × 30 course days.

## Scoring and interpretation

- **28-30 pts (≥93%)**: excellent M1 mastery; trainee ready for more demanding M2 stations (saw operator, quality control).
- **22-27 pts (73-90%)**: good mastery, standard M2 recommendation.
- **16-21 pts (53-73%)**: basic knowledge, M2 recommendation with additional support from a mentor foreman in the first month of work.
- **<16 pts (<53%)**: gaps in deeper understanding; in retake mode added to the l8 test; in self-assessment mode: guidance to revisit specific lessons (source in the „M1 source" column of the key).

## Notes for the trainer

This quiz **is not mandatory** for first-time M1 passing. It is:
- **an option for the ambitious** who want to check deeper understanding
- **a reserve** for the retake test (additional differentiating material)
- **a source of debate questions** for the l7 recap session („let's solve this question together, we'll discuss why B and not A")

**Administration format**: 30 minutes, pencil and paper, quiet room. The trainer does not help during the quiz. After collecting the sheets, an **immediate results discussion** – each question reviewed with the group, mistakes turned into learning. This moment (post-quiz discussion) is **often the most productive in all of M1** – trainees are focused because they have just wrestled with the questions.

**Important**: the 15 questions were chosen so that **each tests integration across at least 2 M1 lessons**. They are not single-lesson questions. A trainee who passes shows **networked understanding**, not just fact-by-fact recall.
