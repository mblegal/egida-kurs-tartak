## Content

### Why wood moisture content matters so much

Wood is a **hygroscopic material**. Unlike metal, plastic, or glass, wood **exchanges moisture with its surroundings** throughout its entire life: in the growing tree, after felling, after breakdown, after drying, after being built into a structure or a piece of furniture. Moisture enters and leaves through the board's surface in both directions, until the wood reaches an **equilibrium moisture content** (EMC, *Equilibrium Moisture Content*) corresponding to the temperature and relative humidity (RH, *Relative Humidity*) of the surrounding air.

Freshly felled wood (the raw material coming into the sawmill) has a moisture content of **50-80%** (calculated as the mass of water divided by the mass of dry wood, the so-called absolute moisture content). After breakdown (sawing the log into boards) the moisture content drops to **35-45%** (thanks to exposure of a larger surface). After a week of spontaneous drying in the wind it drops to **25-30%**. After three weeks in a drying chamber (80°C, forced circulation, controlled RH) it drops to **8-18%** depending on the programme.

**Why this process must be controlled:**

Wood shrinks and expands as its moisture content changes. **Longitudinal shrinkage along the grain** (0.1-0.4%, negligible in practice). **Radial shrinkage** (4-6% from 30% down to 0% moisture). **Tangential shrinkage** (8-12% from 30% down to 0% moisture). Tangential shrinkage is twice as large as radial, which is why boards **warp** (cup into an inverted-U shape) when they dry unevenly. End cracks form when the outer zone of the board dries faster than the core.

The customer **cannot accept** wood that, after installation, begins to shrink, warp, or crack. A flooring board with a moisture content of 20% at the moment of laying, after 6 months in a home with 10% humidity (residential EMC), will shrink by 3% in the tangential direction. For a board 150 mm wide that is a 4.5 mm gap between boards. Unacceptable aesthetically and functionally.

That is why the customer dictates the target moisture content in the order, and the sawmill **must deliver it** within a tolerance of ±2 percentage points (a typical contractual tolerance). The buyer checks the moisture on receipt with their own instrument; a discrepancy above tolerance equals a complaint.

### Three target thresholds by end use

**Threshold 8-10% (furniture joinery):**

- Customer's target environment: the **interior of a centrally heated residence**, temperature 20-22°C in winter, relative humidity 30-40% (low on account of the heating, especially in modern buildings with heat-recovery ventilation). The EMC of this environment is about 6-8%.
- **Applications:** furniture inside residences (tables, wardrobes, desks, beds, chests of drawers), interior claddings (wainscoting in living rooms), premium ash and oak parquets, musical instruments, wooden indoor toys.
- **Why this threshold:** wood installed in a residence with EMC 6-8% **will adjust downward** from the inlet 8-10% to the final 6-8% (annual cycle with winter dryness). This downward movement is small (1-3 percentage points), so shrinkage is negligible. If the wood were delivered at 15% moisture, the shrinkage from 15% to 7% would be 6-8 percentage points, translating into 3-5% tangential shrinkage (for a 150 mm board = a 4.5-7.5 mm gap).
- **Drying method:** chamber with a slow programme (14-21 days), temperature 60-70°C (lower for oak and other hardwoods to avoid cracks), end-conditioning cycles (2-3 days at 85°C with humid air, to equalise moisture between the exterior and the core of the board).

**Threshold 12-15% (construction joinery):**

- Target environment: the **interior of a residence not always heated** or intermediate zones (entrance lobbies, heated garages, kitchens with frequent steam, bathrooms with ventilation). Temperature 15-22°C, relative humidity 45-65%. The EMC of this environment is 10-13%.
- **Applications:** wooden windows and doors (interior, and exterior with impregnation), pine floors, interior wooden stairs, wainscoting in rooms not constantly heated, wooden fittings in kitchens, terrace tables (outdoor, covered).
- **Why this threshold:** wood delivered at 12-15% moisture content in a surrounding EMC of 10-13% will make a small movement (1-3 percentage points), with shrinkage within the acceptable tolerance. A higher moisture content than 8-10% (furniture) is possible, because construction joinery has a larger cross-section (boards 25-50 mm thick instead of 18 mm for furniture), which dampens the effects of a small movement.
- **Drying method:** chamber with a standard programme (8-14 days), temperature 70-80°C, less end-conditioning (1 day).

**Threshold 18% ± 2% (roof trusses and sheltered external structures):**

- Target environment: an **unheated, covered attic** (under the roof, but without heating and without mechanical ventilation). Annual temperature fluctuates from −15°C in winter to +30°C in summer under a roof warmed by the sun. Relative humidity 60-85% (depending on season). The EMC of this environment is 14-20%.
- **Applications:** rafters, purlins, wall plates, truss posts, struts, load-bearing roof elements, **all under a covering** (roof tile, sheet metal, felt). Not for external structures without cover (those require pressure impregnation, the topic of m3-w3-l3).
- **Why this threshold:** wood at 18% in an environment with EMC 15-18% is in equilibrium, does not shrink or expand significantly. If you delivered it at 10%, the wood would quickly start absorbing moisture (annual cycle of winter with high RH in the attic), swelling and cracking at joining points. If you delivered it at 25%, the wood would dry, shrink, and form gaps at joints.
- **Drying method:** chamber with a fast programme (6-10 days), temperature 80-90°C, without conditioning (a coarse-grained effect acceptable for concealed structures).

::: info
**Why not dry everything to 8%, since you can**. Two reasons. **Cost:** each day of chamber drying costs EGIDA about 180 zł/m³ (electricity, natural gas, chamber amortisation). Drying to 8% takes 21 days, to 12% 14 days, to 18% 7 days. The difference: 21 days × 180 = 3,780 zł/m³ vs 7 days × 180 = 1,260 zł/m³ for a 15 m³ stack. **Technology:** too aggressive a drying of hardwoods (oak, beech, ash) down to 8% in the chamber produces internal cracking (*honeycomb*), because the core releases moisture more slowly than the surface. For these species the only way to 8% is **combined drying**: the chamber to 12-14%, then out-of-campaign seasoning in a climate-controlled store for 3-6 months (further moisture release at the wood's own pace, without cracks).
:::

### Two technologies of moisture measurement

**Pin moisture meter (IRI-5P, Delmhorst, Wagner):**

Two electrode pins driven into the wood to a depth of **30 mm** (for boards 32-50 mm thick). Between the pins a direct current flows (low voltage, 9-24 V), the resistance measured by the meter. Dry wood is an **insulator** (resistance of millions of ohms), wet wood **conducts** (resistance of tens of kiloohms). Resistance falls exponentially with moisture. The microprocessor in the moisture meter converts the resistance into a moisture value according to the calibration curve for the specific wood species (you must select, before measurement: pine, spruce, oak, beech, ash).

**Advantages:**
- Accuracy ±0.5 percentage point (with correct species calibration).
- Cheapness (1,200-1,800 zł for a mid-range instrument).
- Reliability (little electronics, simple mechanics).
- Repeatability (the same point measured again gives the same result).

**Disadvantages:**
- **Leaves pin marks** (two points 2 mm in diameter each, 30 mm deep). For premium-class furniture boards this is a problem (you have to measure in a zone that will be removed in carpentry processing). For structural boards it is not a problem.
- **Measurement only up to 30 mm depth** (short pins). For boards 50-100 mm thick you can only measure the external zone, not the core.
- **Requires species selection** before measurement (calibration curve). A species mistake equals a systematic error of 1-3 percentage points.
- **Temperature affects** resistance: cold wood (below 10°C) gives higher resistance leading to a higher reading than the actual moisture content. Compensation is automatic in newer IRI-5P models, manual in older ones.

**Pinless moisture meter (Gann RTU 600, Merlin PM1E):**

An electrode built into the instrument's housing, **applied to the wood surface** without penetration. It measures the **dielectric conductance** of wood in a high-frequency electromagnetic field (MHz). The conductance correlates with water content (water has a high dielectric constant of 80, dry wood about 2-3). Measurement depth 20-40 mm depending on model.

**Advantages:**
- **Without penetration** (leaves no marks, irreplaceable for premium boards).
- **Speed** (1-2 second measurement, a large batch can be processed in a few minutes).
- **Does not require species selection** in the newest models (the device automatically recognises it from the conductance pattern).
- **Good for thicker boards** (measurement depth of 40 mm reaches the core of 50 mm boards).

**Disadvantages:**
- Accuracy ±1 percentage point (about 2x less precise than a pin meter).
- **Sensitivity to wood density**: dense hardwood (oak, ash, beech) gives a higher signal at the same moisture content as pine; calibration for dense species is not always accurate.
- **Sensitivity to temperature**: cold wood (< 10°C) gives lower readings. Measurement in winter in a fresh yard requires an additional correction.
- **Cost** (3,500-6,000 zł for mid-range Gann).

**Why we use both at the same time:**

Two instruments built on **different physical principles** (resistance vs conductance) have **independent sources of error**. The pin meter may have a species-calibration error (+2%), the pinless may have a temperature error (−1%). If both show 9.8% and 9.7% (a 0.1% difference), confidence in the result is high (independent errors do not cumulate in the same direction). If they show 9.8% and 12.2% (a 2.4% difference), one of the instruments is badly calibrated or damaged, both must be checked.

### Five-point stack measurement procedure

A sawn-timber stack after removal from the chamber typically has **20-50 layers**, each layer 4-8 boards, together 80-400 boards. Measuring every board would be unrealistic (2-3 hours per 100 boards), so we use **five-point sampling** (the PN-EN 13183-2 (Polish/European standard for measuring the moisture content of sawmill timber) principle).

**The five points:**

1. **Front-left corner of the stack**, layer 3 from the top (not the outermost, which is often the driest; the third is representative).
2. **Front-right corner**, layer 3 from the top.
3. **Rear-left corner**, layer 3 from the bottom (deep in the stack, the slowest to dry).
4. **Rear-right corner**, layer 3 from the bottom.
5. **Centre of the stack**, middle layer (horizontally and vertically).

For a 40-layer stack, points 1-2 are in layer 3 (from the top), points 3-4 are in layer 37 (3 from the bottom), point 5 in layer 20.

**At each point** a board is selected **at random** from the boards available in that layer (not the one at the edge, which is the outermost; pick the third from the edge). Pull the board onto the measurement table, or measure it in place if the instrument allows (the Gann pinless meter allows measurement in the stack, the pin meter requires space for driving the pins).

**Measurement:** with each instrument three times at different places on the board (end, centre, end); record the average of the three measurements. The initial count: 5 points × 3 measurements × 2 instruments = **30 measurements**, summarised in 10 numbers (5 points × 2 instruments).

**Interpretation:**

- **The average of the five points** gives the value representative of the batch.
- **The maximum deviation** (the difference between the highest and the lowest) gives an indication of homogeneity:
  - **≤ 2%**: class A (homogeneous drying, batch ready for release).
  - **2-3%**: class B (satisfactory drying, release with a note about the spread).
  - **> 3%**: class C (non-homogeneous drying, requires additional treatment: either re-drying the whole stack or splitting into homogeneous sub-groups).

### Equilibrium moisture content (EMC)

Wood in a room of a defined temperature and relative humidity (RH) of the air reaches, after a few weeks, a stable moisture level called the **equilibrium moisture content** (EMC, *Equilibrium Moisture Content*). EMC table for softwood (pine, spruce):

| Temperature | RH 30% | RH 40% | RH 50% | RH 60% | RH 70% | RH 80% | RH 90% |
|-------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| 0°C | 6.6% | 8.0% | 9.5% | 11.2% | 13.3% | 16.4% | 22.4% |
| 10°C | 6.3% | 7.7% | 9.2% | 10.9% | 13.0% | 16.0% | 21.8% |
| 20°C | 6.1% | 7.5% | 8.9% | 10.6% | 12.6% | 15.5% | 21.1% |
| 30°C | 5.8% | 7.2% | 8.6% | 10.2% | 12.1% | 14.9% | 20.3% |

Values from the USDA Wood Handbook, corrected for Polish wood (±0.3 percentage point).

**How to read this:**

- A detached house in Piaseczno, winter, central gas heating, temperature 22°C, RH 35% (dry from heating): EMC about 7%.
- The same house, summer, temperature 22°C, RH 55%: EMC about 10%.
- An unheated attic, winter, temperature 0°C, RH 80%: EMC about 16%.
- An unheated attic, summer, temperature 20°C, RH 60%: EMC about 11%.
- A garage, over the year, average temperature 8°C, RH 75%: EMC about 15%.

Wood adjusts to the average EMC in an **annual cycle** (drier winter, wetter summer). For a detached house, furniture placed in the living room will reach an EMC of about 8-9% (the average of 7% in winter and 10% in summer). For the furniture firm "Dąb-Meble" from Piaseczno, then, the customer orders sawn timber in the **8-10%** threshold: wood delivered in this threshold, once laid up in the furniture, does not change substantially.

::: tip
**How does the customer know which threshold to order in?**. From industry practice and normative requirements. The standard **PN-EN 14298** (Polish/European standard for the assessment of lumber drying quality) defines four drying classes: **KT-I** (8%, ±1, for furniture joinery), **KT-II** (12%, ±2, for construction joinery), **KT-III** (18%, ±3, for structures), **KT-IV** (>20%, for temporary structures). The customer's order refers to the KT class (e.g., "80 m³ of pine C24 class KT-II" = 12% ±2%). Moisture classification is thus a **separate parameter** from the strength class (C) and the visual class (G2). The full designation of a board is **"G2-2/C24/KT-II"** (visual G2-2, strength C24, moisture 12% ±2%).
:::

### Operator decisions at measurement: diagram

| Average | Deviation | Target threshold | Decision |
|---------|-----------|----------------|---------|
| Within threshold | ≤ 2% | 8-10% (KT-I) | **Release**, WSP-002 card class A |
| Within threshold | 2-3% | 8-10% | **Release** with a note about the spread, class B |
| Within threshold | > 3% | 8-10% | **Split the stack** into homogeneous sub-groups, re-measure |

| Above threshold by 1.5-3% | any | 8-10% | **Re-drying P6**, 7-14 days |

**A note on "too dry" wood**: if a batch at 7% moisture is delivered to a customer on 8-10%, the wood **will start to absorb moisture** from the customer's environment (EMC 8%). Within 2-4 weeks it will rise to 8%. **Swelling of 0.5-1% of tangential shrinkage** = boards wider by 1-2 mm. For furniture with frames this may be a problem (a drawer sticks, doors pinch). That is why the customer sometimes writes explicitly: not less than 8%. PN-EN 14298 permits delivery of wood drier than the threshold, but the carpenter does not always accept it.

### Documentation: the WSP-002 card

EGIDA form WSP-002 (pre-release stack moisture measurement card):

- **Batch identifier:** stack number, species, cross-section, length, volume.
- **Drying programme:** chamber, programme (fast/standard/slow), start date, end date, total number of days.
- **Measurement:**
  - Date, hour, wood temperature (°C).
  - Instrument 1 (model, serial number, date of last calibration): 5 point results × 3 measurements per point.
  - Instrument 2 (model, serial number, date of last calibration): 5 point results × 3 measurements per point.
  - Average of the 5 points (for each instrument).
  - Maximum deviation (highest − lowest).
  - Homogeneity class: A (≤ 2%), B (2-3%), C (> 3%).
- **Target moisture threshold:** KT-I (8-10%), KT-II (12-15%), KT-III (18±2%).
- **Operator's decision:** release / re-drying P6 / full cycle / splitting the stack / other.
- **Operator's signature** (with the permission category noted: a helper M1 signs alongside the operator, a self-reliant M3 operator signs alone).
- **Control signature** (grader or shift leader, once a week randomly, to validate the quality of the measurement).

The card goes into the **batch file** together with other documents (grading card KLS-001 or KLW-001, raw-material intake card, WZ dispatch note). Retention **3 years**. It serves as evidence against a customer's complaint (the customer claims: "I received wood 16% wet"; the sawmill shows WSP-002 with a result of 9.3% on the day of release, arguing that the wood absorbed moisture in transit or at the customer's).

### Summary of lesson five

Wood moisture content is a **parameter parallel** to the strength class (C) and the visual class (G2). Three target thresholds in line with PN-EN 14298: **KT-I (8-10%)** for furniture joinery, **KT-II (12-15%)** for construction joinery, **KT-III (18% ±2%)** for roof trusses and sheltered external structures.

Two measurement technologies: **pin** (electrical resistance, accuracy ±0.5 percentage point, leaves marks) and **pinless** (dielectric conductance, accuracy ±1 percentage point, no penetration). Using both at the same time provides **cross-verification**: a difference up to 1 percentage point is acceptable, above that means one of the instruments has drifted.

The **five-point stack measurement** procedure: four corners (layer 3 from the top/bottom) plus the centre. Each point three times, with both instruments. Interpretation: average (representative value) plus maximum deviation (homogeneity, class A/B/C).

The equilibrium moisture content (EMC) of wood depends on temperature and relative humidity of the environment. In a heated residential home EMC 7-10% (hence the KT-I threshold). In an unheated attic EMC 14-18% (hence the KT-III threshold).

The operator's decision depends on the comparison of the average with the threshold and on the deviation. Release when within threshold and deviation class A/B. Re-drying P6 (5-14 days) when above threshold by 1.5-3%. Full second cycle when above by more than 3%. Splitting the stack when the deviation is class C. Documentation on the WSP-002 card (3-year retention, evidence against complaints).

Today Rustam learned that two almost identical batches (4.8 m³ pine and 6.2 m³ spruce for the same customer "Dąb-Meble") produced two different decisions: pine to release (9.3% within threshold), spruce to re-drying (11.9% above threshold). The difference stemmed from the drying programme: pine 22 days (slow), spruce 14 days (medium). A shorter programme, a lower drying effect, wet wood. Planning the drying campaign (the topic of m3-w3-l3) is **a conscious decision about the cycle length** depending on species and target threshold.

In the next lesson (m3-w2-l6) we move to **bands and saws**: choice of band type and tooth geometry for the wood species and the assortment of the order. Stellite steel for dry oak, alloy for fresh pine, three tooth pitches, band width for P3 vs P1.

## Key terms

- **Wood moisture content** (the mass of water in wood divided by the mass of dry wood, expressed as %; fresh wood 50-80%, lumber after drying 8-18%, measured with a pin or pinless moisture meter)
  - pl: wilgotność drewna (masa wody w drewnie podzielona przez masę drewna suchego, wyrażona w %; drewno świeże 50-80%, tarcica po suszeniu 8-18%, mierzy się wilgotnościomierzem iglicowym lub pojemnościowym)
  - es: contenido de humedad de la madera (la masa de agua en la madera dividida por la masa de madera seca, expresada en %; madera fresca 50-80%, madera aserrada tras secado 8-18%, se mide con higrómetro de punzones o sin punzones)
  - uk: вологість деревини (маса води в деревині поділена на масу сухої деревини, виражена у %; свіжа деревина 50-80%, пиломатеріали після сушіння 8-18%, вимірюється голчастим або безголчастим вологоміром)
- **The three target thresholds KT-I/II/III** (PN-EN 14298, KT-I 8-10% for furniture joinery, KT-II 12-15% for construction joinery, KT-III 18±2% for roof trusses, the threshold choice depends on the customer's target environment and that environment's equilibrium moisture content)
  - pl: trzy progi docelowe KT-I/II/III (PN-EN 14298, KT-I 8-10% dla stolarki meblowej, KT-II 12-15% dla stolarki budowlanej, KT-III 18±2% dla więźby dachowej, wybór progu zależy od docelowego otoczenia klienta i wilgotności równowagowej tego otoczenia)
  - es: los tres umbrales objetivo KT-I/II/III (PN-EN 14298, KT-I 8-10% para carpintería de muebles, KT-II 12-15% para carpintería de obra, KT-III 18±2% para cerchas de cubierta, la elección del umbral depende del entorno objetivo del cliente y del contenido de humedad de equilibrio de ese entorno)
  - uk: три цільові пороги KT-I/II/III (PN-EN 14298, KT-I 8-10% для меблевої столярки, KT-II 12-15% для будівельної столярки, KT-III 18±2% для кроквяної конструкції, вибір порога залежить від цільового середовища клієнта і рівноважної вологості цього середовища)
- **Equilibrium moisture content (EMC)** (the moisture level wood reaches in a given environment of temperature and relative humidity; 7-10% for a heated residential interior, 14-18% for an unheated attic)
  - pl: wilgotność równowagowa EMC (*Equilibrium Moisture Content*, poziom wilgotności, który drewno osiąga w danym otoczeniu temperatury i wilgotności względnej; dla domu mieszkalnego 7-10%, dla strychu nieogrzewanego 14-18%)
  - es: contenido de humedad de equilibrio (EMC, el nivel de humedad que la madera alcanza en un entorno dado de temperatura y humedad relativa; 7-10% para un interior residencial calefactado, 14-18% para un desván sin calefacción)
  - uk: рівноважний вміст вологи (EMC, рівень вологості, якого деревина досягає у даному середовищі температури і відносної вологості; 7-10% для опалюваного житлового інтер'єру, 14-18% для неопалюваного горища)
- **Pin moisture meter** (an instrument measuring electrical resistance between two pins driven into the wood to 30 mm depth; accuracy ±0.5 percentage point, leaves pin marks, requires species selection, IRI-5P a typical Polish model)
  - pl: wilgotnościomierz iglicowy (przyrząd mierzący opór elektryczny między dwiema igłami wbitymi w drewno na 30 mm głębokości; dokładność ±0,5% punkt, zostawia ślady igieł, wymaga wyboru gatunku, IRI-5P typowy model polski)
  - es: higrómetro de punzones (instrumento que mide la resistencia eléctrica entre dos punzones clavados en la madera a 30 mm de profundidad; precisión ±0,5 puntos porcentuales, deja marcas de punzones, requiere selección de especie, IRI-5P modelo polaco típico)
  - uk: голчастий вологомір (прилад, що вимірює електричний опір між двома голками, вбитими в деревину на глибину 30 mm; точність ±0,5 відсоткового пункту, залишає сліди голок, вимагає вибору породи, IRI-5P типова польська модель)
- **Pinless moisture meter** (an instrument measuring the dielectric conductance of wood without penetrating the surface, measurement depth 20-40 mm, accuracy ±1 percentage point, fast and non-invasive, Gann RTU 600 a typical German model)
  - pl: wilgotnościomierz pojemnościowy (przyrząd mierzący przewodność dielektryczną drewna bez penetracji powierzchni, głębokość pomiaru 20-40 mm, dokładność ±1% punkt, szybki i nieinwazyjny, Gann RTU 600 typowy model niemiecki)
  - es: higrómetro sin punzones (instrumento que mide la conductancia dieléctrica de la madera sin penetrar la superficie, profundidad de medición 20-40 mm, precisión ±1 punto porcentual, rápido y no invasivo, Gann RTU 600 modelo alemán típico)
  - uk: безголчастий вологомір (прилад, що вимірює діелектричну провідність деревини без проникнення в поверхню, глибина вимірювання 20-40 mm, точність ±1 відсотковий пункт, швидкий і неінвазивний, Gann RTU 600 типова німецька модель)
- **Cross-verification of instruments** (using two instruments based on different physical principles (resistance + conductance) to minimise the calibration error; a difference up to 1 percentage point is acceptable, above it signals a drift)
  - pl: krzyżowa weryfikacja przyrządów (użycie dwóch przyrządów o różnych zasadach fizycznych (opór + przewodność) dla minimalizacji błędu kalibracji; różnica do 1% punkt akceptowalna, powyżej sygnał rozregulowania)
  - es: verificación cruzada de instrumentos (uso de dos instrumentos basados en principios físicos distintos (resistencia + conductancia) para minimizar el error de calibración; diferencia hasta 1 punto porcentual aceptable, por encima indica una desviación)
  - uk: перехресна перевірка приладів (використання двох приладів на різних фізичних принципах (опір + провідність) для мінімізації похибки калібрування; різниця до 1 відсоткового пункту прийнятна, вище сигналізує про збиття)
- **Five-point stack measurement** (the PN-EN 13183-2 procedure, four corners of the stack (layer 3 from top/bottom) plus the centre; each point is measured three times with both instruments; the average gives the representative value, the maximum deviation gives the homogeneity class A/B/C)
  - pl: pięć punktów pomiaru na stosie (procedura PN-EN 13183-2, cztery rogi stosu (warstwa 3 od góry/dołu) plus środek; każdy punkt trzykrotnie oboma przyrządami; średnia daje wartość reprezentatywną, maksymalne odchylenie daje jednorodność klasa A/B/C)
  - es: medición de cinco puntos en la pila (procedimiento PN-EN 13183-2, cuatro esquinas de la pila (capa 3 desde arriba/abajo) más el centro; cada punto se mide tres veces con ambos instrumentos; la media da el valor representativo, la desviación máxima da la homogeneidad clase A/B/C)
  - uk: вимірювання у п'яти точках штабеля (процедура PN-EN 13183-2, чотири кути штабеля (шар 3 зверху/знизу) плюс центр; кожну точку вимірюють тричі обома приладами; середнє дає репрезентативне значення, максимальне відхилення дає однорідність класу A/B/C)
- **WSP-002 card** (EGIDA's pre-release stack moisture measurement card, with the 5 measurement points, average, deviation, operator decision (release / re-dry / split stack), operator signature, 3-year retention, evidence against complaints)
  - pl: karta WSP-002 (karta pomiaru wilgotności stosu przed wydaniem, EGIDA formularz z 5 punktami pomiaru, średnią, odchyleniem, decyzją operatora (wydanie/dosuszenie/rozdzielenie), podpisem operatora, retencja 3 lata, dowód przed reklamacjami)
  - es: ficha WSP-002 (ficha de medición de humedad de la pila antes de la salida, de EGIDA, con los 5 puntos de medición, la media, la desviación, la decisión del operador (salida / resecar / separar pila), la firma del operador, retención de 3 años, prueba frente a reclamaciones)
  - uk: картка WSP-002 (картка вимірювання вологості штабеля перед видачею, формуляр EGIDA з 5 точками вимірювання, середнім, відхиленням, рішенням оператора (видача / досушування / поділ штабеля), підписом оператора, зберігання 3 роки, доказ проти рекламацій)
- **Three post-measurement operator decisions** (release (within threshold, deviation class A/B), re-drying in P6 for 5-14 days (above threshold by 1.5-3%), stack splitting (deviation class C); the decision is recorded in WSP-002 and governs the batch's next step)
  - pl: trzy decyzje operatora po pomiarze (wydanie (w progu i odchylenie klasy A/B), dosuszenie P6 5-14 dni (powyżej progu o 1,5-3%), rozdzielenie stosu (odchylenie klasy C); decyzja zapisana w WSP-002, warunkuje kolejny krok partii)
  - es: tres decisiones del operador tras la medición (salida (dentro del umbral, desviación clase A/B), resecado en P6 durante 5-14 días (por encima del umbral en 1,5-3%), separación de la pila (desviación clase C); la decisión queda registrada en WSP-002 y determina el siguiente paso del lote)
  - uk: три рішення оператора після вимірювання (видача (у межах порога, відхилення класу A/B), досушування в P6 протягом 5-14 днів (понад поріг на 1,5-3%), поділ штабеля (відхилення класу C); рішення записують у WSP-002, воно визначає наступний крок партії)
- **The full board designation G2-x/Cy/KT-z** (three independent parameters describing the board: visual class G2 (0-3), strength class C (16-35), moisture class KT (I-IV); a full designation on the board end-face e.g. G2-2/C24/KT-II)
  - pl: oznaczenie pełne deski G2-x/Cy/KT-z (trzy niezależne parametry opisujące deskę: klasa wizualna G2 (0-3), klasa wytrzymałościowa C (16-35), klasa wilgotności KT (I-IV); pełne oznaczenie na czole deski np. G2-2/C24/KT-II)
  - es: designación completa de la tabla G2-x/Cy/KT-z (tres parámetros independientes que describen la tabla: clase visual G2 (0-3), clase de resistencia C (16-35), clase de humedad KT (I-IV); designación completa en el testero de la tabla p. ej. G2-2/C24/KT-II)
  - uk: повне позначення дошки G2-x/Cy/KT-z (три незалежних параметри, що описують дошку: візуальний клас G2 (0-3), клас міцності C (16-35), клас вологості KT (I-IV); повне позначення на торці дошки напр. G2-2/C24/KT-II)
