## II. Course data

**Title of the Course**: „Praca w tartaku" ("Work in a sawmill") - a course preparing for the performance of work at Polish sawmills and for the acquisition of in-demand qualifications on the Polish labour market, in particular in the area of wood processing for the needs of the construction, joinery and furniture industries.

**Current status of the Course Participant's attendance**[^4]:

| Field to be marked | Scope |
|--------------------|-------|
| ☐ Module M1 - Auxiliary worker at the sawmill | 4 weeks / 72 didactic hours |
| ☐ Module M2 - Sawmill equipment operator under supervision | 4 weeks / 112 didactic hours |
| ☐ Module M3 - Independent operator | 4 weeks / 132 didactic hours |
| ☐ Entire course „Praca w tartaku" (M1 + M2 + M3 in progress) | 12 weeks / 316 didactic hours |

One of the above scopes is to be selected, corresponding to the current stage of the Course Participant's attendance in the Course as at the date of issuance of this Certificate. This Certificate confirms exclusively the **fact of attendance** of the selected scope of the Course and is not a document confirming its completion or the acquisition of qualifications.

## IV. Organisational structure of the Course

The Course consists of three didactic components, the location of which depends on organisational conditions:

| Component | Character | Location |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **Theoretical part** | lectures, discussions, debates, written tests | training room of the Foundation in Opole (ul. Ozimska 14-16); admissibly the workshop of the Foundation or a room at the Entrepreneur's plant, provided that organisational conditions are ensured |
| **Workshop part** | simple practical exercises, demonstrations, instructional sessions | workshop of the Foundation in Opole; admissibly the Entrepreneur's plant, provided that organisational conditions are ensured |
| **Core practical part** | performance of actual sawmill tasks under supervision | **always at the Entrepreneur's sawmill plant** (address as in section III) |

## VI. Legal basis and status of the Course

The Course is conducted as part of the **statutory activity of the Foundation**, on the basis of the provisions of its statute (the statutory objectives of the Foundation have been made available for inspection on the website: https://fundacjaegida.eu), in particular on the basis of:

- Article 3 paragraph 1 in conjunction with Article 4 paragraph 1 items 2, 5a and 8 of the "Act of 24 April 2003 on Public Benefit Activity and Volunteerism" (consolidated text Journal of Laws of 2024, item 1491, as amended),
- Article 93 of the "Act of 7 September 1991 on the Education System" (consolidated text Journal of Laws of 2024, item 750, as amended), to the extent that the Course prepares for the recognition of qualifications obtained outside Poland,

and on the basis of the **agreement on co-organisation of the practical course** concluded between the Foundation and the Entrepreneur and the **agreement on conducting a practical course** concluded with the Course Participant.

The practical course is free of charge for the Course Participant.

---

## VII. Purpose of the Certificate

This Certificate is issued for the purposes of:

a) presentation to competent public administration authorities, in particular to the authorities competent in matters of residence and work of foreigners on the territory of the Republic of Poland,

b) confirmation of the status of a participant of the Course before employers, labour market institutions and other entities requiring such confirmation,

c) internal documentation of the Course maintained by the Foundation and the Entrepreneur.

The Certificate does not replace a document confirming the completion of a module or of the Course, and in particular does not confirm the acquisition of vocational qualifications within the meaning of separate provisions.
