2. You understand the **function of the tooth set** (*tooth set*). Without a set the band would cut **a kerf exactly the thickness of its own strip** (for a 38 mm band with a 1.0 mm strip = a 1.0 mm kerf), but then the band would have no clearance in the kerf, it would jam, overheat, break. A set of 0.6 mm to each side gives a **2.2 mm kerf** (1 mm strip + 2 x 0.6 mm set), provides a 0.6 mm clearance between band and wood on both sides of the kerf, wood does not touch the strip, only the teeth, friction minimal. **Permissible tolerance**: 0.6 ± 0.1 mm. The hook angle is factory-set, **it cannot be changed at the sawmill**. In reconditioning we grind only the tooth face (sharpening); the shape and angle remain. For a stellite 38/15 oak band the hook angle is 10°. For an alloy 38/22 pine band it is 20°.
4. You know the **four signs of tooth dulling** visible without instruments during normal cutting. **Wavy kerf** (*wavy kerf*, the board surface has regular "waves" every 100-200 mm, dulled teeth do not bite in consistently), **burn marks** (*burn marks*, brown stripes with a burnt smell, excessive friction carbonises the lignin), **smoking from the kerf** (*smoking*, kerf temperature exceeds 120°C, wood decomposes thermally), **increased feed resistance** (the operator feels in the lever that the saw "pulls hard", the motor runs at higher revs). One sign = warning, **two or more at the same time = STOP cutting, replace the band**.
5. You know about **micro-cracks at the tooth root** (*tooth base cracks*, the most dangerous band defect, invisible without a loupe). They arise through **material fatigue** at the point of greatest stress (the tooth gullet, where the tooth base joins the strip). After 3-5 m³ of oak or beech cutting, after 8-12 m³ of pine or spruce. ## Content

### The anatomy of a band-saw tooth

A band saw on a Wood-Mizer LT70 has **280 teeth over a length of 4.2 m** (for the 15 mm pitch from l6). Each tooth is a tiny metal structure with strictly defined geometric parameters. Five basic elements you must recognise:

**1. The tooth tip** (*tooth tip*, the cutting edge).

The most forward point of the tooth, the one that **first comes into contact with the wood** during cutting. It has the shape of a triangular tip with a 10-15° apex angle. A sharp tip = minimal contact with the wood = low friction = little heat. A dulled tip = contact over a larger surface = high friction = overheating.

New band: the radius of tip rounding is ≤0.02 mm (almost a point, under a microscope there is a clear vertex). After 2-3 m³ of cutting the radius grows to 0.05-0.08 mm (the onset of dulling visible under a loupe). After 5-6 m³: 0.15-0.20 mm (clear curvature under a 10x loupe, the tip looks like "a rounded eraser"). After 8-10 m³: >0.3 mm (visible with the naked eye, without a loupe, the symptoms begin to affect the kerf).

**2. The tooth gullet** (*tooth gullet*, the space between two adjacent teeth).

The pocket into which chips fall during cutting. The size of the gullet is set by the **pitch** from l6 (22 mm coarse = large gullet for wet wood, 15 mm fine = small gullet for dry wood). The gullet has a drop shape, with a narrow bottom (where the tooth "sits" on the strip) and a wider top (where chips can be ejected).

**A critical diagnostic point**: in the gullet, exactly at the place where **the tooth base joins the strip**, micro-cracks arise (described below). That is why the loupe during inspection looks first of all into the gullet, not at the tip.

**3. The tooth root** (*tooth root*).

The point of physical connection between the tooth and the band strip. For stellite the root is **the point of thermal overlay** (at the factory stellite was deposited on the steel base). For alloy the root is **an integral part of the strip** (the tooth cut from the strip, one material). Bi-metal: the root is **an electron weld** between the strip and a tooth of a different material.

Stresses during cutting flow from the tip downward, through the tooth, to the root. The root takes the **full load** onto the strip. Statistically this is where micro-cracks begin.

**4. The hook angle** (*hook angle* or *rake angle*, the front face of the tooth).

The angle between the front face of the tooth (the one that bites into the wood) and the axis perpendicular to the strip of the band. It defines the **aggressiveness of cutting**.

A positive angle (teeth leaning "forward", in the cutting direction) = aggressive. A negative angle (teeth leaning "backward") = gentle. In sawmill practice we only use positive angles, but of different values (10° for hard species, 20° for soft).

**5. The clearance angle** (*clearance angle*, the back face of the tooth).

The angle between the back face of the tooth and the surface of the wood. It ensures that the back of the tooth **does not touch the wood** after leaving the kerf; only the tip bites in, and the back moves freely. Without a clearance angle the back of the tooth would rub against the wood = friction = heat = quick dulling.

The clearance angle is standard for all sawmill bands: **5-8°**. It does not require operational adjustment.

**6. The tooth set** (*tooth set*, sideways deflection of the tooth from the plane of the strip).

A separate category. It is not a feature of the **shape** of the tooth, but of its **position in space**. Teeth are alternately deflected sideways from the axis of the strip: every second one to the left, every second one to the right, by 0.5-0.7 mm each. That is why the set can be changed during reconditioning (the teeth can be bent anew at the workshop), while the hook angle cannot (that would be a completely different tooth, a different cutting geometry).

::: info
**Why do we distinguish hook angle from set?**

The hook angle decides **how deep** the tooth bites into the wood in a single cycle (aggressiveness). The set decides **the width of the kerf** (clearance between band and wood). These are two independent parameters, even though both concern tooth geometry.

Example. The same 38 mm stellite band can have:
- Hook angle 10° (for oak, factory-set, not changeable) + set 0.6 mm (standard, adjustable).
- Hook angle 10° + set after reconditioning reduced to 0.4 mm (too little).
- Hook angle 10° + set after reconditioning re-set to 0.6 mm (restored).

The angle does not change, the set does. That is why **reconditioning restores the set and sharpens the tip, but does not change the aggressiveness of the cut**.
:::

### Tooth set: mechanism and values

Imagine a band with no set, all teeth exactly in the plane of the strip. Strip thickness 1.0 mm, so the kerf would be 1.0 mm. The band enters the wood, the teeth would cut the wood, chips would form in the kerf, but the walls of the kerf would touch the **strip of the band** directly. Friction between the strip (metal) and the wood (fibres) would be enormous, heat would rise rapidly, the band would overheat after 30-40 cm of cutting and break at the weld or in a tooth gullet.

That is why teeth are deflected sideways: every second one to the left, every second one to the right, by 0.5-0.7 mm each. Thanks to this **the kerf is wider than the strip**: for a 1.0 mm strip + 2 x 0.6 mm set = **a 2.2 mm kerf**. In the kerf there is 0.6 mm clearance between strip and wood on each side. Wood touches only **the teeth** (which are hardened, with a stellite overlay), not the strip (which is soft, wears away quickly). Chips fall freely downward without blocking.

**Set values for the Wood-Mizer LT70 stellite 38 mm:**

- **Factory set (new band)**: 0.6 mm on each side.
- **Permissible tolerance**: ±0.1 mm (so 0.5-0.7 mm is still serviceable).
- **Kerf in the wood**: 1.0 (strip) + 2 x 0.6 = **2.2 mm**.
- **Measuring gauge**: a metal plate with a 0.6 mm reference notch and 0.5 mm, 0.7 mm, 0.8 mm slots. The operator touches the tip of the tooth and the strip of the band and sees which slot the tooth "enters". Direct reading.

**Set wear during use:**

During cutting the tooth presses to the left (or to the right, depending on the side) against the wood. The pressure gradually "straightens" the tooth back towards the axis of the strip. After 6-8 m³ of oak the set drops on average to 0.4-0.5 mm (the teeth "straighten out"). The kerf becomes narrower (1 + 2 x 0.45 = 1.9 mm), clearance between band and wood decreases, symptoms of jamming appear: band vibration, light burning of the kerf, band wander to the side.

Then reconditioning (external workshop, Ostrzex Skawina or Zmechanizowane Drewno Poznań from l6) **sets the teeth again** (bends them back to 0.6 mm). Such reconditioning costs 60-80 zł for stellite (see l6), takes 2-3 working days.

**Too much set (>0.8 mm)** is equally bad:
- Kerf 2.6 mm, wood loss 1-2% yield (wood turned into chips unnecessarily).
- The band has too much clearance in the kerf, loses stiffness, vibrates sideways, the kerf is crooked.
- It appears after incorrect reconditioning (an inexperienced workshop).

That is why after reconditioning we **always** verify the set with the gauge before mounting the band on the saw.

### The hook angle and the match to species

The hook angle decides **how aggressively the tooth bites into the wood**. Analogy: a kitchen knife can be sharpened at different angles. A very sharp edge (small hook angle) penetrates the meat fibre more easily, but also dulls faster. A gentler edge (larger angle) keeps its form longer, but requires more pressure.

In saw bands we have three standard hook angle values.

**10° for dry hardwoods (oak, beech, ash, tropical species):**

- The tooth "undercuts" the wood delicately, the edge goes in shallow in a single pass.
- Typical feed: 1.5-2.5 m/min for 30-50 mm thick oak boards.
- Advantages: **the wood does not split** (hard, silica-rich wood such as oak is brittle laterally; at too great an angle the tooth "invites" the wood to break into splinters around the kerf).
- Disadvantages: slower cutting, greater tooth wear (more cycles per m³).
- **Stellite 38/15 oak band: typical angle 10°.**

**12-15° for universal use (semi-dry pine, dried spruce, wet beech):**

- Compromise between aggressiveness and delicacy.
- Typical feed: 2-3 m/min.
- Advantages: **universality**, works across a wide range of species and moistures.
- Disadvantages: not optimised for extreme cases (too small for fresh soft pine, too large for very dry oak).
- **Standard alloy 38/19 band: typical angle 12-15°.**

**20° for fresh soft pine:**

- The tooth "bites in" deep in a single pass.
- Typical feed: 3-4 m/min for 50 x 150 mm boards of fresh pine.
- Advantages: **fast cutting**, high m³/hour output (for the seasonal pine campaign).
- Disadvantages: greater motor load, greater risk of the tooth breaking at the root (the tooth works deeper), unsuitable for hard wood.
- **Alloy 38/22 pine band: typical angle 20°.**

::: warning
**The hook angle is FACTORY-set, not operational.**

When we buy a band (stellite 320 zł/m, alloy 180 zł/m, bi-metal 450 zł/m), the hook angle is already defined in production and **cannot be changed at the sawmill**. Not even during external reconditioning. That is why, when you plan an oak campaign, you have to **buy** a band with a 10° angle, not "twist" an old pine 20° band into an oak one.

Consequences of an angle mismatch:

- **Angle too small (10°) for fresh pine**: the band slides over the wood, does not bite in effectively, feed has to be heavier, the teeth overheat (they work longer at each cycle), a tooth breaks at the root after 4-5 m³ instead of the expected 8-12. Economic loss: 30-50% of service life.

- **Angle too large (20°) for dry oak**: the tooth "invites" the oak to tear splinters around the kerf, the board surface is ragged (class G2 impossible, downgraded to G4 packaging class, value loss 600-900 zł/m³), the teeth remove too much wood at once, the band vibrates, the kerf is crooked, and even a band break under load is possible.

Check the band label before mounting: type + width + pitch + hook angle + date of reconditioning.
:::

### The four dulling signals (assessment during cutting)

Tooth dulling does not appear suddenly, but gradually. The band "works" harder and harder; the operator **feels and hears it** before seeing it in the kerf. Four signals grouped in order of occurrence (from early to advanced):

**Signal 1: increased feed resistance (*increased feed resistance*), early signal after 4-5 m³ of oak cutting.**

The operator feels in the feed lever that the saw "pulls hard". That means: at the same pressure on the lever as before, feed is slower. The 7.5 kW P3 motor runs at higher revs (the sound audibly higher, the bass of the motor becomes thinner and sharper).

The operator can continue cutting, but only for another 1-2 m³, then has to **stop** for inspection.

**Signal 2: wavy kerf (*wavy kerf*), medium signal after 5-7 m³.**

The surface of the board that has come out of the cut is not flat, it has regular "waves" every 100-200 mm along the length of the board. Wave amplitude 0.5-2 mm.

Cause: dulled teeth do not bite in consistently. Every second cycle the tooth of the band "skips" a layer of wood (not aggressive enough, tip rounded), then the next cycle "catches up" excessively (compensation). Result: surface waving.

A wavy kerf **always** means the board will not meet class G2-1 (smooth surface). It has to go to planing (*kantówkowanie*) before grading = a loss of time and money.

**Signal 3: burn marks (*burn marks*), advanced signal after 7-9 m³.**

On the kerf surface **brown stripes** of 10-50 cm are visible, sometimes accompanied by a **burnt smell** (the characteristic smell of carbonised wood lignin).

Cause: excessive friction between the (dulled) band and the wood raises the kerf temperature to 150-250°C. Above ~200°C cellulose decomposes thermally, lignin carbonises, leaving a brown trace.

Burning = **STOP cutting within 1-2 m³**. Continuation leads to further overheating, a tooth break at the root and potentially a band break.

**Signal 4: smoking from the kerf (*smoking*), a critical signal; cutting further endangers safety.**

Visible smoke (whitish or brown) comes out of the kerf during cutting. The kerf temperature exceeds 250°C, the wood starts to decompose thermally.

Smoking = **IMMEDIATE STOP**. Procedure:

1. Release the feed lever (the band stops entering the wood).
2. Switch off the band drive motor (it keeps rotating by inertia for 5-10 seconds).
3. Wait until the band has come to a complete stop.
4. Main switch + LOTO padlock (the procedure from m3-w1-l4).
5. Call the foreman or the grader to assess the band.

**Rule for combining signals:**

- One single signal = **warning**, you can finish the current log, but plan an inspection before the next one.
- Two signals at the same time = **STOP**, band replacement before continuing. Not planned.
- Three or four signals = **immediate danger**, all the smoking procedure steps + full band inspection.

### Micro-cracks at the tooth root

The most dangerous band defect, invisible to the naked eye, leading to **a band break during cutting**. A band break on a Wood-Mizer LT70 is a serious safety event: the band jumps off the wheels (one side loose, the other under 2200-2400 PSI tension), it can hit the guard (the guard is designed to withstand it, but the operator should not stand on the line of impact), in the worst case an operator injury. That is why regular inspection of micro-cracks is one of the **three key safety checks** on a band saw (together with tension and guides).

**Mechanism of micro-crack formation:**

The tooth is attached to the strip by the root (tooth root). Strip + root + tooth form one geometric structure, but are non-homogeneous in material (for stellite: steel strip, steel root, tooth with a stellite overlay). The transition between strip and tooth is **the point of the greatest change in stress** during cutting.

During cutting the tooth presses on the wood, the pressure transfers through the tooth down to the root, there disperses into the strip. The pressing phase = the tooth works. The release phase (when the tooth leaves the kerf between wheel revolutions) = the tooth returns to an uncut state. These two phases repeat **thousands of times per minute** (band speed on a Wood-Mizer LT70: 1500-2000 m/min, that is 280 teeth x 20-25 revs/s = 5600-7000 cycles per tooth per second).

Material fatigue: each load cycle causes **a microscopic plastic deformation** at the point of greatest stress. After thousands of cycles microscopic discontinuities (dislocations in the crystalline lattice of the steel) link up into **a micro-crack**, a thin line 0.05-0.15 mm long at the start.

The micro-crack typically appears in **the tooth gullet**, exactly where the tooth base begins its cut-out from the strip. Perpendicular to the axis of the strip (across, not along). Invisible to the naked eye, visible only under a 10x loupe with oblique light.

**Micro-crack propagation:**

A micro-crack is not static. Under each successive load cycle it **grows**. After 100-200 more cycles the length grows by 0.01-0.03 mm. The speed of propagation depends on:

- Stress (band tension 2200 PSI = slower, 2400 PSI = faster).
- Wood (oak = faster, pine = slower).
- Contaminants (sand, stones, nails hidden in the log = step propagation).

Once a micro-crack reaches a length of **1-2 mm**, it exceeds the critical strength threshold of the strip. In the next cycle it runs through the whole tooth base and strip = **strip break = band rupture**.

**When micro-cracks appear:**

- After **3-5 m³** of oak or beech cutting (faster for harder species).
- After **8-12 m³** of pine or spruce cutting.
- **Earlier** if: band tension too high (>2400 PSI, mounting error), wood with sand (the log lay in mud, the operator did not clean it), frozen wood without pressure reduction (from l6), strikes against nails hidden in the log (farmland, barbed wire, unexpected inclusions).

**Assessment of micro-cracks during inspection:**

Tools:
- **10x loupe** (mandatory, the kind used by Pani Ania, standard instrument of an accredited grader).
- **LED lamp** with a thin strip of light (oblique light at 30-45° from the surface of the band).
- **Results sheet** (a simple sheet with tooth numbers 1-280 and columns "micro-crack: yes/no/length").

Procedure:
1. Take the band off the saw (release the tension from l6, take it off the upper wheel, hold it by the ends).
2. Lay the band flat on a clean surface (workshop table with a cotton pad).
3. Turn on the LED lamp, set it at 30-45° from the surface of the band (oblique light makes scratches and cracks visible as dark shadows).
4. With the 10x loupe inspect **each gullet** (the space between teeth), precisely at the point where the strip joins the tooth base.
5. For each crack record: tooth number (from the factory weld, number 1, 2, 3, ...), length (0.1 mm / 0.2 mm / 0.3 mm), direction (across/along).

Inspection time for a stellite 38/15 oak band (280 teeth): 45-60 minutes (manually, thoroughly). With a second person (operator + grader, or operator + helper) it can be shortened to 30 minutes.

**Interpretation of results:**

- **0 micro-cracks on 280 teeth**: band fully serviceable, continue.
- **1 micro-crack, length <0.3 mm**: warning, reconditioning planned after the current campaign.
- **2-3 micro-cracks, lengths up to 0.3 mm**: **reconditioning or replacement**, depending on the band's history. If this is the first life cycle (new band) = reconditioning. If the third cycle (after 2 previous reconditionings) = replacement.
- **4+ micro-cracks, or one >0.5 mm**: **replacement, reconditioning too risky**. Responsible external workshops (Ostrzex, Zmechanizowane Drewno) do not accept bands with such damage.
- **Crack >1 mm**: **immediate replacement**, the band will break within 1-2 m³. Do not start the saw.

**What Mykoła learned from this inspection:**

- **Three inspection tools** (10x loupe, LED lamp with side light, set gauge). Each gives different information, together they create the full picture.
- **Six checkpoints** (tip, gullet, set, strip continuity, corrosion, tension). Missing any checkpoint = incomplete inspection.
- **Three-tier decision** (continue / recondition / replace) based on interpretation of results, not on "feel".
- **Thinking about the band's history** (which life cycle, how many previous reconditionings, strip width after grinding) is key to the decision. A new band with 1 micro-crack = reconditioning. A third reconditioning of a band with 3 cracks = replacement. The same picture under the loupe, different decisions.
- **KKS documentation** after each inspection (workstation maintenance card, more in l8).
:::

### Band inspection checklist before a campaign

Six items, filled in before every campaign or after every 5 m³ of hardwood cutting or 10 m³ of softwood:

| No. | Checkpoint | Tool | Expected value | Consequence of failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tooth tip (radius) | 10x loupe | <0.2 mm for 280 teeth | Reconditioning |
| 2 | Tooth gullet (micro-cracks) | 10x loupe + oblique light | 0-1 crack <0.3 mm | 2-3: reconditioning/replacement, 4+: replacement |
| 3 | Set (3 measurement points) | Set gauge | 0.6 ± 0.1 mm | Reconditioning |
| 4 | Strip continuity (weld) | 10x loupe | No cracks | Immediate replacement |
| 5 | Surface corrosion | Visual inspection | 0% (≤5% tarnish allowed) | Reconditioning or replacement |
| 6 | Tension (on the saw) | Pressure gauge | 2200-2400 PSI | Adjustment |

**Decision scheme**:

- All 6 points passed = **continue** (band fully serviceable).
- Points 3 + 1 (light dulling + set out of tolerance) = **reconditioning**.
- Point 2 (2-3 micro-cracks) + history not in third cycle = **reconditioning**.
- Point 2 (2-3 micro-cracks) + third life cycle = **replacement** (as in the scene above).
- Point 2 (4+ micro-cracks) or point 4 (weld crack) = **immediate replacement**.
- Point 6 (tension) always adjustable without replacing the band.

### Entries in the workstation maintenance card (KKS-001)

Every band inspection = an entry in the **workstation maintenance card KKS-001** (monthly, three-year retention, in line with WSP-002 from l5). The entry contains:

- Date and time of inspection.
- Operator (first and last name) + grader/inspector (if assisted).
- Band number (from the label: type + width + pitch + serial number + date of last reconditioning).
- Results of points 1-6 from the checklist.
- Decision: continue / recondition / replace.
- Signatures of operator and inspector.

KKS-001 is the main workstation maintenance documentation, described **in more detail in the next lesson** (l8, daily saw maintenance).

::: info
**What the inspection delivers in numbers:**

- **Preventing band breaks**: 90-95% of breaks can be predicted by loupe inspection of micro-cracks. The remaining 5-10% are sudden events (a nail strike, a factory defect, extreme material).
- **Cost of inspection**: 45 minutes of operator time + 15 minutes of grader time = ~50 zł (counted in labour hours). With inspection every 5-8 m³ of oak = about 6-8 zł/m³ additional.
- **Cost of a band break**: injury (potentially fatal per M1 L4) + production losses of 1-3 days of downtime (3-10 thousand zł) + new band (1344 zł stellite) + PIP investigation (not priced). Incomparable to the cost of inspection.
- **Conclusion**: inspection every campaign is the **cheapest safety insurance** in the sawmill.
:::

## Key terms

- **Bandsaw tooth anatomy** (five shape elements: tooth tip with a 10-15° apex angle, tooth gullet the space between teeth, tooth root the junction with the strip, hook angle the front face that decides aggressiveness, clearance angle the back face at a standard 5-8°; plus a sixth position element: tooth set, the sideways deflection of the tooth 0.5-0.7 mm per side)
  - pl: anatomia zęba piły taśmowej (pięć elementów kształtu: szpic tooth tip z kątem wierzchołkowym 10-15°, gardło tooth gullet przestrzeń między zębami, podstawa tooth root punkt połączenia z pasem, kąt natarcia hook angle przednia powierzchnia decydująca o agresywności, kąt przyłożenia clearance angle tylna powierzchnia 5-8° standard; plus szósty element położenia: rozwarcie tooth set boczne odchylenie zęba 0,5-0,7 mm na każdą stronę)
  - es: anatomía del diente de sierra de cinta (cinco elementos de forma: punta del diente tooth tip con ángulo de vértice de 10-15°, garganta del diente tooth gullet el espacio entre dientes, raíz del diente tooth root punto de unión con la cinta, ángulo de ataque hook angle la cara frontal que decide la agresividad, ángulo de incidencia clearance angle la cara trasera a 5-8° estándar; más un sexto elemento de posición: trabado tooth set, la desviación lateral del diente 0,5-0,7 mm por lado)
  - uk: анатомія зуба стрічкової пилки (п'ять елементів форми: кінчик зуба tooth tip з кутом вершини 10-15°, горло зуба tooth gullet простір між зубами, основа зуба tooth root місце з'єднання зі стрічкою, передній кут hook angle передня поверхня, що визначає агресивність, задній кут clearance angle задня поверхня зі стандартом 5-8°; плюс шостий елемент положення: розвід tooth set, бокове відхилення зуба 0,5-0,7 mm на кожну сторону)
- **Tooth set** (*tooth set*, sideways deflection of teeth alternately left-right, 0.6 ± 0.1 mm for 38 mm stellite; provides a kerf wider than the strip 2.2 mm for a 1.0 mm strip, 0.6 mm clearance on each side, the band does not jam; adjustable during external reconditioning for 60-80 zł, unlike the hook angle which is factory-set; wears down to 0.4-0.5 mm after 6-8 m³ of oak, then reconditioning is required)
  - pl: rozwarcie zębów (tooth set, boczne odchylenie zębów naprzemiennie lewo-prawo, 0,6 ± 0,1 mm dla stellite 38 mm; zapewnia rzaz szerszy niż pas 2,2 mm przy pasie 1,0 mm, luz 0,6 mm po obu stronach, taśma nie zacina się; regulowalny w regeneracji zewnętrznej 60-80 zł, w odróżnieniu od kąta natarcia który jest fabryczny; zużycie 0,4-0,5 mm po 6-8 m³ dębu, wtedy wymaga regeneracji)
  - es: trabado de dientes (desviación lateral de los dientes alternadamente izquierda-derecha, 0,6 ± 0,1 mm para estelita de 38 mm; proporciona un corte más ancho que la cinta 2,2 mm con cinta de 1,0 mm, 0,6 mm de holgura a cada lado, la cinta no se atasca; regulable en la regeneración externa 60-80 zł, a diferencia del ángulo de ataque que es de fábrica; se desgasta a 0,4-0,5 mm tras 6-8 m³ de roble, entonces requiere regeneración)
  - uk: розвід зубів (бокове відхилення зубів почергово ліво-право, 0,6 ± 0,1 mm для стелітової 38 mm; забезпечує пропил ширший за стрічку 2,2 mm при стрічці 1,0 mm, 0,6 mm зазору з кожного боку, стрічка не заклинює; регулюється в зовнішній регенерації 60-80 zł, на відміну від переднього кута який є заводський; спрацьовується до 0,4-0,5 mm після 6-8 m³ дуба, тоді потребує регенерації)
- **Hook/rake angle** (*hook/rake angle*, the angle of the tooth's front face relative to the axis perpendicular to the strip; decides cutting aggressiveness; 10° for dry hardwoods oak-beech-ash, 12-15° for universal use, 20° for soft fresh pine; factory-set, cannot be changed at the sawmill or during reconditioning; a mismatched angle = 30-50% loss of band life or tooth breakage)
  - pl: kąt natarcia (hook/rake angle, kąt przedniej powierzchni zęba wobec osi prostopadłej do pasa; decyduje o agresywności cięcia; 10° dla gatunków twardych suchych dąb-buk-jesion, 12-15° dla uniwersalnego zastosowania, 20° dla sosny świeżej miękkiej; fabryczny, nie można go zmienić w tartaku ani w regeneracji; niedopasowany kąt = 30-50% utraty żywotności taśmy lub złamanie zęba)
  - es: ángulo de ataque (hook/rake angle, el ángulo de la cara frontal del diente respecto al eje perpendicular a la cinta; decide la agresividad del corte; 10° para frondosas secas roble-haya-fresno, 12-15° para uso universal, 20° para pino fresco blando; de fábrica, no se puede cambiar en el aserradero ni en la regeneración; un ángulo no adecuado = 30-50% de pérdida de vida útil de la cinta o rotura del diente)
  - uk: передній кут (hook/rake angle, кут передньої поверхні зуба відносно осі перпендикулярної до стрічки; визначає агресивність різання; 10° для твердих сухих порід дуб-бук-ясен, 12-15° для універсального застосування, 20° для м'якої свіжої сосни; заводський, не можна змінити на лісопилці ані під час регенерації; невідповідний кут = 30-50% втрати терміну служби стрічки або злам зуба)
- **Four signs of tooth dulling** (observable without instruments during cutting: 1. increased feed resistance after 4-5 m³ of oak, engine at higher revs; 2. wavy kerf, waves every 100-200 mm after 5-7 m³; 3. burn marks, brown stripes with a burnt smell after 7-9 m³, kerf temperature 150-250°C; 4. smoking from the kerf, critical, temperature >250°C requiring an immediate STOP; one sign = warning, two or more = STOP band replacement)
  - pl: cztery sygnały stępienia zęba (obserwowalne bez przyrządów w trakcie cięcia: 1. wzrost oporu posuwu po 4-5 m³ dębu, silnik wyższe obroty; 2. falistość rzazu fale co 100-200 mm po 5-7 m³; 3. ślad palenia brązowe pasma z zapachem spalenizny po 7-9 m³ temperatura w rzazie 150-250°C; 4. dymienie z rzazu krytyczne temperatura >250°C wymagające natychmiastowy STOP; jeden sygnał = ostrzeżenie, dwa lub więcej = STOP wymiana taśmy)
  - es: cuatro señales de embotamiento del diente (observables sin instrumentos durante el corte: 1. aumento de la resistencia al avance tras 4-5 m³ de roble, motor a más revoluciones; 2. corte ondulado, ondas cada 100-200 mm tras 5-7 m³; 3. marcas de quemado, bandas marrones con olor a quemado tras 7-9 m³, temperatura del corte 150-250°C; 4. humo del corte, crítico, temperatura >250°C que exige STOP inmediato; una señal = aviso, dos o más = STOP y cambio de cinta)
  - uk: чотири ознаки затуплення зуба (спостережувані без приладів під час різання: 1. зростання опору подачі після 4-5 m³ дуба, двигун на вищих обертах; 2. хвилястий пропил, хвилі кожні 100-200 mm після 5-7 m³; 3. сліди пропалювання, коричневі смуги із запахом горілого після 7-9 m³, температура в пропилі 150-250°C; 4. дим з пропилу, критично, температура >250°C вимагає негайного STOP; одна ознака = попередження, дві або більше = STOP заміна стрічки)
- **Tooth base cracks** (*tooth base cracks*, the most dangerous band defect; arise from material fatigue after 3-5 m³ of oak or 8-12 m³ of pine, in the tooth gullet the point of highest stress; dark thin lines 0.1-0.3 mm perpendicular to the strip, visible only under a 10x loupe with side light; propagate 0.01-0.03 mm per 100-200 cutting cycles, at a length of 1-2 mm the band breaks; 0-1 crack = reconditioning, 3+ = replacement, >1 mm = immediate replacement)
  - pl: mikropęknięcia u podstawy zęba (tooth base cracks, najbardziej niebezpieczna wada taśmy; powstają przez zmęczenie materiału po 3-5 m³ dębu lub 8-12 m³ sosny, w gardle zęba miejscu największego naprężenia; ciemne cienkie linie 0,1-0,3 mm prostopadłe do pasa, widoczne tylko lupą 10× ze światłem ukośnym; propagują 0,01-0,03 mm na 100-200 cykli cięcia, przy długości 1-2 mm taśma pęka; 0-1 pęknięcie = regeneracja, 3+ = wymiana, >1 mm = bezzwłoczna wymiana)
  - es: microgrietas en la raíz del diente (tooth base cracks, el defecto más peligroso de la cinta; surgen por fatiga del material tras 3-5 m³ de roble o 8-12 m³ de pino, en la garganta del diente el punto de mayor tensión; líneas oscuras finas de 0,1-0,3 mm perpendiculares a la cinta, visibles solo con lupa 10× con luz lateral; se propagan 0,01-0,03 mm por 100-200 ciclos de corte, con una longitud de 1-2 mm la cinta se rompe; 0-1 grieta = regeneración, 3+ = sustitución, >1 mm = sustitución inmediata)
  - uk: мікротріщини біля основи зуба (tooth base cracks, найнебезпечніша вада стрічки; виникають через втому матеріалу після 3-5 m³ дуба або 8-12 m³ сосни, у горлі зуба, у точці найбільшого напруження; темні тонкі лінії 0,1-0,3 mm перпендикулярні до стрічки, видимі лише під лупою 10× з бічним світлом; поширюються 0,01-0,03 mm на 100-200 циклів різання, при довжині 1-2 mm стрічка розривається; 0-1 тріщина = регенерація, 3+ = заміна, >1 mm = негайна заміна)
- **Band inspection with a 10x loupe** (three-step procedure: unaided tooth-tip inspection, 10x loupe with LED side light at 30-45° on each tooth gullet 280 teeth for 38/15 stellite, set measurement with a gauge at three points along the band; 45-60 minutes by one person or 30 minutes by two; results recorded on the EGIDA-TAS-005 card with per-tooth fields)
  - pl: inspekcja taśmy lupą 10× (procedura trzyetapowa: oględziny szpica zęba bez lupy, lupa 10× ze światłem ukośnym LED pod kątem 30-45° na gardło każdego zęba 280 zębów dla stellite 38/15, pomiar rozwarcia szczelinomierzem w trzech punktach taśmy; czas 45-60 minut ręcznie lub 30 minut dwie osoby; wyniki zapisywane w karcie EGIDA-TAS-005 z polami per ząb)
  - es: inspección de la cinta con lupa 10× (procedimiento de tres pasos: inspección de la punta del diente sin lupa, lupa 10× con luz lateral LED a 30-45° en cada garganta de diente de 280 dientes para estelita 38/15, medición del trabado con galga en tres puntos de la cinta; 45-60 minutos por una persona o 30 minutos por dos; resultados registrados en la ficha EGIDA-TAS-005 con campos por diente)
  - uk: інспекція стрічки лупою 10× (трьохетапна процедура: огляд кінчика зуба без лупи, лупа 10× з бічним LED-світлом під кутом 30-45° на кожне горло зуба 280 зубів для стелітової 38/15, вимірювання розведення калібром у трьох точках стрічки; 45-60 хвилин однією особою або 30 хвилин двома; результати записуються в картці EGIDA-TAS-005 з полями на зуб)
- **Three-tier post-inspection decision** (continue all points passed, recondition set out of tolerance or 0-1 crack or light tip dulling, replace 2-3 cracks in the third life cycle or 4+ cracks or weld crack; decision based on results plus band history, not on gut feeling; old band scrapped when reconditioning is impossible, as responsible workshops do not accept bands with 3+ cracks or >0.5 mm)
  - pl: decyzja trzystopniowa po inspekcji (kontynuacja wszystkie punkty zaliczone, regeneracja rozwarcie poza tolerancją lub 0-1 mikropęknięcie lub lekkie stępienie szpica, wymiana 2-3 mikropęknięcia w trzecim cyklu życia lub 4+ pęknięć lub pęknięcie zgrzewu; decyzja oparta na wynikach plus historii taśmy, nie na „czuciu"; stara na złom gdy regeneracja niemożliwa, bo odpowiedzialne warsztaty nie przyjmują pęknięć <3 lub >0,5 mm)

  - es: galga de trabado (una placa metálica con una muesca de referencia de 0,6 mm y ranuras de 0,5/0,7/0,8 mm para medición directa del trabado del diente; el operador toca la placa con la punta del diente y lee por qué ranura entra, lectura directa; instrumento estándar del clasificador acreditado y del operador independiente, coste 40-80 zł, 10 segundos por punto)
