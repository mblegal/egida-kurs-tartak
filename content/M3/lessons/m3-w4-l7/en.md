### Eight principles of the M3 operator

::: warning
**The eight principles are not a list of bullet points to memorize. They are decision patterns.** Each principle answers the question "what do you do in situation X?". Monday's test will check whether you can apply these principles in a new situation you have not seen in the course. That is why the integration cases close this lesson, they show how the principles combine into a single decision.
:::

**Principle 1 (T1, l4 M3) – The external competency boundary is hard, the internal one is soft.** The M3 operator has two boundaries. External: the line between operator and manufacturer service (Wood-Mizer, BTM), hard, technological, not to be crossed. Example: replacement of a wheel bearing requires a hydraulic press + anti-tampering seal + UDT certification of the press + service warranty. Crossing it = void warranty + OHS breach + legal liability. Internal: the line between operator and EGIDA maintenance, soft, with partial overlap, context-dependent. Example: general machine vibration, maintenance diagnoses after the operator's initial assessment; wavy cut, the operator resolves it alone by replacing the blade. **Source: l8 T3 (17.06.2026, fault wheel runout 0.8 mm, escalation to Pan Krzysztof at Wood-Mizer Polska)**.

**Principle 2 (T1, l5 M3) – Parametric decision always before cutting, not during.** The M3 operator fills out card KDP-001 (Parametric Decisions Card) **before starting the bandsaw**, not during. Five parameters: (a) scheme selection (cant sawing A, quarter sawing B), (b) blade selection (stellite vs alloy type, tooth pitch 10/8/7 TPI, width), (c) blade tension (2200-2400 PSI depending on wood), (d) feed speed (pine 32, oak 18, beech 22 ft/min for LT70), (e) blade guides (2.5/3 mm or 3/4 mm). Entry on the form 5 minutes before cutting starts. Correction during = STOP + correction entry + justification. **Source: l1 T3 (26.05.2026, ZLE-2026-05-077, Rustam's first independent decision)**.

**Principle 3 (T2, l1 M3) – Documentation is part of the work, not paper after the work.** The M3 operator fills out **four system forms**: KDP-001 (parametric decisions, before start), KS-001 (shift card, during and at the end), KKS-001 (maintenance and machine condition, weekly and at faults), KAO-001 (operator authorization, during supervision of M2). Plus EGIDA project forms: KEP-001 (batch card with grader), EGIDA-RZ-001 (shift report), EGIDA-PZ-001 (handover to relief). Documentation filled out **in real time**, not from memory in the evening. ISO 9001 requires 15-minute audit availability. Retention 3-5 years paper + 10 years scan. **Source: l1 T4 (18.06.2026, KEP-001 with Pani Ania, first full batch of birch for MebelPro)**.

**Principle 4 (T2, l6 T3) – The economics of a single work order is not the economics of the sawmill.** The M3 operator sees the shift report of their own work orders (EGIDA-RZ-001 tabs 1-3). The margin on a single order can be negative without operator error. ZLE-2026-05-077 Rustam minus 529 zł, the loss results from small logs Ø 32 cm (lower limit of scheme A) + Drew-Sus cost 212 zł/m³ premium (deliberate investment in the customer's deadline). The operator does not take the loss personally. EGIDA sawmill cost structure for pine: raw material 34% + labor 19% + processing 17.5% + depreciation 16% + drying 7.5% + materials 3% + other 3%. Sale prices 2026: pine C24 1700 zł/m³, oak C24 2800, beech C24 2400. Impact of M3 parametric decisions on a 5 m³ batch: blade +/-800 zł, tension +/-370, speed +/-150, scheme +/-1500, total potential +/-2800 zł difference between an experienced M3 and a beginner. **Source: l6 T3 (10.06.2026, shift report ZLE-077)**.

**Principle 5 (T3, l2 T4) – A customer complaint is cooperation with the Civil Code.** The M3 operator understands the legal framework of complaints. Five citations from the Civil Code (Kodeks cywilny, KC) in practice: **Art. 556 of the Polish Civil Code** seller's warranty for physical and legal defects; **Art. 556¹ § 1 and § 4 KC** definition of a defect (non-compliance with the contract, lack of proper features); **Art. 560 § 1 and § 4 KC** buyer's rights (price reduction, withdrawal, non-material defect); **Art. 561 § 3 KC** reasonable period for removing the defect; **Art. 563 § 1 KC** buyer's obligations (inspection and notification within the customarily accepted period). **Polish Supreme Court ruling I NSNc 152/20 dated 17.02.2021**: the defect must be material to justify withdrawal. PN-EN 336 dimensional tolerance +/-2 mm non-material. Case Dom-Bud Mrągowo ZLE-2026-06-015: pine 174 mm instead of 175, customer accepts the non-material defect, documentation retention 12 months. **Source: l2 T4 (19.06.2026, Jacek Wesołowski Dom-Bud)**.

**Principle 6 (T3, l7 T3) – Supervision of M2 is pedagogy, not control.** The M3 operator supervising M2 applies the principle "I correct when wrong, not when different from mine" (EGIDA regulations § 8 sec. 3). The difference: supervision is a space with a safety net (correction on a substantive error); control is a shadow over the shoulder (correction on a style difference). Rustam supervises Adrian on 16.06.2026: three corrections during the shift (feed speed, misaligned blade guide, log in KDP-001), one uncorrected style difference (cutting order left-right vs center-outside). Card KAO-001 has five evaluation criteria (OHS, parameters, technique, sorting, documentation) x scale 1-5. Three decision thresholds: full 30 days (>=20/25), conditional 15 days (17-19/25), refusal (<17/25 or any criterion <3). **Supervisory bonus 15%** on top of the M3 rate. Rotation of supervision (different M3s for the same M2, to avoid style matching). **Source: l7 T3 (16.06.2026, Adrian Kowal, full authorization 21/25)**.

**Principle 7 (T3, l8 T3) – Fault diagnosis through three senses: sight, hearing, touch.** The M3 operator uses three diagnostic senses. **Sight** (the easiest, trained earliest): wave on the board, smoke, oil, blade teeth, chip color. **Hearing** (after a year of M3): normal motor vs moan, bearings even hum vs grinding, blade smooth vs whistle, hydraulic pumps buzz vs squeal. **Touch** (rarely appreciated, after a year of work): handle vibration (frequency, amplitude), panel heat, floor tremors, draft from extraction. EGIDA maxim: "we hear with the ear, we feel with the hand". Case: lower wheel runout 0.8 mm felt by touch at 10 Hz in the head handle after 30 s of cutting; if Rustam had not felt it, the fault would have been detected after 4-5 logs, loss of 20% of the batch = several thousand zł. Table of 7 types of faults (A3 poster next to P3) with operator/maintenance/service boundary. EGIDA statistics: 85% of stoppages = operator-level faults (blade change, motor thermal reset, extraction cleaning). **Source: l8 T3 (17.06.2026, wavy cut fault + wheel runout)**.

**Principle 8 (T4, l6 T4) – Your professional resources are your Excel account.** The M3 operator plans their career numerically. 8 paths after the basic course: (a) kiln drying master at BTM 8500 zł gross (6-month course 14000 zł), (b) foreman at AKS 4200 zł (4-month course 7500 zł), (c) ITB-certified grader 14500 zł (12-month course 18500 zł), (d) CNC operator (CadWork/SolidWorks course 5000-8000 zł), (e) UDT IIWJO W-M ZDZ Olsztyn 15-19.09.2026 (2500 zł, +350 zł/month), (f) SEP G1-E (1-week course 350-500 zł, +150 zł/month), (g) power saw operator (3-day course 450 zł), (h) UPP Poznań studies 3 years 24000 zł. **Technical Supervision Act of 2000, Art. 12-13** (UDT exam) and **Energy Law of 1997, Art. 54** (SEP qualifications) and **Labor Code Art. 103¹** (training leave) are the legal framework. Rustam chooses UDT + SEP, ROI 5.7 months, an investment of 3000 zł will yield 500 zł/month extra. **Source: l6 T4 (26.06.2026, Marek + Rustam at Mazurska café, Kętrzyn)**.

### Three meta-principles of the M1 → M2 → M3 arc

::: tip
**The meta-principles are not in the test material.** They are in the way of thinking about the course. A migrant student who has understood them sees their path as **expanding the circle of responsibility** (body → batch → shift), **deepening the formality of communication** (gesture → command → document), **reversing the direction of orders** (you receive → you issue).
:::

**Meta-principle 1 – From body safety to batch safety to shift safety.** In M1 you learned how not to die in the sawmill: STOP gestures, oak dust IARC 1 carcinogen, S3 boots, hearing protection at 85 dB, manual transport 30 kg man, log yard 37% of deaths. Responsibility = your own body and the bodies of colleagues within 5 m. In M2 you learned how not to waste a batch: cutting parameters blade 2200-2400 PSI, final moisture 12-15% for construction, grading A/B/C/D weakest-link rule, shift log 8 fields T/M/O/P, escalation 4W. Responsibility = 1-3 m³ batch under your tool. In M3 you learned how to run a shift: coordination of 4 communication channels, supervision of M2 with card KAO-001, handover to relief with EGIDA-PZ-001, shift report with margin, decision on fault escalation. Responsibility = 8-hour shift, the whole team available on the floor, economic value 5-15 thousand zł of daily production.

**Meta-principle 2 – From gestures to commands to documentation.** In M1 you communicated with **gestures** (5 basics: STOP, FEED, WAIT, STEP BACK, REVERSE) with the forklift operator and colleagues. Noise 95-110 dB forced non-verbal communication. Words a reserve. In M2 you learned **commands** (Polish words in conditions of silence, short orders "UWAGA", "STOP", "ZABLOKUJ") plus a **written dispatch** (10 fields: no., date, client, species, dimensions, quantity, priority, FSC, notes, signature) and a **shift log** (8 main fields + T/M/O/P + blades + 3-part faults). Communication grows in structure. In M3 you manage **multi-track documentation** (KDP-001 + KS-001 + KKS-001 + KAO-001 + KEP-001 + EGIDA-RZ-001 + EGIDA-PZ-001) + **you cite the law** (KC Art. 556-563 on complaints, KP Art. 100-151 on overtime, Technical Supervision Act of 2000 Art. 12-13 on UDT, Energy Law of 1997 Art. 54 on SEP) + **you write emails to customers**. The gesture has almost completely disappeared (only in brigade coordination), the word has become a tool of negotiation, the document has become a tool of legal evidence.

**Meta-principle 3 – From receiving orders to issuing orders.** In M1 you **received orders** from Wahan (stationary helper), from Marek (foreman), from Damian (operator). Your role: to execute. The question "why?" was forbidden in the first month, allowed in the fourth. In M2 you **received and acknowledged the dispatch** (5 steps: read twice, underline 3 parameters, ask using the scheme "I see X I understand Y", sign two copies, file the yellow one in the folder). A horizontal dialogue between operator and foreman. The question "why this scheme?" allowed, expected in the second month of work. In M3 you **issue orders** to Adrian (M2 under supervision), to Wahan (stationary M1 assisting at the trolley), to Anton Bondarenko (yard foreman, forklift operator, works in parallel). Card KAO-001 is your authorization decision. Your oral command "stop, change the blade" is binding for the mentee. Legal responsibility for the command, Art. 120 § 1 KP (the employee is liable for damage caused during the performance of work), Art. 100 § 1 KP (the employer/superior issues orders). Rustam went from receiving to issuing in 9 months. That is the promotion to M3.

### M3 operator profile – five competencies

::: example
**The profile is not a list of traits.** It is **five integrated competencies** that the foreman checks during the M2 → M3 promotion decision. Lack of one = postponing the promotion by 3-6 months. All five together = the foreman leaves you with an 8-hour shift without supervision and checks the results at the end (shift report + KS-001).
:::

**Competency 1 – Parametric decision before cutting, documented on KDP-001.** The M3 operator knows that a wrong parametric decision costs 150-2800 zł per batch. They do not guess. They consult doubts before start (a question to Marek or Pani Ania), write the decision on the form, justify the choice in one sentence. After cutting they verify (yield, blade wear, average ammeter). Rustam in this competency is rated 4/5 by Marek (June 2026): strong in pine and oak, average in beech (still few batches), weak in premium output class C30 (0 batches). To improve: supplementary course 15-19.09.2026 UDT.

**Competency 2 – Multi-form documentation in real time.** The M3 operator fills out 4-7 forms per shift (depending on the type of work order). Each form has legal retention (3-10 years). Each signed by the operator = professional responsibility. Rustam in this competency 5/5: writes in legible print, one-sentence justifications, 0 corrections from Marek in 3 weeks. Pani Ania confirms: "Rustam has the most careful KEP-001 in the M3 team".

**Competency 3 – Supervision of M2 pedagogically, not in a controlling way.** The M3 operator distinguishes a substantive error from a style difference. Rustam in this competency is rated 4/5 (Adrian's first authorization came out 21/25, good). To improve: be silent more at style differences, speak more at Adrian's good decisions (positive feedback in the middle, not only at the end). Foreman Marek suggests a technique: "1 praise per day minimum, 2 corrections maximum, separated in time".

**Competency 4 – Competency boundary: when alone, when to escalate.** The M3 operator knows when to escalate. Five tiers: operator → foreman → shift master → production manager → director. Plus an external path: operator → EGIDA maintenance → manufacturer service. Three urgency categories (IMMEDIATELY by phone, END OF SHIFT log, IN WRITING form within 30 days). Rustam in this competency 5/5: wheel runout escalated correctly (operator → foreman → Wood-Mizer Polska in 15 minutes), wavy cut resolved independently (85% of faults = operator-level, rightly).

**Competency 5 – Economic thinking of the shift.** The M3 operator reads shift report EGIDA-RZ-001 and understands the margin of their work orders. Does not take the loss personally, sees 6 process stages. Suggests improvements without crossing the decision boundary (the foreman decides). Rustam in this competency 3/5 (developing): sees the margin, does not yet suggest improvements. Pan Henryk forecasts: "in October Rustam will reach 4/5, in December 5/5, that is full M3 maturity".

## Key terms

- **operator M3** (PL) / **Level M3 operator** (EN) / **operador M3** (ES) / **оператор M3** (UK): independent operator, 3rd tier of EGIDA competency, runs the shift without ongoing supervision from the foreman
- **retrospektywa kursu** (PL) / **course retrospective** (EN) / **retrospectiva del curso** (ES) / **ретроспектива курсу** (UK): summary of 3 modules x 4 weeks = 96 lessons in one final lesson
- **8 zasad operatora M3** (PL) / **8 principles of the M3 operator** (EN) / **8 principios del operador M3** (ES) / **8 принципів оператора M3** (UK): principles 1-8 derived from blocks T1-T4 of the M3 course
- **3 meta-zasady kursu** (PL) / **3 course meta-principles** (EN) / **3 meta-principios del curso** (ES) / **3 мета-принципи курсу** (UK): the M1→M2→M3 arc in three dimensions (body/batch/shift, gestures/commands/documentation, receiving/issuing orders)
- **5 kompetencji operatora M3** (PL) / **5 M3 operator competencies** (EN) / **5 competencias del operador M3** (ES) / **5 компетенцій оператора M3** (UK): parametric decision, multi-form documentation, supervision of M2, competency boundary, economic thinking
- **KDP-001** (PL) / **KDP-001** (EN) / **KDP-001** (ES) / **KDP-001** (UK): Parametric Decisions Card, EGIDA form for decisions before cutting
- **KAO-001** (PL) / **KAO-001** (EN) / **KAO-001** (ES) / **KAO-001** (UK): Operator Authorization Card, EGIDA form for evaluating M2 under M3 supervision
- **granica kompetencji zewnętrzna** (PL) / **external competency boundary** (EN) / **límite externo de competencia** (ES) / **зовнішня межа компетенції** (UK): the line between operator and manufacturer service (hard, technological)
- **granica kompetencji wewnętrzna** (PL) / **internal competency boundary** (EN) / **límite interno de competencia** (ES) / **внутрішня межа компетенції** (UK): the line between operator and EGIDA maintenance (soft, with partial overlap)
- **piramida M3→M2→M1** (PL) / **M3→M2→M1 pyramid** (EN) / **pirámide M3→M2→M1** (ES) / **піраміда M3→M2→M1** (UK): hierarchy of operator competencies, with rotation of supervision
