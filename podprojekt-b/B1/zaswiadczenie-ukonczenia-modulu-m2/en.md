## II. Data of the Completed Module

**Course Title**: "Praca w tartaku" (Sawmill Work), a course preparing participants to perform work in Polish sawmills and to acquire qualifications in deficit in the Polish labour market, in particular in the area of wood processing for the needs of construction, joinery and the furniture industry.

**Completed Module**:

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Designation: | Module M2 |
| Vocational profile: | Operator of sawmill machines under supervision |
| Volume: | 4 weeks / 112 teaching hours |
| Scope of competences: | supervised operation of basic sawmill machines (band saws, frame saws, gang saws, circular saws), control of cutting parameters, knowledge of product assortments, identification of mechanical hazards and emergency procedures, keeping basic technical documentation of the processing operations |

This certificate confirms only the **successful completion of Module M2** and is not a document confirming completion of the entire "Praca w tartaku" Course, nor does it confirm the acquisition of vocational qualifications within the meaning of separate regulations.

## IV. Organisational Structure of the Module

Module M2 consisted of three didactic components, the location of which depended on organisational conditions:

| Component | Nature | Location |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| **Theoretical part** | lectures, discussions, debates, written tests | the Foundation's training room in Opole (ul. Ozimska 14-16); permissibly the Foundation's workshop or a room at the Entrepreneur's facility, provided they ensure the organisational conditions |
| **Workshop part** | simple practical exercises, demonstrations, instructions | the Foundation's workshop in Opole; permissibly the Entrepreneur's facility, provided it ensures the organisational conditions |
| **Core practical part** | performance of actual sawmill tasks under supervision | **always at the Entrepreneur's sawmill facility** (address as in section III) |

## VIII. Purpose of the Certificate

This certificate is issued for the purposes of:

a) presentation to competent public administration authorities, in particular authorities competent in matters of residence and employment of foreigners in the territory of the Republic of Poland,

b) confirmation of completion of Module M2 before employers, labour market institutions and other entities requesting such confirmation, in particular in order to demonstrate competences within the scope resulting from the profile "Operator of sawmill machines under supervision",

c) the internal documentation of the Course kept by the Foundation and the Entrepreneur.

The certificate confirms the successful completion of **only Module M2** of the "Praca w tartaku" Course. **It does not replace** the certificate of completion of the entire Course, which is issued separately upon successful passing of the final test of the entire Course (passing threshold: 70%, a minimum of 76 points out of 108 possible) in accordance with the agreement on conducting the practical course.
