# M3 Supplementary Final Quiz

## Purpose

This quiz is a **supplement to the l8 final test of Module 3** (108 pts, 70% pass threshold = 76 pts, weighting 20% M1 / 30% M2 / 50% M3). It contains **15 integrative questions**: each question **combines a minimum of two M3 lessons** (or lessons from two different modules in the case of meta-arc M1 -> M2 -> M3 questions). The answer key includes justifications referencing specific lessons and legal articles (Polish Civil Code, Polish Labour Code, Polish Technical Inspection Act, Polish Energy Law, PN-EN standards).

Used in two modes:

1. **Self-assessment mode**. The trainee solves it after the l8 test (if passed) to verify deeper integration of knowledge before the solo practical exam (LT70 oak FSC 12 m³ Bouwhout van Doorn, deadline +14 days). No impact on certification.
2. **Focused remedial mode**. A trainee who fails l8 solves selected quiz questions corresponding to areas with gaps (e.g. a trainee weak in block T3 does questions 9-12 integrating supervision + diagnosis + boundary of competence). There is no aggregate pass threshold here: the trainer reconstructs the gap diagnosis.

## Instructions

- **15 questions, 4 pts each = 60 pts maximum total**. Point distribution: 2 pts for the correct A/B/C/D selection + 2 pts for a written 2-4 sentence justification referencing a lesson or a legal provision.
- **13 integrative questions** (combining >= 2 M3 lessons) + **2 meta-arc questions** (M1 -> M2 -> M3 arc).
- Time: **60 minutes**. No consultations, no phone, no notes.
- If you do not know, write "I do not know" (0 pts, information for the trainer about the gap, no penalty).
- Write on paper with a pen. Corrections must be unambiguous (clear strikethrough + marking of the new answer).

## Questions

### Block T1: Responsibility

**Question 1 (integrates T1 l4 + l5 + T3 l8, 4 pts)**

You arrive for the 7:00 shift on sawmill P3 LT70. You begin start-up according to KDP-001 for order ZLE-2026-06-025 (oak C30, 5 m³). After the first test cut you hear an uneven hum from the lower wheel bearing + you feel an 8-10 Hz vibration in the head handle. Ammeter normal. Cut surface slightly wavy. What is this from the perspective of the M3 operator's boundary of competence?

A) Operator-level problem: replace the band, fill in KKS-001 and continue.
B) Maintenance (UR, EGIDA in-house maintenance) problem: soft internal boundary, report to the mechanic, wait for diagnosis, in the meantime KS-001 with an entry.
C) External service problem: hard external boundary, escalation to Mr. Krzysztof at Wood-Mizer Polska, immediate STOP, entry in KKS-001 + 4W call to Marek.
D) Non-issue: continue cutting, inspection only at the end of the shift.

*Justify in 2-3 sentences, referencing the source lesson and the boundary of competence.*

**Question 2 (integrates T1 l5 + T2 l6, 4 pts)**

You are filling in KDP-001 for fresh pine 32% MC (order Dom-Bud Mragowo, rafters 38x175x4050 mm, priority A, deadline +3 days). You have a choice between: scheme A cant sawing (faster, yield 52-55%) vs scheme B quarter sawing (slower, yield 48-51%, but better for furniture customers). The customer is a construction client, not a furniture client. Which parameters do you choose and how does this affect the order margin?

A) Scheme A + stellite band 7/8-A13 + speed 32 ft/min + tension 2200 PSI. Margin positive about +600-900 zł.
B) Scheme B + alloy band + speed 22 ft/min + tension 2400 PSI. Margin negative minus 800-1200 zł.
C) Scheme A + cheap alloy band + speed 40 ft/min + tension 1900 PSI. Margin theoretically positive but risk of waviness + shortened band life.
D) Scheme B + stellite band + speed 32 ft/min + tension 2600 PSI. Margin about +200 zł, excessive cost for a construction client.

*Justify the choice and explain the impact on margin by referring to the cost structure of a sawmill and the +/-2800 zł potential of parametric decisions from l6 T3.*

**Question 3 (integrates T1 l6 + KP art. 120 + T2 l6, 4 pts)**

After the shift it turns out that the LT70 sawmill ammeter is damaged (repair cost 4200 zł). The day before, the shift report showed a margin of minus 529 zł because of small logs. As an M3 operator you read Polish Labour Code (KP) art. 120 § 1 and § 2. What is your financial liability?

A) I am liable for the full ammeter cost (4200 zł) + cover the negative margin (529 zł) = 4729 zł deducted from salary.
B) For the ammeter damage: yes, capped at three months' salary (about 11000 zł), in practice 1/3 of the damage = about 1400 zł, if unintentional; for the negative margin: no, that is sawmill economics, not damage caused by the operator's act.
C) For nothing: in Poland an employee is never financially liable.
D) For everything without a cap, including criminal liability under the Polish Criminal Code (KK) art. 266.

*Justify by citing KP art. 120 § 1 and § 2 and explaining why a negative margin is not damage attributable to the operator.*

### Block T2: Systems

**Question 4 (integrates T2 l1 + T4 l1, 4 pts)**

Ms. Ania (ITB classifier) after classifying batch ZLE-2026-06-020 (common beech, 89 boards) fills in KEP-001 jointly with you. Her measurements: 72 boards C24, 14 boards C16, 3 cut down for paneling. Yield 54.2%. Ms. Ania hands you the card to sign. You notice that the "dimensional tolerance" field is empty. What do you do?

A) I sign without filling it in, because Ms. Ania is an ITB-certified classifier.
B) I do not sign. The "I see X, I understand Y, is that correct?" pattern: "Ms. Ania, I see the tolerance field is empty, I understand that by default we apply +/-2 mm per PN-EN 336, is that correct?". After confirmation we fill it in in pencil + initial.
C) I sign, but I report to Marek after the shift.
D) I do not sign, I escalate immediately to Anna Kwiatkowska, the production manager.

*Justify by citing the M2 principle "the operator proposes an understanding" + the importance of documentation under ISO 9001 (15-min audit availability).*

**Question 5 (integrates T2 l6 + T4 l2, 4 pts)**

The shift report EGIDA-RZ-001 shows a margin of minus 529 zł on order ZLE-2026-05-077 (pine Ø 32 cm, scheme A). A week later the customer Dom-Bud Mragowo calls with a complaint: "boards from ZLE-077 are 174 mm instead of 175, exceeding +/-1 mm". You check PN-EN 336 (standard tolerance +/-2 mm, +/-1 mm only on special requirement). How do you connect these two facts?

A) Margin minus 529 zł + complaint = total loss on the order, the customer must be refunded, escalate to Anna Kwiatkowska.
B) The minus 529 zł margin results from small logs + Drew-Sus premium drying, unrelated to the complaint. The 174 mm complaint = non-material defect under Polish Civil Code (KC) art. 560 § 4 (PN-EN 336 tolerance +/-2 mm), refusal to acknowledge with the citation. Two independent facts.
C) The negative margin and the complaint are the same matter: you lost the customer through a parametric error.
D) I do not know, we have to ask Marek.

*Justify the distinction of the facts + cite KC art. 560 § 4 + PN-EN 336.*

**Question 6 (integrates T2 l6 + T1 l5 + T4 l6, 4 pts)**

You have a margin of minus 529 zł on ZLE-077. You are planning the UDT IIWJO W-M course (2500 zł) + SEP G1-E (500 zł). Should the loss on the order be taken into account when deciding about the course?

A) Yes, first recover the loss, then the course.
B) No. Order margin is sawmill economics, the ROI of the course concerns your salary. UDT+SEP = +500 zł/month extra salary, investment 3000 zł, ROI 5.7 months (3000/500 + amortization). Independent economic dimensions.
C) Yes, but only if the 15% supervisory bonus does not cover the loss.
D) It does not matter, because EGIDA pays for the course.

*Justify the distinction "order economics vs personal economics" + calculate the course ROI showing the logic.*

### Block T3: Processes

**Question 7 (integrates T3 l1-l4 + T4 l3, 4 pts)**

Order Stolarz Meblowy Szczytno: beech 5 m³ for upholstered furniture, required final moisture 8-10% (furniture grade, lower than construction 12-15%). Drew-Sus premium drying cycle 7 days (+50% cost surcharge for on-time delivery). FSC auditor Krzysztof Kowalski next week. How do you plan operations for this week?

A) Standard 10-day drying cycle, ignore the premium, the audit is secondary.
B) Premium 7-day cycle in accordance with the customer contract + KEP-001 filled in with Ms. Ania + preparation of FSC chain of custody documentation (quarterly FSC Connect register, package labels, intake register) + consultation with Mr. Henryk regarding monitoring of the 8-10% final moisture for beech.
C) Premium 7-day cycle, but FSC documentation only after the audit.
D) Refuse the Drew-Sus premium, too expensive.

*Justify the order of actions + reference the FSC-C112233 audit + PN-EN 13183-2 (MC measurement at 4 positions).*

**Question 8 (integrates T3 l7 + KP art. 100, 4 pts)**

Your trainee Adrian Kowal (M2) during the shift on 16 June 2026 makes 3 substantive errors (feed speed 85% instead of 62%, guides 4.2 mm instead of 3, log book in pencil) + 1 stylistic difference (cutting order left-right instead of your center-outward). You fill in KAO-001 after the shift. How do you assess and do you correct during the shift?

A) I correct all 4 (3 errors + 1 difference). Score 16/25 conditional 15 days.
B) I correct the 3 substantive errors during the shift (EGIDA regulation § 8 sec. 3 "I correct when wrong, not when different from mine"), I do not touch the stylistic difference. Score 21/25 full authorization 30 days.
C) I do not correct anything during the shift, I record everything in KAO-001 at the end. Score 14/25 refusal.
D) I correct only 1 safety error (speed), I ignore the rest as "details". Score 24/25.

*Justify by citing EGIDA regulation § 8 sec. 3 + KP art. 100 § 2 (employee duties) + the supervision-vs-control distinction.*

**Question 9 (integrates T3 l8 + T1 l4, 4 pts)**

After 30 seconds of cutting oak you feel **by touch** in the head handle a 10 Hz vibration, **by hearing** a rhythmic bearing knock every half second, **by sight** a wavy cut surface with 2 mm amplitude. The EGIDA table of 7 fault types classifies this as "lower wheel runout > 0.5 mm". Whose competence is this?

A) Operator: replace the band, that should help.
B) EGIDA maintenance: internal boundary, the mechanic will fix it with a wrench.
C) External service Wood-Mizer Polska (Mr. Krzysztof Wieliczko): hard external boundary, bearing replacement requires a hydraulic press + tamper-evident seal + service warranty. STOP immediately, KKS-001 entry, 4W call to Marek, Marek calls Wood-Mizer.
D) You repair it yourself, that is how Mr. Henryk taught you.

*Justify by citing l8 T3 (the 0.8 mm wheel runout case) + the principle of external boundary = seal + warranty.*

**Question 10 (integrates T3 l8 + T3 l7, 4 pts)**

You on P3 LT70 have a 10 Hz vibration (wheel runout). Adrian on P2 LT30 (your trainee that day) calls out: "Mykoła, I think I have waviness on a board". You are in the middle of escalating to Mr. Krzysztof at Wood-Mizer. Who has priority? How do you act?

A) Leave Adrian alone, focus on the urgent LT70 fault.
B) Adrian first: stop your LT70 (it is on STOP anyway because of the runout), walk over to Adrian 8 m away, visually check the waviness; if it is band dulling (operator-level), instruct Adrian to replace it under your observation, fill in an entry in his log book; then return to LT70 and finish the call to Mr. Krzysztof.
C) First call Marek (the foreman), let Marek decide.
D) STOP both machines, wait until Marek arrives, then sort it out.

*Justify the prioritization + the role of supervision + why Adrian's waviness is probably operator-level (band replacement) vs your fault being service-level.*

### Block T4: Organization

**Question 11 (integrates T4 l2 + Polish Supreme Court ruling I NSNc 152/20, 4 pts)**

The customer Jacek Wesolowski from Dom-Bud Mragowo calls: "Mykoła, the pine batch ZLE-2026-06-015 has 174 mm boards instead of the ordered 175 mm, I am withdrawing from the contract, refund the money". You fill in EGIDA-RPW-001 (complaint report). How do you classify it and what response do you formulate?

A) I accept the withdrawal, refund the money, KEP-001 update for the loss.
B) I classify it as a **non-material defect under KC art. 556¹ § 4 + art. 560 § 4** (a 1 mm tolerance fits within the standard PN-EN 336 +/-2 mm). Polish Supreme Court ruling I NSNc 152/20 of 17 Feb 2021: a defect must be material to justify withdrawal. Refusal of withdrawal, I propose a price reduction of 3-5% (KC art. 560 § 1). Email to the customer using the EGIDA template variant 1, electronic signature, 12-month retention.
C) I escalate to Anna Kwiatkowska, I say nothing myself.
D) I refuse curtly: "this is tolerance, goodbye".

*Justify with full citation of KC art. 556¹ § 4 and art. 560 § 1 and § 4 + the Supreme Court ruling + PN-EN 336.*

**Question 12 (integrates T4 l3 + T4 l1 + T3 l1-l4, 4 pts)**

NEPCon auditor Krzysztof Kowalski arrives for the FSC-C112233 audit. He asks you: "Mr. Mykoła, please show me the chain of custody for order ZLE-2026-06-020 (beech MebelPro)". What do you show?

A) Only KDP-001 (parametric decisions).
B) The full chain: (1) intake document for the package with the FSC label, (2) KDP-001, (3) KEP-001 filled in with Ms. Ania, (4) KS-001 for the entire shift, (5) Drew-Sus premium drying report, (6) final batch label, (7) quarterly FSC Connect register, (8) sales invoice to the customer. 15-min availability (ISO 9001).
C) I will call Marek, he has the documents.
D) I will tell the auditor: "please ask specific, not general, questions".

*Justify the scope of the FSC chain of custody + connect it with KEP-001 and KS-001 showing that the documentation is one system, not separate pieces of paper.*

**Question 13 (integrates T4 l5 + T4 l6 + Polish Technical Inspection Act 2000, 4 pts)**

You hand over the shift to Bohdan (M3 trainee, Ukrainian) by filling in EGIDA-PZ-001. In the field "Planned absences in the coming week" you write: "15-19 Sep 2026, UDT IIWJO W-M + SEP G1-E course at ZDZ Olsztyn, requesting training leave under KP art. 103¹ § 1". Bohdan asks: "What does that get you financially?". Your response?

A) Nothing, only a diploma.
B) Investment 3000 zł (UDT 2500 + SEP 500). Extra salary 500 zł/month (350 UDT + 150 SEP per the EGIDA pay regulations). ROI 5.7 months. Training leave under KP art. 103¹ = paid, you do not lose pay for the week of the course. Polish Technical Inspection Act 2000 art. 12-13 defines UDT entitlements, Polish Energy Law 1997 art. 54 defines SEP entitlements.
C) I earn more, but I do not know how much.
D) Salary minus 2500, because the course is out of my pocket.

*Justify by calculating the ROI + cite KP art. 103¹ + Polish Technical Inspection Act art. 12-13 + Polish Energy Law art. 54.*

### Meta-arc M1 -> M2 -> M3

**Question 14 (integrates T4 l7 M3 + all of M1 + all of M2, 4 pts)**

It is Friday 26 June 2026 and you are with Marek in the office. Marek asks: "Mykoła, three images of your journey, which would you choose for a summary?". Which answer demonstrates **understanding of the meta-principle of the body -> batch -> shift arc**?

A) November 2025 helper -> March 2026 operator -> June 2026 foreman.
B) M1 = my body and my colleagues' bodies within 5 m (STOP gestures, IARC dust, S3 boots) -> M2 = a 1-3 m³ batch under my tool (band parameters, moisture, class A/B/C/D) -> M3 = an 8-hour shift with 15 people (4 communication channels, Adrian's KAO-001, customer complaint, batch margin 5-15 thousand zł per day). Responsibility grows concentrically, not linearly.
C) M1 cheap -> M2 more expensive -> M3 most expensive course.
D) Wasyl -> Damian -> on my own, without a mentor.

*Justify by developing meta-principle 1 from l7 M3 + give one specific example from each module.*

Which situation from your 9-month journey **best illustrates** meta-principle 3, "from receiving instructions to issuing instructions", in action?

A) Wasyl saying to you "pass the log" in November 2025.
B) You saying to Adrian "stop the saw, replace the band, drop the feed speed to 62%" in June 2026, and you sign KAO-001. This is a formal operator instruction (oral + written) with legal liability under KP art. 120 § 1 and § 2 + KP art. 100 § 1. You moved from being given instructions to giving them.
C) Damian showing you how to connect the guides in March 2026.
