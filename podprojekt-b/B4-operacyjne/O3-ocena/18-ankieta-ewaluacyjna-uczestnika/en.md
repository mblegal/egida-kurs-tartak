# TRAINEE COURSE EVALUATION SURVEY

**Practical course "Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill)**: a vocational course for foreigners legally residing in the territory of the Republic of Poland, organised free of charge by Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA.

---

## Introductory note

This survey is intended for the **anonymous evaluation of the Course** by the Trainee after its completion. Your responses help the Foundation improve the Course for subsequent groups. The survey is **anonymous**: you do not provide your first name, last name or participant card number. Completion takes about 15 minutes. After completing it, drop the survey into the box in the training room.

## Note on the project

The Course "Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill) was created as a result of the potential developed under the project "Budowa fundamentów wsparcia cudzoziemców na rynku pracy w województwie opolskim" ("Building the foundations of support for foreigners on the labour market in the Opolskie Voivodeship"), implemented by Stowarzyszenie SMART (lead partner) in cooperation with Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (partner) in the years 2026-2027 from funds of the European Social Fund Plus. The Course itself is not financed from the project's funds and does not constitute a project activity; it is an independent statutory undertaking of the Foundation, conducted free of charge for the benefit of foreigners.
