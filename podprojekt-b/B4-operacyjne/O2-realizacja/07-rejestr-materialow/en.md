# REGISTER OF MATERIALS ISSUED TO THE COURSE PARTICIPANT

**Individual copy of the Course Participant** taking part in the practical Course "Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill), organised free of charge by Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation) for the benefit of foreign nationals legally residing on the territory of the Republic of Poland.

---

## Preliminary information

This register documents the transfer of teaching and auxiliary materials to the Course Participant during the Course. Each entry in the register is confirmed by the Course Participant's signature at the moment the material is received.

Materials are distinguished into **materials transferred permanently** (which remain with the Course Participant after the Course ends) and **materials lent (for return)** (which the Course Participant is obliged to return after a specified period or after the Course ends). Each entry indicates the type of transfer.

The register is drawn up in two counterparts: one remains in the Course files kept by the Foundation, the other is given to the Course Participant. Both counterparts are identical in content.

## Note on the project

The Course "Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill) was created as a result of the potential developed within the project "Budowa fundamentów wsparcia cudzoziemców na rynku pracy w województwie opolskim" (Building the foundations of support for foreign nationals on the labour market in the Opolskie Voivodeship), implemented by Stowarzyszenie SMART (the lead partner) in cooperation with Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (the partner) in the years 2026-2027 with funding from the European Social Fund Plus. The Course itself is not financed from the project funds and does not constitute a project activity; it is an independent statutory undertaking of the Foundation, conducted free of charge for the benefit of foreign nationals.

[^5]: Technical condition of the returned material: a brief designation, e.g., "without remarks / no issues", "minor signs of use", "damaged (describe)", "missing elements (specify)", "illegible", "other description".
