# ABSENCE EXCUSE

**Practical Course "Work at a sawmill"**: a vocational course for foreigners legally residing in the territory of the Republic of Poland, organised free of charge by Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation).

---

## Introductory information

This document serves to **justify the absence** of the Trainee from Course classes. The Trainee fills in the form no later than within **7 days from the day of the absence** and submits it to the Course coordinator together with supporting documents (e.g. medical certificate, official letter, document confirming a family situation). The coordinator decides whether to recognise the absence as excused and records the decision in the Trainee's **consolidated attendance record**.

The attendance threshold required to pass the Course is **80%** of classes in a cycle (in accordance with the Rules of the Course). Excused absences may be counted towards attendance to a limited extent specified by the coordinator.

## Note on the project

The Course "Work at a sawmill" was created as a result of the potential developed under the project „Budowa fundamentów wsparcia cudzoziemców na rynku pracy w województwie opolskim" ("Building the foundations of support for foreigners on the labour market in the Opolskie Voivodeship"), implemented by Stowarzyszenie SMART (the leader) in cooperation with Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (the partner) in the years 2026-2027 with funding from the European Social Fund Plus. The Course itself is not financed from project funds and does not constitute a project activity; it is an independent statutory undertaking of the Foundation, conducted free of charge for the benefit of foreigners.
