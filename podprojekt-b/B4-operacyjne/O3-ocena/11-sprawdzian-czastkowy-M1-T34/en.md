# MODULE 1 PARTIAL CHECK - BLOCK T3+T4 (WORK PROCESSES + ORGANIZATION)

**Practical course "Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill)**: a vocational course for foreigners legally residing in the territory of the Republic of Poland, organised free of charge by Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation).

---

This document contains **the partial check of Module 1** covering the material from block **T3 (Work processes)** and **T4 (Organization)**. The check is a tool of mid-semester diagnostics of the Trainee's progress, carried out after the third week of the Module (as a readiness check before the module test). It consists of **15 multiple-choice questions** A/B/C/D, each worth **2 points**, totalling **30 points**, with a duration of **30 minutes** and a pass threshold of **70% = 21 points**. The check alone does not determine completion of the Module, but provides the Trainee and the instructor with a picture of the areas requiring review before the module test (lesson l8 of week 4).

## Instructions for the Trainee

- Read each question carefully, **twice** before marking the answer.
- Mark **one** answer out of four (A, B, C, D).
- If you change your mind, **cross out clearly** the previous answer and mark the new one.
- **Do not leave empty fields** - an empty field counts as a wrong answer (0 pts).
- The check is performed **independently**, without consulting other Trainees and without using lesson notes.
- Each question is worth **2 points**. Total **30 points**. Pass threshold **21 points (70%)**.
- Duration of the check: **30 minutes**.

---

## Questions

**T3 Work processes (questions 16-23)**

**16. The maximum mass that under Regulation 2017 a MAN may lift manually at one time:**
A) 15 kg
B) 20 kg
C) 30 kg
D) 50 kg

**17. The maximum mass that under Regulation 2017 a WOMAN may lift manually at one time:**
A) 8 kg
B) 12 kg
C) 20 kg
D) 30 kg

**18. On the log yard the **most frequent** fatal event is:**
A) Lightning strike
B) Heart attack from exhaustion
C) Crushing by a rolling log / collapsing stack
D) Slipping on ice

**19. „I help but I do not operate" means:**
A) The helper never touches a tool
B) The helper does not operate machines requiring authorizations, but supports the operator (feeding, receiving, cleaning)
C) The helper works only 4 hours a day
D) The helper works without a contract

**20. When feeding material to the saw operator, you stand:**
A) Directly in front of the machine to see the blade
B) On the safe side, behind the zone line, out of kickback range
C) Exactly where the operator points with a gesture, regardless of the zone
D) In the cut line, to control the accuracy

**21. The most important rule when working with hand tools in sawing and shaping:**
A) Grip with both hands tightly and work fast
B) Hand on the far side (not in the tool's line), tool sharp, secure grip
C) Work only with anti-slip gloves
D) Seated work, so you do not get tired

**22. Oak dust from the extractor, not properly cleaned, poses:**
A) Only an aesthetic issue (dirty station)
B) Explosive + IARC 1 carcinogenic
C) Only an allergic risk
D) None, dust is safe

**23. Log defect marking before cutting (red colour):**
A) Good log, cut first
B) Defective log, emergency / special cut / rejection
C) Wet log
D) Export log

**T4 Organization (questions 24-30)**

**24. „The 15+30 ritual" means:**
A) 15 min work + 30 min break
B) 15 min morning briefing + 30 min workstation prep (6-point list) before starting
C) 15 breaks × 30 min each
D) Shift name 15:00-30:30

**25. In what order do you perform the 6-point workstation-prep list?**
A) PPE → cleanliness → walkways → lighting → tools → emergency stop
B) Emergency stop → tools → lighting → walkways → cleanliness → PPE
C) Randomly, as you remember
D) Only PPE and emergency stop; the rest is bureaucracy

**26. The STOP gesture in sawmill communication is:**
A) Both hands on the hips
B) Hand raised above the head, palm open, fingers straight, facing the person
C) Finger pointing to the side
D) Nod of the head

**27. How many Polish sawmill words should you understand passively (by ear) after the first month?**
A) 5
B) 15
C) 30
D) 100

**28. Which 4 documents are at your workstation every day?**
A) Work card, leave, sick note, bonus
B) Workstation card, attendance log, minor-incident log, tool-use authorization
C) Contract, annex, certificate, diploma
D) Report, invoice, order, delivery

**29. „5W1H" in a report is:**
A) 5 questions + 1 hypothesis
B) what, when, where, who, why (cause hypothesis), how
C) 5 lessons + 1 summary
D) 5 OHS rules + 1 reservation

**30. The Heinrich statistic 1:30:300:3000 means:**
A) 1 lesson : 30 min : 300 words : 3000 zlotys
B) 1 serious / fatal accident : 30 minor : 300 near-misses : 3000 unsafe behaviours
C) 1 helper operates 30 machines with 300 cycles on 3000 logs
D) An arbitrary number with no meaning

## Scoring and evaluation

- Each correct answer: **2 points**
- Wrong or unmarked answer: **0 points**
- Total points: **15 × 2 = 30 points**
- Pass threshold: **21 points (70%)**

After the check, the instructor returns the graded sheets to the Trainee and discusses the areas in which the Trainee obtained less than 50% of points within a single block (T1, T2, T3 or T4). The Trainee receives a recommendation as to which lessons to revisit before approaching the module test (l8 of week 4).
