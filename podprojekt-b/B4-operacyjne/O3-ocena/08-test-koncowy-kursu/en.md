# FINAL COURSE TEST

**Practical course "Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill)**: a vocational course for foreigners legally residing in the territory of the Republic of Poland, organised free of charge by Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation).

---

This document contains **the final test of the entire Course**. The test covers the content of all three Modules (M1 Helper, M2 Operator under supervision, M3 Independent operator) in the proportion 20% / 30% / 50% (in line with the competence hierarchy) and is a condition for admission to the practical exam and the issuance of the Course completion certificate. The test consists of three parts: multiple-choice questions (60 pts), decision scenarios (30 pts) and practical tasks (18 pts), totalling **108 points** and a duration of **120 minutes**, with a pass threshold of **70% = 76 points**.

## Introduction

This is the **final lesson of M3 and of the entire EGIDA sawmill course**. Over the previous 95 lessons you have learned: in M1 the hazards, material, processes and helper organisation (November 2025), in M2 the bandsaw, sorting, drying and the team of an operator under supervision (March 2026), in M3 the responsibility, systems, processes and organisation of an independent operator (May-June 2026). In l7 you carried out a 9-month retrospective (8 rules + 3 meta-rules + 5 competencies + 3 cases + voices of 6 people). Today **you verify whether you can apply this in new situations**.

The M3 final test covers **all three modules in a 20/30/50 proportion** (20% M1, 30% M2, 50% M3) and consists of **3 parts**, totalling **108 points**, duration **120 minutes**, passing threshold **70% = 76 points**:

1. **Multiple-choice questions** (30 questions x 2 pts = **60 pts**, **60 min**). Coverage: M1 (6 questions) + M2 (9 questions) + M3 (15 questions). Multiple choice, 4 answers, one correct. No consultations, independent work, on paper.
2. **Decision scenarios** (3 scenarios x 10 pts = **30 pts**, **30 min**). One scenario from each module (M1 OHS + M2 bandsaw/material + M3 coordination/complaint). You describe **what you do and why**, scoring criteria: safety (3 pts) + sequence of actions (3 pts) + reference to a rule (2 pts) + realism (2 pts).
3. **Practical tasks** (6 tasks x 3 pts = **18 pts**, **30 min**). Mixed tasks: filling EGIDA forms (KDP-001 + KAO-001), citing legal articles (KC + KP + statutes), calculations (yield, margin, career ROI), fault recognition via 3 senses, escalation structure, legal citation of defective tolerance.

**Whoever passes (76+ pts)** receives the **EGIDA Sawmill Course Completion Certificate – Module 3 Independent Operator** (10-year retention in personnel files), is admitted to the **practical exam** (independent 8-hour shift on P3 LT70 under external observation by the EGIDA commission + foreman + internal auditor, scheduled +2 weeks after the theoretical test), and to **solo export orders** (first batch of FSC oak to the Netherlands Bouwhout van Doorn, 12 m³, priority A).

**Whoever does not pass** repeats selected M3 lessons (Marek's decision after analysing gaps from the test), takes the make-up test in **3 weeks**, and in the meantime works as an **extended M2** (independent under M3 supervision on LT30, but without LT70 and without supervisory tasks).

This lesson contains the **student test** (questions without answers) and the **answer key + scoring criteria + practical exam scenario** (in a separate Phase 4 artefact for the instructor). The student receives only the „Test" section + a short mental instruction from l7.

## Objectives

After this lesson:

- **You have passed the M3 test** with at least 70% (76/108 pts).
- You know **in which areas you have gaps** (if you did not pass – which lessons to return to; if you did – where to deepen through practice).
- You understand that **passing the test is not the end of development**. Passing = admission to the practical exam and solo orders. Full M3 maturity grows over 6-12 months of post-course practice.
- You understand the **career structure after the course**: 3 specialisation paths (UDT+SEP, BTM drying internship, UPP studies), with financial and time dimensions, letting you plan the next 2-5 years.
- You are prepared for the **practical exam** (independent shift) in +14 days, and you know what to expect.

## Content

### Student instructions

**Part 1 – multiple-choice questions (60 min, 30 questions x 2 pts = 60 pts)**:
- Read each question **twice** before marking.
- Mark **one** answer (A, B, C, D).
- If you do not know, **do not leave it blank** – mark the most likely answer. Blank = 0 pts automatically.
- If you correct your answer, **cross out the old one clearly** and mark the new one.
- Manage your time: 60 minutes / 30 questions = 2 minutes per question on average.

**Part 2 – decision scenarios (30 min, 3 scenarios x 10 pts = 30 pts)**:
- The instructor hands out a sheet with three scenarios.
- You answer in writing: **what you do and why**, minimum 4 sentences, maximum 10.
- Scoring criteria: (a) **immediate safety** (3 pts) + (b) **correct sequence of actions** (3 pts) + (c) **reference to a rule / course lesson** (2 pts) + (d) **realism and specificity** (2 pts).
- Use the **4W** scheme: What I did / Where / When / What's next.

**Part 3 – practical tasks (30 min, 6 tasks x 3 pts = 18 pts)**:
- Mixed tasks: filling forms (blank sheets KDP-001, KAO-001), calculations (yield, margin, ROI), citing legal articles, sensory recognition (verbal description from material), 5-rung escalation structure.
- Each task 3 pts: 3/3 (fully correct), 2/3 (minor error), 1/3 (correct direction with substantial gaps), 0/3 (incorrect or blank).

**Integrity rule**: the test is **individual**. Copying, cheat sheets, phones, consulting a colleague: **0 pts for that part** + disciplinary conversation with the production manager.

### Part 2: 3 decision scenarios (30 pts, 30 min)

**Scenario 1 – M1 OHS (10 pts, about 10 minutes)**

Wednesday, August 12, 2026, 11:40. You are an M3 operator on P3 LT70, cutting pine. Suddenly you hear a shout from the log yard – 20 m from the hall. You run over: Wasyl Melnyk (stationary M1, 41 yrs, your first mentor from November 2025, since June 2026 forklift assistant) is lying on the ground next to a stack of logs, his left leg bent unnaturally below the knee, complaining of severe pain. There was no machine accident. The stack of logs was undermined by weekend rain; one log (200 kg) slid onto Wasyl as he was removing the top layer. Wasyl is conscious, blood from a forehead cut, pale face, shallow breathing. Krzysztof Zagrodny (forklift operator) is 30 seconds behind you. Marek is in the admin office 120 m from the yard. The emergency number 112 is written on the plaque by the entrance gate. The first-aid kit with a Thomas splint is in the foreman's office, the AED defibrillator is at the main entrance.

**What do you do first?** Describe in **4-8 sentences** with justification for each step. Criteria: Wasyl's immediate safety (3 pts), sequence of actions (3 pts), reference to M1 OHS lessons (e.g. first aid l8 M1, Art. 162 of the Polish Penal Code (KK), 112 script of 7 pieces of information) (2 pts), realism (2 pts).

**Scenario 2 – M2 material and bandsaw (10 pts, about 10 minutes)**

Thursday, September 3, 2026, 9:25. M3 operator for 10 weeks. ZLE-2026-09-045, common beech Fagus sylvatica, 6 logs 4.0 m average Ø 38 cm moisture 35% class A/B, boards 28×180×3600 mm C30 premium for oak parquet for Stolarz Meblowy Szczytno (yield requirement >42%, class higher than C24). KDP-001 filled 30 min earlier: pattern A cant sawing, stellite blade 7/8-A13 (tension 2400 PSI, speed 22 st/min, guides 3/4 mm). You cut log 1, log 2 – 2 class A boards without issue. You start log 3. After 2 cuts you see on the third board **an internal radial crack 18×6 mm with a rusty patina** (a defect disqualifying C30, probably disqualifying C24, possibly even an internal defect requiring assessment by the grader). At the same time you notice the ammeter at 18 A (yellow, warning), although parameters have not changed. You hesitate: continue to the end of log 3 and see the scale of the defect, or stop immediately.

**What do you do?** 4-8 sentences. Criteria: batch and machine safety (3 pts), sequence of actions (3 pts), reference to M2 lessons (e.g. l3 T3 M2 ammeter, l4 T2 M2 wood defects, T/M/O/P log) (2 pts), realism (2 pts).

**Scenario 3 – M3 shift coordination + complaint + supervision (10 pts, about 10 minutes)**

Friday, November 14, 2026, 13:15 (5.5 months after the M3 test). Marek is on a business trip in Warsaw, Damian is ill at home, Pan Henryk is in Mikołajki. You are **the only M3 operator** in the hall. In progress: (a) ZLE-2026-11-078 pedunculate oak 8 logs for C24 structural beams for Bouwhout van Doorn Holandia (FSC 100%, priority A, shipping deadline 17.11); (b) Olha Prokopenko (M2 since 01.07.2026, 4.5 months of practice) under your supervision on P2 LT30 with ZLE-079 birch for MebelPro strips. 13:15 the phone rings – Pan Jacek Wesołowski (Dom-Bud Mrągowo, client): „Mr Mykoła, the pine C24 batch bought in October has boards with a 45 cm longitudinal crack, three pieces out of 80, it looks like an internal defect, can we return them?". At the same time, 13:18, Olha comes to you agitated: „Mr Mykoła, the blade on LT30 broke, I had splinters in my eyes but the goggles saved me, what now?". 13:20 an SMS from Anton Bondarenko (yard foreman): „Mykoła, Wasyl reports the oak log stack from Nadleśnictwo Strzałowo looks unstable after yesterday's rain, I'm waiting for your decision". Krzysztof Zagrodny forklift is available.

**What do you do in the first 10 minutes?** 6-10 sentences with sequence of actions and justification. Criteria: immediate safety of Olha + stack stability (3 pts), sequence of the 4 matters (complaint, Olha, Wasyl/stack, ZLE-078) (3 pts), reference to M3 rules (e.g. rule 5 complaint = cooperation KC, rule 6 supervising Olha, rule 7 blade diagnosis) (2 pts), realism + communication (SMS to Marek about your decisions, because the foreman is absent) (2 pts).

### Part 3: 6 practical tasks (18 pts, 30 min)

**Task 1 – Fill KDP-001 for order ZLE-099 (3 pts, 5 min)**

The instructor gives you a blank KDP-001 card + order description: „pedunculate oak 5 logs Ø 35-40 cm moisture 32% green, boards 25×150×3000 mm C24 for Dom-Bud Mrągowo, priority B, shipment in 7 days". Fill in all 5 decision parameters with justification in 1 sentence each: (a) cutting pattern, (b) blade type, (c) blade tension, (d) feed speed, (e) blade guides.

Criterion: 3/3 = all 5 parameters correct with justification; 2/3 = 4 parameters correct; 1/3 = 3 parameters correct or no justifications; 0/3 = ≤2 parameters correct.

**Task 2 – Fill KAO-001 for a new M2 (3 pts, 5 min)**

The instructor gives you a blank KAO-001 card + situation description: „Olha Prokopenko, M2 since 01.07.2026, first shift under your supervision on P3 LT70 with order ZLE-100 (pine C16 panelling 5 logs). You observed the shift 7:00-15:00. Results: OHS 5/5 (PPE always worn, twice asked for LOTO), parameters 3/5 (twice asked about tension, once wrong feed speed on log 3 ammeter 19 A but reacted in 10 seconds), technique 4/5 (good reaction to log 4 flaking, too rapid head movement on log 1), sorting 4/5 (correct A/B class for all boards, minor uncertainty on one B/C board), documentation 3/5 (KDP-001 incomplete, no blade justification, log readable)". Fill in section 3 (evaluation) + section 4 (authorisation decision with deadline) + justification of 2-3 sentences.

Criterion: 3/3 = total 19/25 (full authorisation 30 days) with good pedagogical justification; 2/3 = correct total and decision, weak justification; 1/3 = error in the total but correct decision; 0/3 = error in the decision (e.g. full instead of conditional or vice versa).

**Task 3 – Cite 3 legal articles (3 pts, 3 min)**

Write from memory the **exact numbers** of 3 articles that are central for an M3 operator, in the context of their application:

(a) The article of the Polish Civil Code (KC) regulating **the seller's warranty for physical defects**.

(b) The article of the Polish Labour Code (KP) regulating **the employee's right to refrain from work in threatening conditions**.

(c) The act and articles regulating **UDT examinations for operator authorisations** (full name of the act with year + articles).

Criterion: 3/3 = all 3 citations correct (Art. 556 KC + Art. 210 § 1 KP + Technical Supervision Act of 2000, Art. 12-13); 2/3 = 2 correct; 1/3 = 1 correct; 0/3 = 0.

**Task 4 – Calculate margin and yield (3 pts, 5 min)**

Data: ZLE-123 Scots pine 2 logs Ø 35 cm length 4 m (volume 1.54 m³ raw material × 380 zł/m³ = 585 zł raw material). M3 operator work 5 h × 55 zł = 275 zł + 25 zł consumables. In-house EGIDA drying in the BH-50 kiln (cost 85 zł/m³ sawn timber) 7 days. Final machining planing + formatting (200 zł/m³ of product). Sawn timber after drying and machining: 0.62 m³ (0.55 C24 + 0.07 C16). Sales: C24 1700 zł/m³ + C16 1100 zł/m³.

Calculate: (a) final yield log → product (%, 1 decimal place); (b) sales revenue (zł); (c) total costs (zł); (d) margin (zł, with plus/minus).

Criterion: 3/3 = all 4 numbers correct (±5%); 2/3 = 3 correct; 1/3 = 2 correct; 0/3 = ≤1 correct.

**Task 5 – Sensory fault recognition (3 pts, 5 min)**

The instructor reads 3 descriptions of bandsaw symptoms. You answer **in one sentence**: which sense detected it (sight/hearing/touch), what is the probable fault, what is the reaction (operator alone / maintenance escalation / manufacturer service escalation).

(a) „After 30 seconds of cutting the third log you feel in your hand an 8 Hz vibration that was not present on logs 1 and 2. The motor sounds normal, the board looks straight".

(b) „On board 5 of stack 2 you see a 2-3 mm wave every 10 cm, a regular pattern. The blade counter shows 167 cuts since sharpening. You hear a normal motor sound".

(c) „You hear a new sound – the hum has stopped being even, it is starting to sound `grinding`, like dry branches breaking. It has lasted 5 seconds. The blade looks normal, handle vibration unchanged".

Criterion: 3/3 = all 3 descriptions correctly identified (touch → wheel runout → service, sight → blade for replacement → operator alone, hearing → bearings → maintenance or service depending on machine); 2/3 = 2 correct; 1/3 = 1 correct; 0/3 = 0.

**Task 6 – Escalation structure and career ROI (3 pts, 4 min)**

(a) List the **5 escalation rungs at EGIDA** from operator to director (1 pt).

(b) Calculate ROI in months for the investment: SEP G1-E course (cost 450 zł, salary supplement 150 zł/month net). ROI = cost / supplement (1 pt).

(c) List **3 career paths after the M3 course** with exact amounts (e.g. „UDT IIWJO W-M, cost 2500 zł, supplement 350 zł/month, ZDZ Olsztyn 15-19.09.2026") (1 pt).

Criterion: 3/3 = all 3 parts correct; 2/3 = 2 correct; 1/3 = 1 correct; 0/3 = 0.
