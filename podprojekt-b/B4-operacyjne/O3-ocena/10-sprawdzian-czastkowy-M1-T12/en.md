# MODULE 1 PARTIAL CHECK - BLOCK T1+T2 (OHS + WOOD MATERIAL)

**Practical course "Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill)**: a vocational course for foreigners legally residing in the territory of the Republic of Poland, organised free of charge by Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation).

---

This document contains **the partial check of Module 1** covering the material from block **T1 (Occupational health and safety)** and **T2 (Wood material)**. The check is a tool of mid-semester diagnostics of the Trainee's progress, carried out after the second week of the Module (before entering the procedural blocks T3 and organisational T4). It consists of **15 multiple-choice questions** A/B/C/D, each worth **2 points**, totalling **30 points**, with a duration of **30 minutes** and a pass threshold of **70% = 21 points**. The check alone does not determine completion of the Module, but provides the Trainee and the instructor with a picture of the areas requiring review before the module test (lesson l8 of week 4).

## Instructions for the Trainee

- Read each question carefully, **twice** before marking the answer.
- Mark **one** answer out of four (A, B, C, D).
- If you change your mind, **cross out clearly** the previous answer and mark the new one.
- **Do not leave empty fields** - an empty field counts as a wrong answer (0 pts).
- The check is performed **independently**, without consulting other Trainees and without using lesson notes.
- Each question is worth **2 points**. Total **30 points**. Pass threshold **21 points (70%)**.
- Duration of the check: **30 minutes**.

---

## Questions

**T1 OHS (questions 1-8)**

**1. How many dB does a band saw typically produce in a sawmill?**
A) 50-70 dB (loud conversation)
B) 75-85 dB (busy street)
C) 95-110 dB (aircraft taking off)
D) 130-150 dB (explosion)

**2. Which wood-dust species is classified by IARC as a class 1 carcinogen?**
A) Pine and spruce
B) Oak and beech
C) Poplar and birch
D) All wood dusts equally

**3. The safety-boot class required in a sawmill is:**
A) S1 (steel toe, no oil resistance)
B) S2 (toe + water resistance)
C) S3 (toe + anti-slip + sole puncture resistance)
D) S5 (for underwater work)

**4. When are gloves MANDATORY and when FORBIDDEN in a sawmill?**
A) Mandatory always, no exceptions
B) Mandatory for manual handling and tools; forbidden at rotating machines (saw, planer)
C) Forbidden at all, they reduce touch feel
D) At the foreman's discretion

**5. What type of dust mask is used with oak and beech?**
A) FFP1 (simple disposable)
B) FFP2 (industrial dusts)
C) FFP3 (carcinogenic dusts)
D) Pharmacy cotton mask

**6. Noise threshold above which hearing protection is mandatory under Polish Regulation of the Minister of Economy, Labour and Social Policy 2007:**
A) 65 dB
B) 75 dB
C) 85 dB
D) 100 dB

**7. Which behaviour in a sawmill fire is CORRECT?**
A) Use water regardless of source (dust, oil, electricity)
B) Use an ABC or CO₂ extinguisher per the pictogram next to it, evacuate through marked routes
C) Open windows to let in air
D) Stay in the hall and wait for the fire brigade

**8. „Red workstation" under Labour Code art. 210 means:**
A) A workstation painted red
B) A new workstation not yet set up
C) A workstation where you refuse work because of a health / life hazard
D) A workstation for the experienced

**T2 Wood material (questions 9-15)**

**9. „Kłoda" in sawmill vocabulary is:**
A) A finished board for sale
B) An unprocessed tree trunk brought from the forest
C) Cutting waste
D) A sticker between stack layers

**10. The difference between dry and wet timber:**
A) None, they are synonyms
B) Dry has moisture <18%, wet >30%; dry for construction / furniture, wet to be dried
C) Dry is winter-cut, wet summer-cut
D) Dry weighs more than wet

**11. Which species is NOT carcinogenic (for dust)?**
A) Oak
B) Beech
C) Pine
D) All equally carcinogenic

**12. The wood defect „sinizna" (blue stain) is:**
A) A crack along the grain
B) Fungal discolouration, does not reduce strength but lowers aesthetic value
C) Mechanical saw damage
D) Shape deformation

**13. Typical chamber-drying temperatures for oak:**
A) 15-25°C
B) 30-60°C (slow, to prevent cracking)
C) 60-100°C (fast)
D) 100-150°C (extremely fast)

**14. „Stickers" in a timber stack serve to:**
A) Compress the stack more tightly
B) Ensure airflow between layers during drying
C) Mark the batch
D) Decoration

**15. Band saw vs circular saw: which has the bigger kickback?**
A) Band, because the blade is longer
B) Circular, because the rpm is high and the material bounces back
C) Equal
D) Neither, kickback is only for routers

## Scoring and evaluation

- Each correct answer: **2 points**
- Wrong or unmarked answer: **0 points**
- Total points: **15 × 2 = 30 points**
- Pass threshold: **21 points (70%)**

After the check, the instructor returns the graded sheets to the Trainee and discusses the areas in which the Trainee obtained less than 50% of points within a single block (T1, T2, T3 or T4). The Trainee receives a recommendation as to which lessons to revisit before approaching the module test (l8 of week 4).
