# REGULATIONS OF THE PRACTICAL COURSE "WORK IN A SAWMILL" ("PRACA W TARTAKU")

**Version 1.0, in force as of 1 January 2026.**

---

## I. Mission and objectives of the Course

1. These Regulations set out the rules for the organisation of and participation in the practical course entitled **"Praca w tartaku" ("Work in a Sawmill")** (hereinafter: the "**Course**"), conducted by **Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA** (EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation; hereinafter: the "**Foundation**") in cooperation with a business partner (hereinafter: the "**Entrepreneur**").

2. The Course is implemented in the spirit of **lifelong learning** and on the basis of the conviction that the proper integration of foreigners into Polish society can only take place together with equipping them with real competences to perform work in accordance with the law and with the social and economic needs of the Republic of Poland.

3. The mission of the Course is:

   a) to prepare foreigners legally residing in the territory of the Republic of Poland to perform work in Polish sawmills and in related areas of wood processing for the needs of construction, joinery and the furniture industry;

   b) to equip Course Participants with verifiable, documented professional competences, confirmed by module certificates and by the certificate of completion of the entire Course;

   c) to support migration that is in compliance with the law and with the socio-economic needs of Poland, and to counteract the phenomena of illegal migration and the burdening of the Polish economy with work performed outside the legal framework.

4. The Course is not a project activity nor is it financed from the funds of the project "Budowa fundamentów pod lokalną politykę migracyjną i integracyjną" ("Building the foundations of a local migration and integration policy"); it has, however, been made possible thanks to the strengthening of the Foundation's institutional capacity within that project (information as set out in the Closing Mention of these Regulations).

## II. General provisions

1. These Regulations constitute a **standard contract template** (wzorzec umowny) within the meaning of Article 384 § 1 of the Polish Civil Code and are delivered to the Course Participant before the conclusion of the agreement on the conduct of the practical course (hereinafter: the "**Course Agreement**"). The provisions of these Regulations bind the Course Participant from the moment of signing the Course Agreement.

2. The terms used in these Regulations have the following meanings:

   a) **Course** – the practical course entitled "Praca w tartaku" ("Work in a Sawmill"), conducted in three modules M1, M2 and M3 with a total duration of 316 teaching hours over 12 weeks;

   b) **Module** – a separate didactic stage of the Course (M1, M2 or M3), ending with a certificate of completion of the Module on the terms set out in section VIII;

   c) **Foundation** – Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA, with its registered seat in Opole, ul. Ozimska 14-16/314A, 45-057 Opole, KRS (National Court Register, in Polish: Krajowy Rejestr Sądowy): 0000957190, NIP (Tax Identification Number): 7543348716, REGON (Statistical Number): 521360040, as the organiser of the Course;

   d) **Entrepreneur** – an entrepreneur being a party to a separate agreement on the co-organisation of the Course concluded with the Foundation, providing services in a sawmill establishment and making workstations available for the purposes of the principal practical part; the Entrepreneur may be the owner of the sawmill establishment or may conduct activity therein on the basis of an outsourcing agreement with the owner;

   e) **sawmill establishment** – the physical location at which the Course Participant performs the tasks of the principal practical part; the address of the establishment may be different from the registered seat of the Entrepreneur;

   f) **Course Participant** – a foreigner legally residing in the territory of the Republic of Poland, qualified for the Course and being a party to the Course Agreement;

   g) **teaching hour** – a unit of class time lasting 45 minutes;

   h) **theoretical part** – lectures, discussions, debates and written tests, conducted as a rule in the training room of the Foundation in Opole;

   i) **workshop part** – simple practical exercises, demonstrations and instructions, conducted as a rule in the workshop facility of the Foundation;

   j) **principal practical part** – the performance of actual sawmill tasks under the supervision of the Entrepreneur, conducted always at the Entrepreneur's sawmill establishment;

   k) **module test** – an assessment test at the end of a given Module, which does not condition the issuance of the certificate of completion of the Module (the sole condition for a module certificate is attendance pursuant to section VII);

   l) **final examination** – a test at the end of the entire Course, conditioning the issuance of the certificate of completion of the Course, with a passing threshold of 70%, that is 76 points out of 108 points available;

   m) **examiner** – a person designated by the Foundation, responsible for conducting the final examination and the module tests, and taking the decisions referred to in section VII paragraph 5.

3. These Regulations do not govern the rules of recruitment to the Course; those rules are set out in a separate document made available to candidates before the submission of an application.

## III. Organisational structure of the Course

1. The Course is organised in **three modules** of increasing competence complexity:

| Module | Title | Duration | Volume | Professional profile |
|--------|-------|----------|--------|----------------------|
| **M1** | Auxiliary worker in a sawmill | 4 weeks | 72 teaching hours | helper performing tasks under supervision |
| **M2** | Operator of sawmill equipment under supervision | 4 weeks | 112 teaching hours | operator performing tasks on an entrusted machine under supervision |
| **M3** | Independent operator | 4 weeks | 132 teaching hours | operator independently making technological decisions and supervising persons from M1 and M2 |

A total of **316 teaching hours over 12 weeks** (approximately 3 calendar months), unless the parties have agreed otherwise in the Course Agreement or in a separate scheduling annex.

2. Each Module comprises three didactic components, the location of which depends on organisational conditions:

| Component | Character | Primary location | Permissible alternative location |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|----------------------------------|
| Theoretical part | lectures, discussions, debates, written tests | training room of the Foundation in Opole (ul. Ozimska 14-16) | workshop facility of the Foundation or a room at the Entrepreneur's establishment, provided they ensure adequate organisational conditions |
| Workshop part | simple practical exercises, demonstrations, instructions | workshop facility of the Foundation in Opole | the Entrepreneur's establishment, provided it ensures adequate organisational conditions |
| Principal practical part | performance of actual sawmill tasks under supervision | **always at the Entrepreneur's sawmill establishment** | no permissible alternative location |

3. The principal practical part in a real sawmill is the essence of the Course and cannot be replaced by simulation or by workshop exercises.

4. Classes are conducted in the working languages of the Course: Polish, English, Spanish and Ukrainian, to the extent resulting from the communication needs of the group. Didactic materials are made available in all four language versions.

## IV. Conditions of participation in the Course

1. A participant in the Course may be exclusively a person who jointly satisfies the following conditions:

   a) is a foreigner within the meaning of the relevant provisions on foreigners and resides in the territory of the Republic of Poland legally, on the basis of a valid visa, residence permit or other document authorising residence;

   b) has reached 18 years of age;

   c) has submitted a complete recruitment application and has been qualified for the Course in the manner set out in a separate recruitment document;

   d) has signed the Course Agreement between the Course Participant, the Foundation and the Entrepreneur;

   e) has signed a declaration of having read and accepted these Regulations (section XII paragraph 1);

   f) has signed the GDPR information clause and consents made available by the Foundation as a separate document;

   g) has submitted a declaration of having become acquainted with the scope of tasks, hazards and health requirements for work in a sawmill and of the absence of medical contraindications to participation in the principal practical part; the Foundation may require the presentation of a medical certificate within a scope analogous to preliminary examinations for work in the wood industry.

2. **The Course is free of charge for the Course Participant.** The Course Participant does not bear any fees for participation in any of the Modules or for the issuance of certificates.

3. The Course Participant does not receive remuneration on account of participation in the Course, in particular on account of performing tasks in the principal practical part, unless a separate agreement is concluded (e.g. an employment contract, a contract of mandate or a graduate internship agreement).

4. The number of places in a given edition of the Course is limited by the organisational conditions of the training room, the workshop facility and the Entrepreneur's sawmill establishment. Qualification is determined by the recruitment result and the place limit, not by the order of applications.

## V. Rights and obligations of the Course Participant

### 1. The Course Participant has the right to:

a) participate in all Course classes in accordance with the schedule;

b) receive free of charge a complete set of didactic materials in the chosen language version (PL, EN, ES or UK), and upon request also in the remaining versions;

c) make use of the Foundation's language support in the event of difficulties in understanding the content of classes in Polish, in particular through oral interpretation of key occupational health and safety messages and discussions;

d) address the Foundation's coordinator with questions, doubts and notifications concerning the conduct of the Course;

e) have absences excused on the terms set out in section VII paragraph 2;

f) submit a complaint or an appeal on the terms set out in section X;

g) protection of personal data in accordance with the GDPR information clause (section XI);

h) obtain certificates of completion of the Modules and the certificate of completion of the Course on the terms set out in section VIII;

i) be exempted from participation in individual classes for justified reasons (e.g. on religious or health grounds), provided this does not result in exceeding the absence threshold set out in section VII paragraph 1.

### 2. The Course Participant undertakes to:

a) participate systematically and punctually in Course classes in accordance with the schedule;

b) behave in a cultured manner, not disturbing the conduct of classes and not endangering the safety of other participants, the teaching staff, the Entrepreneur's employees and third parties;

c) strictly observe occupational health and safety regulations and fire safety regulations as well as workstation instructions in force in the Foundation's workshop facility and at the Entrepreneur's sawmill establishment;

d) use personal protective equipment (PPE) provided by the Entrepreneur in accordance with the recommendations of the supervising person;

e) observe the work regulations in force at the Entrepreneur's premises to the extent applicable to the Course Participant, in particular as regards working time, breaks, the prohibition of consuming alcohol and using narcotic substances;

f) maintain the confidentiality of the Entrepreneur's commercial and technological information of which the Course Participant becomes aware during the Course, and not to communicate such information to third parties during the Course and after its conclusion;

g) use the Foundation's didactic materials exclusively for the purposes of the Course Participant's own education within the Course and not to share them with third parties without the Foundation's consent;

h) report immediately to the Foundation's coordinator and to the supervising person at the Entrepreneur any situation endangering health or life, any occupational health and safety incident, and any organisational, language or communication difficulty hindering the implementation of the Course;

i) inform the Foundation immediately of the loss of validity of the residence document, identity document or of any other change in legal status affecting the ability to participate in the Course;

j) excuse absences in a timely manner pursuant to section VII paragraph 2.

### 3. The Course Participant is not permitted to:

a) undertake sawmill tasks outside the didactic scope of the Module currently being implemented;

b) operate machines and equipment to which the Course Participant has not been admitted by the instructor or by the supervising person at the Entrepreneur;

c) be present at the sawmill establishment outside the hours of Course classes without the express consent of the Entrepreneur;

d) make the Foundation's didactic materials available to third parties without the Foundation's consent.

## VI. Rights and obligations of the Foundation and the Entrepreneur

1. **The Foundation undertakes to:**

   a) provide teaching staff (trainers, coordinator, classifiers) with qualifications adequate to the scope of the Course;

   b) make available the training room and the workshop facility in Opole for the purposes of the theoretical and workshop parts;

   c) develop and make available didactic materials in four language versions (PL, EN, ES, UK);

   d) keep documentation of the conduct of the Course (attendance lists, assessment cards, protocols of module tests and of the final examination);

   e) conduct the module tests and the final examination, and issue the certificates referred to in section VIII;

   f) provide the Course Participant with accident insurance for the duration of the Course, unless the Entrepreneur covers the Course Participant with its own insurance;

   g) provide language support in the working languages of the Course to the extent resulting from the communication needs of the group;

   h) examine complaints and appeals from Course Participants on the terms set out in section X.

2. **The Entrepreneur undertakes to:**

   a) make workstations available to the Course Participant at the sawmill establishment indicated in the Course Agreement, to the extent necessary for the implementation of the principal practical part;

   b) provide the Course Participant with the supervision of a person possessing competences at least at the level of M3 of these Regulations or equivalent (e.g. a foreman, a master, an experienced operator with documented seniority);

   c) provide the Course Participant with personal protective equipment (PPE) consistent with the occupational risk assessment for a given workstation;

   d) conduct workstation occupational health and safety instruction before admitting the Course Participant to perform tasks;

   e) observe the provisions of the Labour Code and occupational health and safety regulations in respect of the Course Participant on terms analogous to those applicable to its own employees, as regards working time, breaks and safety;

   f) not employ the Course Participant in overtime hours during the Course and not issue to the Course Participant instructions exceeding the didactic scope of the Module currently being implemented.

3. **The Foundation and the Entrepreneur are entitled to:**

   a) remove the Course Participant from the list of Course Participants in the manner set out in section IX;

   b) change the Course schedule for organisational, technical or force majeure reasons, with the obligation to notify Course Participants immediately by electronic means;

   c) change the location of the theoretical part or the workshop part within the limits set out in section III paragraph 2, subject to the inviolability of the location of the principal practical part (the sawmill establishment);

   d) introduce amendments to these Regulations on the terms set out in section XII paragraph 3.

## VII. Attendance, assessment and passing

1. The **attendance threshold** for the issuance of a certificate of completion of a given Module is **75% of the teaching hours delivered** for the given Module, that is:

   a) Module M1: a minimum of **54 teaching hours** out of 72 hours;

   b) Module M2: a minimum of **84 teaching hours** out of 112 hours;

   c) Module M3: a minimum of **99 teaching hours** out of 132 hours.

   Attendance is calculated separately for each Module. Attendance not meeting the threshold results in the refusal to issue the certificate of completion of the given Module and, consequently, in the impossibility of obtaining the certificate of completion of the Course (section VIII paragraph 4).

2. **Excusing absences**:

   a) excused absences (medical certificate, written confirmation of a fortuitous event, summons of a public authority, official matter documented by a summons) **are counted towards the attendance percentage** only if the Course Participant presents to the Foundation's coordinator an appropriate document within a period not exceeding 7 days from the day of the absence;

   b) unexcused absences **are not counted towards the attendance percentage** and reduce it in proportion to the number of hours missed;

   c) the 75% threshold is calculated from the full pool of teaching hours of a given Module; excused absences may be counted towards the threshold only up to 25% of the Module hours, regardless of their number.

3. The **module test** (at the end of each of Modules M1, M2, M3) serves to verify the competences acquired within the Module and is formative in character. **The result of the module test does not condition the issuance of the certificate of completion of the Module**; the module certificate is issued solely on the basis of meeting the attendance threshold set out in paragraph 1.

4. The **final examination** (at the end of the entire Course) covers the material of all three Modules (M1, M2, M3) and consists of a theoretical part (written test) and a practical part (workshop task). The total points pool is **108 points**. **The passing threshold for the final examination is 70%, that is a minimum of 76 points out of 108 points available**.

5. **Make-up examination of the final examination**:

   a) a Course Participant who has not passed the final examination (a result below 76 points) may be granted the possibility of taking **a one-time make-up examination**;

   b) the decision on admission to the make-up examination is **discretionary** in character and is taken individually by the examiner, on the basis of a written, motivated application of the Course Participant submitted within 14 days of the announcement of the result;

   c) the examiner's decision requires approval by the Foundation's coordinator within 7 days of its issuance;

   d) grounds justifying the granting of a make-up examination are in particular: illness of the Course Participant on the day of the final examination confirmed by a medical certificate, a fortuitous event preventing preparation or attendance, a qualified failure of public transport, an official matter documented by a summons or other significant, objectively verifiable circumstances;

   e) mere poor form on the day of the test, insufficient preparation or dissatisfaction with the result **do not constitute a ground** justifying a make-up examination;

   f) the date of the make-up examination is set by the Foundation, no earlier than 14 days and no later than 60 days from the announcement of the result of the first attempt;

   g) the make-up examination is conducted at the cost of the Foundation and has an identical scope and passing threshold as the first attempt (70%, 76 out of 108 points);

   h) failing the make-up examination or failing to take it within the designated period results in the lack of grounds for the issuance of the certificate of completion of the Course; the Course Participant retains the right to certificates of completion of individual Modules previously issued.

6. **Refusal to grant a make-up examination** requires written justification and is subject to challenge in the complaint procedure set out in section X.

## VIII. Rules for issuing certificates

1. The Foundation issues, in the course of the Course, four types of certificates:

   a) **certificate of attendance of the Course** (upon the Course Participant's request, issued at any moment during the Course, for the purposes of presentation to the competent authorities);

   b) **certificate of completion of Module M1** (upon meeting the M1 attendance threshold);

   c) **certificate of completion of Module M2** (upon meeting the M2 attendance threshold);

   d) **certificate of completion of Module M3** (upon meeting the M3 attendance threshold);

   e) **certificate of completion of the Course** (upon meeting the attendance thresholds of all three Modules and passing the final examination with a result of at least 76 points out of 108).

2. **Module certificates** (letters b, c, d) are issued within 14 days of the conclusion of the given Module. The issuance of a module certificate is not conditional on the result of the module test or on the completion of subsequent Modules.

3. **The certificate of completion of the Course** (letter e) is issued within 30 days of the announcement of the result of the passed final examination (or of the make-up examination, if the first attempt was not passed).

4. A Course Participant who has met the attendance thresholds of all three Modules but has not passed the final examination (and has not made use of the make-up examination or has likewise not passed it) receives only three module certificates (letters b, c, d). Module certificates confirm the achievement of professional competences corresponding to the individual profiles (M1, M2, M3) but do not replace the certificate of completion of the entire Course.

5. All certificates are issued in four language versions (PL, EN, ES, UK) and are delivered to the Course Participant free of charge in one copy in the language of the Course Participant's choice; the remaining versions are delivered upon the Course Participant's request.

6. Templates of the certificates constitute separate documents; the Foundation ensures their public availability for inspection before the commencement of the Course.

## IX. Removal from the list of Course Participants

1. The Foundation, in agreement with the Entrepreneur, may **remove the Course Participant from the list of Course Participants** in the event of:

   a) **a gross breach of occupational health and safety regulations or fire safety regulations**, in particular one creating an immediate threat to the health or life of the Course Participant, other participants, the teaching staff, the Entrepreneur's employees or third parties;

   b) **being present on the premises of the sawmill establishment, the training room or the workshop facility in a state after the consumption of alcohol** or under the influence of narcotic substances;

   c) **the use of physical or psychological violence** against other participants, the teaching staff, the Entrepreneur's employees or third parties, including mobbing, threats, intimidation;

   d) **theft or wilful damage to property** of the Foundation, the Entrepreneur or third parties;

   e) **exceeding the permissible absence threshold**, that is a situation in which it is mathematically no longer possible to achieve 75% attendance in the Module currently being implemented, regardless of the remaining classes;

   f) **failure to satisfy the conditions of participation** set out in section IV (e.g. loss of validity of a residence document undeservedly concealed from the Foundation);

   g) **a material breach of the Course Participant's obligations** set out in section V paragraphs 2 and 3, if despite a written demand to cease the breaches, the Course Participant has not ceased them within the designated period.

2. Removal from the list of Course Participants takes place on the basis of a written decision of the Foundation's coordinator, served on the Course Participant in person or to the correspondence address indicated in the Course Agreement. The decision contains a justification and an instruction on the complaint procedure (section X).

3. **A decision in matters under paragraph 1 letters a, b, c, d** may have immediate effect. A decision in matters under paragraph 1 letters e, f, g enters into force within 7 days of its service, unless the Course Participant submits a complaint.

4. **Effects of removal**: the Course Participant loses the right to further participation in the Course. Module certificates issued before the removal retain their validity. No further certificates, including the certificate of completion of the Course, are issued.

5. Removal from the list of Course Participants does not exclude the Course Participant's liability for damage caused to the Foundation, the Entrepreneur or third parties, nor criminal liability if the circumstances of the removal constitute a prohibited act.

## X. Complaints and appeals

1. The Course Participant has the right to submit a **complaint** in matters concerning the conduct of the Course, the assessment of passing, the decision to remove from the list of Course Participants, the refusal to issue a certificate, the refusal to grant a make-up examination of the final examination, and other actions of the Foundation or the Entrepreneur affecting the Course Participant's situation as a participant in the Course.

2. The complaint should be submitted in writing (in person, by registered letter or electronically to the e-mail address of the Foundation indicated on the website fundacjaegida.eu) within **14 days** of the day on which the Course Participant became aware of the event being the subject of the complaint.

3. The complaint shall contain:

   a) the Course Participant's name and surname and contact details;

   b) an indication of the event being the subject of the complaint together with its date;

   c) a justification of the complaint;

   d) the Course Participant's request (e.g. suspension of the effects of a decision, reconsideration, admission to a make-up examination, issuance of a certificate).

4. The Foundation examines the complaint within **30 days** of its receipt and notifies the Course Participant of the resolution in writing.

5. The submission of a complaint in matters under section IX paragraph 3 second sentence **suspends the effects of the decision** to remove until the day of the resolution of the complaint, unless the Foundation's coordinator decides otherwise on important organisational or safety grounds.

6. Detailed rules of complaints and the appeals procedure are set out in the Foundation's **Complaints and Appeals Policy**, constituting a separate document.

## XI. Protection of personal data

1. The controller of the Course Participant's personal data within the scope of the organisation and implementation of the Course is **Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA** with its registered seat in Opole, ul. Ozimska 14-16/314A, 45-057 Opole.

2. The Entrepreneur processes the Course Participant's personal data within the scope and for the purpose necessary for the implementation of the principal practical part, in particular for the purposes of occupational health and safety instruction, attendance records and supervision of work safety, as a separate controller in this scope.

3. Detailed information on the processing of personal data is set out in the **GDPR information clause** made available to the Course Participant before the signing of the Course Agreement, as a separate document.

4. In the scope not regulated by the GDPR clause and these Regulations, the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (GDPR) and of the Act of 10 May 2018 on the Protection of Personal Data shall apply.

## XII. Final provisions

1. **Course Participant's declaration**: the Course Participant signs at the end of these Regulations a declaration of the following content:

   > "I declare that I have read the content of the Regulations of the practical course "Praca w tartaku" ("Work in a Sawmill"), I understand its provisions and I accept them. I have received one copy of the Regulations in the language of my choice."

   The signing of the declaration (section XIII) is a condition for admitting the Course Participant to participate in the Course.

2. These Regulations enter into force on **1 January 2026** and remain in force until amendments are introduced on the terms set out in paragraph 3 or until they are repealed by the Foundation.

3. **Amendments to the Regulations**:

   a) the Foundation may introduce amendments to these Regulations in the event of a change in the provisions of law, a change in the organisational conditions of the Course or for other important reasons;

   b) of a planned amendment the Foundation notifies the Course Participants currently participating in the Course at least 14 days in advance, by electronic means to the e-mail address indicated in the Course Agreement;

   c) an amendment to the Regulations may not worsen the situation of the Course Participant as regards passing thresholds, required attendance and the right to certificates, if the Course Participant has already commenced participation in the Course before the amendment enters into force;

   d) a Course Participant who does not accept an amendment to the Regulations may withdraw from the Course Agreement within 14 days of being notified of the amendment; in such case the Foundation issues certificates for the Modules already completed, provided the attendance thresholds have been met.

4. In matters not regulated by these Regulations, the provisions of the Course Agreement, the GDPR information clause and the provisions of the Civil Code, of the acts indicated in the header of these Regulations, as well as occupational health and safety regulations and labour law provisions shall apply.

5. **The official language** of these Regulations is the Polish language. Translations of the Regulations into English, Spanish and Ukrainian are made available as aids for Course Participants. In the event of discrepancies between the language versions, the Polish version shall prevail.

6. **The governing law** for these Regulations and for the relationships arising from them is the law of the Republic of Poland. Disputes arising from the Regulations or the Course Agreement shall be resolved by the parties amicably and, in the absence of agreement, before the common court competent for the registered seat of the Foundation.
