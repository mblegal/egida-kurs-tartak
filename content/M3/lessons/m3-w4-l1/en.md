### 12:00-12:30, 5-point moisture measurement

**Batch moisture measurement method** (EGIDA guideline § 4 of quality regulation reg.wew.05/2024, based on PN-EN 13183-2 "Moisture content of a piece of sawn timber - Estimation by electrical resistance method"):

1. Measurement at **five points** of the batch: upper corners (1, 2), lower corners (3, 4), center of stack (5).
2. Measurement point: **15 cm from the end-face of the board** (because the end-face dries faster, false reading).
3. Pin depth: **1/3 of board thickness** (for 20 mm = 6-7 mm).
4. Board for measurement: **random from the layer**, not the best and not the worst visually.
5. Three readings per point with 90° rotation, result is the **arithmetic mean**.

## Theory: batch documentation, KEP-001, WZ, division of responsibility

### What is KEP-001

**Batch Record Card (KEP-001)** is an internal EGIDA document, introduced in March 2026 for M3 operators, replacing the earlier practice of "**the grader measures alone**". KEP-001 is the **source of measurement data** handed to the grader before grading, which:
1. **Shortens grading time** by 30-60% (in the case of Rustam's birch from 3 h to 90 min).
2. **Separates responsibility**: the operator is responsible for **measurement**, the grader for **grade assessment**.
3. **Creates archival evidence** in case of complaint (retention 5 years in the ZLE binder).
4. **Elevates the M3 operator to the status of data supplier**, not just raw-material processor.

KEP-001 contains 4 mandatory sections:

| Section | Content | Who fills in |
|---|---|---|
| **Header** | Batch number, species, FSC origin, felling date, seasoning, sawing date, operator, assistant, number of boards, volume, destination, client, expected grade | operator |
| **Moisture measurement** | PN-EN 13183-2 method, instrument + calibration, 5 measurement points (corners + center), 3 readings per point, batch mean, spread, conclusion | operator |
| **List of boards with defects** | Board number, thickness × width × length, defect description, preliminary grade | operator |
| **Grading** (blank for operator) | Grading method, verifier's instruments, final grades, notes, grader's signature + stamp | **grader** completes |

The operator **signs only their sections**, the grader **signs their section**. The operator's signature denotes responsibility for **fidelity of measurements**, the grader's signature denotes responsibility for **grade assessment**.

### Moisture measurement method (PN-EN 13183-2)

The standard **PN-EN 13183-2 "Moisture content of a piece of sawn timber - Estimation by electrical resistance method"** specifies the rules for moisture measurement of lumber with a resistance moisture meter (like Tanel ET-6 used in EGIDA). Key requirements:

1. **Minimum 5 measurement points** for a batch up to 100 boards, 10 points for 100-500, above 500, 2% random sample.
2. **Measurement position**: 15 cm from the board's end-face (the end-face dries faster), middle of the width, pin depth = 1/3 of board thickness.
3. **3 readings per point** with 90° rotation: averaging eliminates local grain inhomogeneity.
4. **Instrument calibration**: every 12 months (certificate retained in EGIDA's metrology archive).
5. **Measurement documentation**: each point recorded with value, **batch mean + spread** calculated automatically.

**Spread < 1 percentage point** = uniform batch, ready for a single drying cycle.
**Spread 1-3 points** = moderately uniform batch, drying with program correction.
**Spread > 3 points** = non-uniform batch, **selection before drying** (separate drier batches).

Rustam today had 0.6 points of spread: **a very good result**.

### Visual defect classes PN-EN 1611-1

**PN-EN 1611-1 "Visual grading of softwood sawn timber"** (applied "as appropriate" to hardwoods in commercial contracts) defines 3 visual classes:

| Class | Admissible defects | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **A** (clean) | none or single sound knots < 10 mm, no cracks | visible joinery, tabletops, furniture |
| **B** (knot) | sound knots up to 40 mm, closed cracks < 30 cm length | building joinery, visible roof truss, floor boards |
| **C** (downgraded) | falling-out knots, open cracks, blue stain | packaging, non-visible construction, pallets |

Classes A and B are **commercial goods**, class C sold at a **reduced price**, **rejects** go to sawdust/fuel briquette.

**In the case of Rustam's birch:** 70 boards class A, 6 boards class B (knot), 2 boards class B (shorter dimension), 0 boards C, 0 rejects: **100% commercial batch**, which is a **very good result** (EGIDA standard is 90% commercial batch, 10% rejects acceptable).

### Operator / grader division of responsibility

| Scope | Operator M3 | ITB Grader | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Moisture measurement** | yes, 5 points PN-EN 13183-2 | spot-check verification | grader checks every 5th board SPC |
| **Dimensional measurement** | yes, every board caliper + tape | full verification of first 10 boards, then SPC | difference between measurements max 0.3 mm admissible |
| **Defect identification** | yes, visually with pencil marking | yes, full inspection with 10× loupe | grader **complements** operator |
| **Grade assessment PN-EN 1611-1** | preliminary grade A/B/C | yes, **final grade** | only grader decides |
| **Structural-strength grade** | none | yes, **only ITB grader** | softwood PN-EN 14081-1, hardwood PN-EN 13366 |
| **KEP-001 signature** | yes, operator sections | yes, grader section | double signature, two responsibilities |
| **WZ signature** | no, operator does NOT sign | yes, **grader + foreman** | WZ = external certification |
| **Civil liability to client** | indirect (to EGIDA) | yes, direct (grader's liability insurance 250,000 zł) | grader insured |
| **Professional liability insurance** | not required for M3 | yes, mandatory (condition of ITB license) | ITB rule since 2019 |

**Canonical rule** (Ms. Ania):
> The operator measures, the grader grades. The operator signs what they measured. The grader signs what they decided. Sign only what you answer for.

### WZ format (Goods-Issue Note)

**Goods-Issue Note** is the document for goods leaving the EGIDA warehouse to the client. It has 6 mandatory sections:

1. **Header**: EGIDA data (NIP, KRS, FSC-C112233), WZ number, date, order number, batch number.
2. **Client data**: full name, tax ID, address, framework agreement (if any), person authorized to receive.
3. **Description of goods**: species (Latin name in brackets), dimensions, quantity, volume, moisture, grade, defects, FSC origin.
4. **Grading**: grader, qualifications (ITB license number, validity date), method, instruments, notes.
5. **Signatures**: **grader** (green ITB stamp), **seller/foreman**, **recipient** (space for signature on receipt).
6. **Retention**: 5 years in EGIDA archive, client's copy, ZLE binder copy, CRM scan.

**The WZ goes out with the client and comes back with their signature**: proof of goods issuance. Legal basis for retention: § 12 sec. 3 of quality regulation reg.wew.05/2024 + generally art. 74 of the Accounting Act (5 years for warehouse documents).

### Pedagogy: documentation before grading

**The most frequent mistake of beginner M3 operators**: filling in KEP-001 **after the fact** (in the evening, "from memory", retrospectively).

**Why this is a mistake**:
1. **Moisture measurement is time-sensitive**: the moisture meter reads the current state, and moisture changes day to day (lumber loses 1-2% daily in the hall in warm conditions). An "evening" measurement is a **different state** than a "morning" measurement.
2. **Visual defects change**: boards lie on a stack, some hidden in the layers, "from memory" one forgets knots in the middle of the stack.
3. **Dimensions are not a question of memory**: 20.1 mm vs 20.0 mm is a 0.5% difference that no one remembers precisely after 6 hours.
4. **A retrospective KEP-001 is false in evidential force**: an auditor or a prosecutor in the case of a court complaint will ask: "when was it filled in". If the date of filling is **later** than the date of measurement, KEP-001 **loses credibility**.

**EGIDA rule**: KEP-001 is filled in **during the measurements** (date = time of measurement, not time of filling in). Wahan's work notebook + transfer to KEP-001 the same day = **compliant with the rule** (it is not "from memory", but "from notes of the same day").

### What Rustam learned today

Looking back at the 18.06 shift:

- **Documentation adds 1 hour to the day**, but **cuts off 90 minutes from the grader** = **net -30 minutes for the whole sawmill**.
- **Measuring 78 boards + moisture in 5 points = 75 minutes of work** (with a helper writing).
- **KEP-001 is filled in during measurements**, not retrospectively.
- **Operator signs KEP, not WZ**: the certification boundary.
- **3 different documents, 3 different responsibilities**: KEP (operator), grading inside KEP (grader), WZ (grader + foreman).
- **Documentation operator's reputation** is built through **a series of good documents**, not through one.
- **3% supplement for 6 months of error-free documentation** is in the regulation: **money for reliability**.
