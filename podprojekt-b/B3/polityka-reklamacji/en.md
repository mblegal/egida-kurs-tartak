# COMPLAINTS AND APPEALS POLICY

**Practical course "Praca w tartaku" ("Work in a Sawmill")**: a vocational course for foreigners legally residing in the territory of the Republic of Poland, organised by **Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA** (EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation), with its registered seat in Opole, ul. Ozimska 14-16/314A, 45-057 Opole, KRS (National Court Register, in Polish: Krajowy Rejestr Sądowy): 0000957190, NIP (Tax Identification Number): 7543348716, REGON (Statistical Number): 521360040, in cooperation with an Entrepreneur operating a sawmill establishment.

This Complaints and Appeals Policy (hereinafter: the "**Policy**") sets out the detailed procedure for submitting, examining and appealing complaints filed by Course Participants in connection with the implementation of the Course. The Policy is an implementing document to **section X of the Course Regulations** and to **section IX of the Agreement on the conduct of the practical Course**, and does not modify the scope of the rights vested in the Course Participant under those documents or under the provisions of generally applicable law, but rather sets out the detailed procedure for their exercise.

---

## I. General provisions

1. **Purpose of the Policy**. The purpose of this Policy is to ensure that the Course Participant has a transparent, accessible and free of charge procedure for challenging actions of the Foundation and of the Entrepreneur taken during the implementation of the Course, as well as an appeals procedure against the Foundation's resolutions. The Policy implements the principle of accountability of a public benefit organisation towards its beneficiaries, and in particular towards foreigners as a group specially protected under the Polish legal order.

2. **Free of charge nature of complaints**. The submission of a complaint and of an appeal is **free of charge**. The Course Participant does not bear, on account of a complaint, any fees, internal costs of the Foundation or handling costs.

3. **Language form**. A complaint may be submitted in one of the **four languages of the Course**: Polish (PL), English (EN), Spanish (ES) or Ukrainian (UK). The Foundation provides its reply in the same language in which the complaint was submitted.

4. **Relation to other documents**. In matters not regulated in this Policy, the provisions of the Course Regulations, the Course Agreement, the GDPR information clause and the provisions of the Civil Code, of the Act on Public Benefit and Volunteer Work, and of the Act on the Protection of Personal Data shall apply.

5. **Definitions**. The terms used in this Policy have the meaning given to them in section II of the Course Regulations (Definitions).

---

## II. Subject matter scope of complaints

A Course Participant may submit a complaint in particular on the following matters:

a) **the quality of the didactic classes** of the Course (substantive value, manner of conduct, language of the lecture, availability of didactic materials in the language chosen by the Course Participant),

b) **the actions of the Foundation's teaching staff** (trainers, the coordinator, the classifiers), including the manner of conducting classes, personal culture and the treatment of the Course Participant,

c) **decisions on the assessment** of module tests or of the final examination, including challenges to the correctness of the scoring or the consistency of the questions with the Course programme,

d) **the decision not to pass a Module** or not to pass the Course, including challenges to the calculation of attendance or to the qualification of absences as unexcused,

e) **the decision to remove the Course Participant from the list of Course Participants** (section IX of the Course Regulations),

f) **the refusal to grant a make-up examination of the final examination** (section VII paragraph 7 of the Course Regulations),

g) **the refusal to issue a certificate** (of completion of a Module, of completion of the Course, of attendance of the Course, of a graduate internship) or the issuance of a certificate containing an error,

h) **the actions of the Entrepreneur during the principal practical part**, in particular the manner of conducting occupational health and safety instruction, the quality and completeness of personal protective equipment, the personal culture of the supervising person, breaches of the principle of not issuing instructions exceeding the didactic scope of the Module (section V paragraph 2 letter f of the Course Agreement),

i) **work safety in the sawmill establishment** (incidents threatening health or life, unreported breaches of occupational health and safety),

j) **the processing of the personal data** of the Course Participant by the Foundation or by the Entrepreneur (subject to section III paragraph 2 of this Policy concerning the right to file a complaint with the President of the UODO),

k) **any other actions** of the Foundation or of the Entrepreneur affecting the situation of the Course Participant as a participant in the Course.

---

## III. Exclusions from the complaints procedure

1. **Inalienable supervisory powers of public authorities**. This Policy does not limit the right of the Course Participant to apply directly to:

   a) the **President of the Office for the Protection of Personal Data** (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO) in matters concerning the processing of personal data,

   b) the **National Labour Inspectorate** (Państwowa Inspekcja Pracy, PIP) in matters of occupational health and safety in the sawmill establishment,

   c) the **Public Prosecutor's Office and law enforcement authorities** in matters bearing the features of prohibited acts,

   d) the **common court** in civil proceedings,

   e) the **authority supervising foundations** (the minister competent for matters of social security) in matters concerning the statutory activity of the Foundation.

2. **Waiver of the complaints procedure**. Recourse to the complaints procedure is not a condition for the effective exercise of the rights referred to in paragraph 1, nor of the right to pursue claims before a common court.

3. **Lack of competence of the Foundation**. The complaints procedure does not cover matters in which the Foundation has no competence to issue a resolution (in particular matters concerning an employment relationship of the Course Participant with an entity other than the Foundation, or immigration matters of the Course Participant before authorities of the Republic of Poland).

## IV. Procedure for submitting a complaint

1. **Time limit**. A complaint shall be submitted within **14 days** of the day on which the Course Participant became aware of the event being its subject or of the day of the service on the Course Participant of the resolution to which the complaint relates. The time limit shall be deemed to have been observed if, before its expiry, the complaint was sent by registered letter, dispatched by electronic means or submitted in person against confirmation of receipt.

2. **Form**. A complaint shall be submitted in one of the following forms:

   a) **in writing**, by registered letter or in person against acknowledgement of receipt, to the address: Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA, ul. Ozimska 14-16/314A, 45-057 Opole, marked "Complaint" ("Reklamacja"),

   b) **electronically**, in an e-mail message to the address: **reklamacje@fundacjaegida.eu** (with a courtesy copy to the Course coordinator, if the Course Participant knows the coordinator's address),

   c) **in person**, during the working hours of the Foundation's office in Opole (the office hours are made available on the website https://fundacjaegida.eu).

3. **Content of the complaint**. A complaint should contain the following elements, the joint indication of which facilitates its examination:

   a) the name and surname of the Course Participant and contact details (correspondence address, e-mail address, telephone number),

   b) the number and date of the Course Agreement to which the complaint relates,

   c) an indication of the event being the subject of the complaint together with the date of the event and, as far as possible, the time and place,

   d) a justification of the complaint indicating the manner in which the action of the Foundation or of the Entrepreneur is inconsistent with the Course Agreement, the Course Regulations, the GDPR information clause, this Policy or the provisions of generally applicable law,

   e) **the request of the Course Participant** (for example: suspension of the effects of a decision, reconsideration, admission to a make-up examination, issuance of a certificate, rectification of data in a certificate, an apology, action against the supervising person, replacement of personal protective equipment),

   f) any evidence (copies of letters, correspondence, photographs, witness statements).

4. **Assistance in formulating a complaint**. A Course Participant who has difficulties in formulating a complaint in writing may apply to the Course coordinator or to the Foundation's office for assistance in drafting it. Such assistance is **free of charge** and does not affect the running of the time limit referred to in paragraph 1.

5. **Form of declaration of intent**. The absence of any of the elements listed in paragraph 3 does not result in the ineffectiveness of the complaint, provided that its content makes it possible to identify clearly the Course Participant submitting the complaint and the subject matter of the complaint. Where necessary, the Foundation requests the Course Participant to supplement the complaint within 7 days.

---

## V. First instance, examination of the complaint by the Foundation

1. **Examining body**. Complaints are examined by the **Course coordinator** indicated in the Course Agreement, and in the event of the coordinator's unavailability, the necessary actions are taken by a member of the Foundation's management board or a person authorised by the management board.

2. **Time limit for examination**. The Foundation examines the complaint within **30 days** of the day of its receipt. In particularly complex cases, this time limit may be extended by a further **30 days**, of which the Foundation informs the Course Participant before the expiry of the original time limit, indicating the reason for the extension.

3. **Suspension of the effects of a decision**. The submission of a complaint in matters concerning the removal of the Course Participant from the list of Course Participants (section II letter e of this Policy) **suspends the effects of the decision** until the day of its resolution, unless the Course coordinator decides otherwise on important organisational or safety grounds, documented in writing.

4. **Investigation procedure**. The Foundation undertakes investigative actions to the extent necessary to assess the merits of the complaint, in particular:

   a) hears the Course Participant, if the Course Participant so wishes,

   b) calls upon for explanations the persons concerned by the complaint (members of the teaching staff, supervising persons at the Entrepreneur),

   c) reviews the Course documentation (attendance lists, assessment cards, test protocols, correspondence),

   d) in matters concerning the Entrepreneur, addresses an inquiry to the Entrepreneur in the procedure provided for in the agreement on the co-organisation of the Course.

5. **Confidentiality**. The investigation procedure is conducted with respect for the privacy of the Course Participant and of the persons concerned by the complaint. Information about the complaint is made available solely to persons participating in the procedure and to the extent necessary for its resolution.

6. **Resolution**. The Foundation resolves the complaint in one of the following ways:

   a) **upholding** the complaint in whole or in part, with the simultaneous taking of the requested action (for example rectification of a certificate, admission to a make-up examination, re-marking of a test, action against the supervising person),

   b) **refusal to uphold** the complaint, with reasons,

   c) **referral of the matter to the competent body**, where the matter exceeds the competence of the Foundation (for example referral to the PIP in a matter of occupational health and safety, to the President of the UODO in a matter of personal data protection); of the referral the Foundation informs the Course Participant and indicates the competent body.

7. **Form of reply**. The Foundation provides the Course Participant with a reply in writing, to the correspondence address or, if the Course Participant has so indicated, to the e-mail address. The reply contains:

   a) a response to each of the requests of the Course Participant,

   b) reasons for the resolution,

   c) **instruction on the appeals procedure**, including the 14-day time limit for submitting an appeal,

   d) the signature of the Course coordinator or of a member of the Foundation's management board.

---

## VI. Second instance, appeal against the Foundation's resolution

1. **Right of appeal**. A Course Participant whose complaint has not been upheld in whole or has been upheld only in part has the right to submit an **appeal to the Foundation's management board** within **14 days** of the day of the service of the first-instance resolution.

2. **Form of appeal**. The appeal is submitted in one of the forms set out in section IV paragraph 2 of this Policy, marked "Appeal" ("Odwołanie") instead of "Complaint" ("Reklamacja"). The appeal should indicate the resolution against which it is brought and contain a justification.

3. **Examining body**. The appeal is examined by the **Foundation's management board** sitting in a multi-member composition, and in the event that the management board cannot be convened within the required time limit, by a member of the management board designated by its chairperson.

4. **Time limit for examination of the appeal**. The Foundation's management board examines the appeal within **30 days** of the day of its receipt, with the possibility of extension by a further 30 days on the terms set out in section V paragraph 2 of this Policy.

5. **Resolution of the appeal**. The Foundation's management board issues an appellate resolution in one of the following ways:

   a) **upholding the appeal** in whole or in part, with the simultaneous setting aside or amendment of the first-instance resolution,

   b) **upholding** the first-instance resolution,

   c) **referral of the matter for re-examination** to the Course coordinator with guidelines as to the procedure to be followed.

6. **Exhaustion of the internal procedure**. The resolution of the Foundation's management board **exhausts the internal complaints and appeals procedure** of the Foundation. Following its service, only the rights referred to in section VII of this Policy are vested in the Course Participant.

## VII. Out-of-court and judicial pursuit of claims

1. **Mediation**. The parties may, by mutual consent, undertake **mediation** conducted by a mediator of their choice, in particular a mediator from the list of permanent court mediators kept by the president of the regional court, or a mediator indicated by the Mediation Centre at the Lewiatan Confederation, or by another mediation centre. The choice of mediator and the coverage of the mediator's remuneration require an agreement between the parties.

2. **Pursuit of claims before a common court**. Exhaustion of the complaints procedure is not a condition for the effective exercise of the right to pursue claims before a common court. The Course Participant may bring an action against the Foundation or the Entrepreneur in civil proceedings. In accordance with section IX paragraph 4 of the Course Agreement, the court competent for disputes between the Foundation and the Course Participant is the **common court competent for the registered seat of the Foundation** (the District Court or the Regional Court in Opole, depending on subject-matter jurisdiction).

3. **Complaint to the supervisory authority in GDPR matters**. In matters concerning the processing of personal data, the Course Participant has the right to file a **complaint with the President of the Office for the Protection of Personal Data** (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, https://uodo.gov.pl), independently of the Foundation's internal complaints procedure. The submission of an internal complaint to the Foundation neither shortens nor suspends the time limit for filing a complaint with the President of the UODO.

4. **Right to information on the procedure for pursuing claims**. At the request of the Course Participant, the Foundation makes available, **free of charge**, general information on the procedure for pursuing claims before a common court, before authorities of public administration and before other bodies competent in matters of the Course. Such information does not constitute legal advice.

---

## VIII. Register of complaints and evaluation

1. **Register**. The Foundation maintains a **register of complaints** of Course Participants, in which it records the date of submission of the complaint, its subject matter (categorised in accordance with section II of this Policy), the date of resolution, the manner of resolution and information about any appeal and its resolution. The register is maintained in a manner preventing the identification of the Course Participant by unauthorised persons.

2. **Evaluation**. The Foundation, at least once a year, carries out an **evaluation** of incoming complaints in order to identify recurring irregularities and to introduce preventive measures. The conclusions of the evaluation are taken into account in the work on improving subsequent editions of the Course.

3. **Statutory reporting**. The number and categories of complaints in aggregated form, not allowing for the identification of Course Participants, may be taken into account in the Foundation's statutory reporting to the supervisory authority and in the Foundation's annual reports.

4. **Data in the register**. The personal data of Course Participants in the register of complaints are processed on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR (legitimate interest of the controller) and Article 6(1)(c) of the GDPR (legal obligation in the scope of statutory reporting), for the period and in the manner set out in the GDPR information clause.

---

## IX. Final provisions

1. **Entry into force**. This Policy enters into force on **1 January 2026** and remains in force until amendments are introduced on the terms set out in paragraph 2 or until it is repealed by the Foundation.

2. **Amendments to the Policy**. The Foundation may introduce amendments to this Policy in the event of a change in the provisions of law, a change in the organisational conditions of the Course or for other important reasons. Of an amendment the Foundation notifies Course Participants currently participating in the Course at least 14 days in advance, by electronic means to the e-mail address indicated in the Course Agreement. An amendment may not worsen the situation of the Course Participant as regards matters reported before its entry into force.

3. **Severability clause**. If any of the provisions of this Policy proves to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in force to the fullest extent permitted by law. In place of the invalid or unenforceable provision, the provision closest to the purpose which the parties intended to achieve through the replaced provision shall apply.

4. **Copies**. This Policy is made available to the Course Participant:

   a) as an annex to the Course Agreement in the language chosen by the Course Participant (PL/EN/ES/UK),

   b) on the Foundation's website **https://fundacjaegida.eu** in the section concerning the Course,

   c) at the request of the Course Participant, in printed form, at the Foundation's office in Opole, free of charge.
