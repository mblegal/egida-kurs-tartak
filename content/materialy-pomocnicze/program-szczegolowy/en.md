# Detailed course program "Work in a Sawmill"

This document contains a list of 96 sawmill course lessons with titles, brief learning-goal descriptions and five to eight key Polish terms for each lesson. The list is generated automatically from module specifications and lesson content by the script `scripts/generate_program_szczegolowy.py`. All data comes from completed lesson files M1/M2/M3 in phase 3 of Subproject A. Full lesson content is in the `content/M{1,2,3}/lessons/` directory. Key terms are kept in Polish because the course is taught in Polish and Polish vocabulary is a core learning outcome.

## Module 1 - Helper (support role)

### Week 1 - Safety and responsibility (`bezpieczenstwo`)

#### Lesson 1 (`m1-w1-l1`): Why a Sawmill Is Different

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand how a sawmill differs from other workplaces in terms of hazards. You know why this course starts with safety, not with machine operation.

**Key Polish terms**: tartak, pomocnik, BHP, zagrożenie, ŚOI, strefa niebezpieczna, pilarka, tarcica.

#### Lesson 2 (`m1-w1-l2`): Mechanical Hazards Around You

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You recognize moving machine parts and places where entanglement, impact, or kickback can happen. You know why a helper never steps into a machine's working zone.

**Key Polish terms**: zagrożenie mechaniczne, pochwycenie, odrzut, zmiażdżenie, strefa robocza, osłona stała, osłona ruchoma, wyłącznik awaryjny STOP.

#### Lesson 3 (`m1-w1-l3`): Wood Dust and Noise - Silent Hazards

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand that oak and beech dust is carcinogenic and that long-term noise permanently damages hearing. You know when wearing a mask and hearing protection is mandatory.

**Key Polish terms**: pył drzewny, kancerogen, NDS, maska przeciwpyłowa, wentylacja miejscowa, odciąg, hałas, decybel (dB).

#### Lesson 4 (`m1-w1-l4`): Fire Hazards in the Sawmill

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You recognize ignition sources (friction, sparks, electricity, cigarettes) and places where dust and chips accumulate. You know where the extinguishers and evacuation routes are.

**Key Polish terms**: trójkąt pożaru, źródło zapłonu, paliwo, wybuch pyłu, gaśnica proszkowa ABC, gaśnica CO₂ (śniegowa), alarm pożarowy, droga ewakuacyjna.

#### Lesson 5 (`m1-w1-l5`): Protective Clothing and PPE

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You choose safety-toe boots, a helmet, goggles, gloves, hearing protection, and a mask for the task. You know when gloves MUST NOT be used (rotating machines).

**Key Polish terms**: ŚOI, hełm ochronny, okulary ochronne, rękawice robocze, rękawice antyprzecięciowe, obuwie ochronne, podnosek stalowy, podeszwa antyprzebiciowa.

#### Lesson 6 (`m1-w1-l6`): Work Zones and Floor Markings

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You recognize floor lines, pictograms, and safety signs. You know where you may stand, where you may walk, and where entry is forbidden.

**Key Polish terms**: strefa produkcyjna, strefa komunikacyjna, pas komunikacyjny, strefa magazynowa, strefa sanitarna, piktogram BHP, znak zakazu, znak ostrzegawczy.

#### Lesson 7 (`m1-w1-l7`): First Aid After an Accident

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You know the response for a cut, finger amputation, electric shock, and being hit by kickback. You know where the first-aid kit is and how to call for help.

**Key Polish terms**: pierwsza pomoc, łańcuch przeżycia, RKO, AED, numer 112, opatrunek uciskowy, opaska uciskowa, pozycja boczna bezpieczna.

#### Lesson 8 (`m1-w1-l8`): Evacuation, Alarm, and Emergency STOP

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You recognize alarm signals and know the evacuation route. You know how to use the emergency stop button and whom to report an incident to.

**Key Polish terms**: ewakuacja, alarm pożarowy, sygnał alarmowy, punkt zbiorki, droga ewakuacyjna, wyłącznik awaryjny STOP, STOP lokalny, STOP ogólny.

### Week 2 - Material and tools (`material`)

#### Lesson 1 (`m1-w2-l1`): Wood as a Raw Material - Log Structure

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You recognize sapwood, heartwood, annual rings, and bark in a cross-section. You understand why the same log has different zones of different quality.

**Key Polish terms**: kłoda, przekrój, kora, biel, twardziel, rdzeń, słoje roczne, korowarka.

#### Lesson 2 (`m1-w2-l2`): Local Species - Pine, Spruce, Oak, Beech, Birch

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You can visually tell apart five species used in a Polish sawmill. You know which are coniferous and which are deciduous, and what that means for the work.

**Key Polish terms**: sosna, świerk, dąb, buk, brzoza, drewno iglaste, drewno liściaste, żywica.

#### Lesson 3 (`m1-w2-l3`): From Log Length to Lumber - Yard Terminology

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You can name in Polish: 'dłużyca' (long log), 'kłoda' (log), 'tarcica' (lumber), 'deska' (board), 'bal' (plank), 'krawędziak' (square timber), 'łata' (batten), 'oblina' (slab). You know the order in which material moves through the sawmill.

**Key Polish terms**: dłużyca, kłoda, tarcica, deska, bal, krawędziak, łata, oblina.

#### Lesson 4 (`m1-w2-l4`): Wood Defects Visible to the Naked Eye

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You recognize knots (sound and dead), splits, sweep, rot, and blue stain. You know that defects affect a board's quality grade.

**Key Polish terms**: wada drewna, sęk zdrowy, sęk martwy, pęknięcie, krzywizna, wypaczenie, zgnilizna, zasinienie.

#### Lesson 5 (`m1-w2-l5`): Hand Tools in the Sawmill

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You can name and recognize handsaws, chisels, a plane, hammer, rule, square, and power drill. You know how to pass them safely.

**Key Polish terms**: piła ręczna, dłuto, strug, młotek, przymiar (calówka), kątownik, wkrętarka, rękojeść.

#### Lesson 6 (`m1-w2-l6`): Machines "From the Outside" - Frame, Band, and Circular Saws

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You recognize the three main types of sawmill saws. You don't operate them - you know their names, appearance, and danger zone.

**Key Polish terms**: pilarka ramowa (trak ramowy), pilarka taśmowa, pilarka tarczowa, strefa niebezpieczna, linia cięcia, odrzut materiału, wyłącznik awaryjny STOP, ostrze (taśma, tarcza).

#### Lesson 7 (`m1-w2-l7`): Support and Transport Machinery

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You recognize the forklift, roller and belt conveyors, grapple, and debarker. You know where the pedestrian safety zone ends.

**Key Polish terms**: wózek widłowy, przenośnik rolkowy, przenośnik taśmowy, chwytak (manipulator), korowarka, suwnica, martwa strefa, kontakt wzrokowy.

#### Lesson 8 (`m1-w2-l8`): Dry vs. Wet Wood - What It Means for You

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand the difference between fresh (wet) and kiln-dried lumber - weight, handling behavior, risk of warping. You know why stacking wet and dry lumber differs.

**Key Polish terms**: drewno świeże (mokre), drewno suszone, wilgotność drewna, suszarnia komorowa, przekładka (sticker), stos tarcicy, wentylacja stosu, wykrzywienie (paczenie).

### Week 3 - Production processes (`procesy`)

#### Lesson 1 (`m1-w3-l1`): The Helper's Role in the Production Line

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand where your work fits between the operator and the yard worker. You know what "I help, but I don't operate" means and where the line is.

#### Lesson 2 (`m1-w3-l2`): Manual Wood Handling - Rules and Limits

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You know the lifting limits (for men, women, one-time, repeated) and the bent-knee lifting position. You know when you must always call a second person.

#### Lesson 3 (`m1-w3-l3`): Unloading and Storing Logs in the Yard

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand the rules for storing long logs and logs - chocking, dunnage, spacing. You know where you NEVER stand during unloading.

#### Lesson 4 (`m1-w3-l4`): Stacking Lumber with Stickers

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You stack boards on stickers to ensure airflow and stability. You understand why a badly built stack splits and can collapse.

#### Lesson 5 (`m1-w3-l5`): Feeding and Receiving Material with the Operator

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You know how to stand behind the working-zone line, how to hand over a board, and how to respond to the operator's gesture 'stop' / 'dawaj' (give it).

#### Lesson 6 (`m1-w3-l6`): Hand Sawing, Planing, and Measuring

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You make a basic cut with a handsaw, take shavings with a hand plane, and take a measurement with a rule. You know how to hold the material and the tool safely.

#### Lesson 7 (`m1-w3-l7`): Workstation Housekeeping - Sawdust, Chips, Waste

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand why sawdust underfoot is a slip and fire risk. You know what to clean with, when to clean, and where to take the waste.

#### Lesson 8 (`m1-w3-l8`): Material Marking and Identification

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You read chalk and marker markings on logs and lumber (species, size, date, operator). You know why they must not be wiped off.

### Week 4 - Work organization (`organizacja`)

#### Lesson 1 (`m1-w4-l1`): The Rhythm of a Sawmill Day

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You know a typical day: briefing, workstation check, 5-minute safety talk, work, break, cleanup, report. You know who gives instructions and whom you report to.

#### Lesson 2 (`m1-w4-l2`): Preparing Your Own Workstation

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You check: PPE, cleanliness, walkways, lighting, tools, emergency stop within reach. You know what to do when something is missing.

#### Lesson 3 (`m1-w4-l3`): Crew Communication in Noise

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You use the agreed gestures 'stop', 'podaj' (pass), 'czekaj' (wait), 'uważaj' (watch out), 'cofnij' (back up). You use short Polish commands even if your Polish is weak.

#### Lesson 4 (`m1-w4-l4`): Key Polish Terms and Commands

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand by ear and pronounce about 30 Polish sawmill words. You respond to commands 'uwaga' (attention), 'odsuń się' (step back), 'zablokuj' (block it), 'puść' (let go), 'trzymaj' (hold).

#### Lesson 5 (`m1-w4-l5`): Basic Workstation Paperwork

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You know the documents kept at your workstation: job card, attendance log, minor-incident log, tool-use authorization. You know which you sign yourself and which the foreman fills in.

#### Lesson 6 (`m1-w4-l6`): Reporting Incidents, Faults, and Hazards

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You report to the foreman: a cut, a breakdown, spilled sawdust, a damaged stack, a missing guard. You understand why hiding a minor incident leads to a serious accident.

#### Lesson 7 (`m1-w4-l7`): M1 Recap - What You've Learned

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You review the key concepts from weeks 1-3 and see how they come together in a single workday. You know what you should be able to do before the test.

#### Lesson 8 (`m1-w4-l8`): Theory Test + Practical Prep

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You take the M1 final test (70% passing score) and receive the practical assignment scenario. You know how the foreman will evaluate you.

## Module 2 - Junior operator (autonomous role on limited machines)

### Week 1 - Safety and responsibility (`bezpieczenstwo`)

#### Lesson 1 (`m2-w1-l1`): From Helper to Operator - New Responsibility

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand what changes in your duties when you start operating a machine. You know why every action is done only after the foreman's authorization and after confirming "sprawdź - uruchom - zgłoś" (check - start - report).

**Key Polish terms**: Operator junior, Autoryzacja, Rytuał sprawdź-uruchom-zgłoś, Bieg jałowy, Skrót roboczy, Uprawnienia UDT, Mistrz zmiany.

#### Lesson 2 (`m2-w1-l2`): PPE for Bandsaw and Circular Saw Operators

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You choose goggles, hearing protectors of the right attenuation class, an FFP2/FFP3 mask for oak and beech, footwear and clothing without loose parts. You know why operator PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) differs from the helper's PPE in M1.

**Key Polish terms**: ŚOI operatora, Okulary ochronne EN 166, Klasa tłumienia SNR, Maska FFP2 / FFP3, Obuwie ochronne S3, Zakaz rękawic przy wirujących, Pył dębowy IARC grupa 1.

#### Lesson 3 (`m2-w1-l3`): STOP > START Rule and Emergency Switches

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand that on every machine the stop circuit has priority over the start circuit. You can locate the red mushroom button on a yellow background from every working position and know how to use it in a hazard situation.

**Key Polish terms**: STOP awaryjny (E-stop), Grzybek STOP, PN-EN ISO 13850, Wybieg maszyny, Reset grzybka, Kategoria zatrzymania 0/1/2, Test funkcjonalny E-stop.

#### Lesson 4 (`m2-w1-l4`): Pre-Start Machine Checklist

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: Before every start-up you perform five checks: guards, weld/blade, dust extraction, idle run, clear surroundings. You know that skipping a point = not starting the machine and reporting to the foreman.

**Key Polish terms**: Checklist przed uruchomieniem, Osłona stała / ruchoma, Zgrzew taśmy, Odciąg pyłu, Bieg jałowy, Wolne otoczenie, Zasada „5 z 5 albo zero".

#### Lesson 5 (`m2-w1-l5`): Rozp. MG 2000 - What the Law Requires from the Operator

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You know the key duties of a woodworking machine operator under Rozp. MG 2000 (Polish 2000 Ministry of Economy Regulation on woodworking machines) of 14.04.2000: brake, guards, anti-kickback, no gloves near rotating parts. You know where to find the full text.

**Key Polish terms**: Rozporządzenie MG 2000, Hamulec maszynowy, Wybieg, Klin rozszczepiający, Przeciwodrzut, Osłona stała, Osłona nastawna.

#### Lesson 6 (`m2-w1-l6`): Carcinogenic Dust and Workstation Extraction

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand why oak and beech dust is both carcinogenic and explosive. You check that the extraction is working before start-up and know when to change the filter and report its failure.

**Key Polish terms**: Pył drewna, IARC grupa 1 (kancerogen), NDS (najwyższe dopuszczalne stężenie), Odciąg stanowiskowy, Filtr FFP3, Prędkość transportowa, MIE (minimalna energia zapłonu).

#### Lesson 7 (`m2-w1-l7`): Emergency Cycle Stop Without Losing Fingers

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You know what to do when the material jams, the band comes off the wheel, or a switch trips - always stop, never remove with your hand, wait for a full stop, and call the foreman.

**Key Polish terms**: Zakleszczenie, Wybieg pod obciążeniem, LOTO (lockout/tagout), Popychacz drewniany, Moment resztkowy, Zrzucenie taśmy, Wyłącznik główny.

#### Lesson 8 (`m2-w1-l8`): First Aid for Amputation and Hemorrhage

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You act in the first three minutes after a cut injury: pressure, elevation, tourniquet, securing the amputated part. You know when to call 112 and what to say.

**Key Polish terms**: Krwotok tętniczy, Ucisk bezpośredni, Opaska uciskowa (CAT), Zimny łańcuch (replantacja), Apteczka typu K (DIN 13169), Wstrząs hipowolemiczny, Karta wypadku (ZUS-IWA).

### Week 2 - Material and tools (`material`)

#### Lesson 1 (`m2-w2-l1`): Wood Moisture - What Your Work Depends On

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand that fresh wood has 50-100% moisture, air-dried 15-20%, and export lumber 12-18%. You know why moisture determines cutting, drying, and storage.

#### Lesson 2 (`m2-w2-l2`): Moisture Measurement with a Resistance Meter

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You drive the electrodes into the board from both sides and at two points along the length, then read the result in percent. You know why a one-sided reading is unreliable.

#### Lesson 3 (`m2-w2-l3`): Handling a Long Log - Before It Reaches the Sawmill

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand why a *dłużyca* (whole felled trunk before crosscutting) is cut into logs of different quality: *odziomek* (butt log), *środek* (middle log), *wierzchołek* (top log). You know how the foreman marks the cut points and what the operator's role is.

#### Lesson 4 (`m2-w2-l4`): Sawlog Defects Critical for Breakdown Sawing

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You recognize spiral grain, false heartwood, butt rot, and bark pockets. You know why these defects change the cutting program and must be reported before starting the machine.

#### Lesson 5 (`m2-w2-l5`): Lumber Assortments - Edged, Unedged, Cant

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You tell edged from unedged lumber and recognize *oblina* (slab), *opół* (half-sawn log), and *pryzma* (cant - squared log before further sawing). You know which assortment comes off the bandsaw and which off the edger.

#### Lesson 6 (`m2-w2-l6`): Introduction to Lumber Grades A/B/C/D

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand that the same board can have different value depending on its defects. You know the four visual grades A, B, C, D and know that the master grader classifies - you sort under his instructions.

#### Lesson 7 (`m2-w2-l7`): Saws and Blades - Structure, Wear, When to Report

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You recognize a band saw, a circular blade, a frame saw, and a multi-rip saw. You know what a tooth, tip, set, and tension are; you recognize wear signs (cracks, dulling, wavy kerf).

#### Lesson 8 (`m2-w2-l8`): Changing the Saw Band Under the Foreman's Supervision

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You observe and assist with removing the worn band, fitting the new one, setting the tension to 2200-2400 PSI, and the guide rollers to 3-4 mm. You know you never do this alone.

### Week 3 - Production processes (`procesy`)

#### Lesson 1 (`m2-w3-l1`): Bandsaw - Step-by-Step Start-Up

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: Under supervision you run the procedure: guards, weld, sharpness, main switch, idle run, band observation, and only then the material. You know five reasons for an immediate STOP.

#### Lesson 2 (`m2-w3-l2`): Band Guide and Tension Adjustment

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: Under supervision you set the guides 3-4 mm from the band edge and check roller alignment in the retracted and extended positions. You know why a value below 3 mm risks a wavy kerf or a band break.

#### Lesson 3 (`m2-w3-l3`): Basic Cutting Parameters and Feed Rate

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand that the feed rate must match the species, moisture, thickness, and band type. You know how to tell the feed is too high (smoke, wavy kerf, motor overload).

#### Lesson 4 (`m2-w3-l4`): Circular and Multi-Rip Saw - Supervised Operation

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You recognize the riving knife, the push stick (for short pieces <300 mm and narrow pieces <150 mm), and the "one piece through at a time" rule. You know that crosscut and rip circular saws carry different risks.

**Key Polish terms**: klin rozszczepiający, popychacz, osłona nastawna, pilarka tarczowa wzdłużna, pilarka tarczowa poprzeczna, pilarka wielopiłowa, odrzut.

#### Lesson 5 (`m2-w3-l5`): Edger and Planer - Position and Feeding

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You stand to the side of the edger, never in the ejection line; you feed lumber into the surface planer-jointer one piece at a time, with the grain. You know where the danger zone of each of these machines begins.

**Key Polish terms**: pozycja z boku, linia wyrzutu, walki dociskowe, głowica frezowa, struganie w kierunku włókien, struganie pod włos, strefa niebezpieczna.

#### Lesson 6 (`m2-w3-l6`): Sorting Lumber by Size and Species

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You separate boards by thickness, length, and species into marked stacks, according to the foreman's instructions. You know why a label with the date and batch code is added.

**Key Polish terms**: partia jednorodna, kod partii, etykieta partii, reguła najsłabszego kryterium, tolerancja wymiarowa, podawanie dwuetapowe, długość handlowa.

#### Lesson 7 (`m2-w3-l7`): Kiln Drying - The Loader's Role

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand that pine tolerates 35-70°C, while oak and beech only 30-60°C due to the risk of deformation and resin bleed. You know that cooling must not exceed 6°C/h, and wrong temperature = cracks.

**Key Polish terms**: Suszenie komorowe, Przekładka, Fazy cyklu suszenia, Chłodzenie gradientowe, Wilgotność docelowa, Żywica w sośnie, Mistrz suszarni.

#### Lesson 8 (`m2-w3-l8`): Post-drying Inspection and Unloading the Batch

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You measure moisture with a resistance meter in 4 positions (from Week 2 Lesson 2), assess surface and end checks, and reclassify boards A→B when class thresholds are exceeded. You update the batch label and the kiln log entry. You know that class is not permanent - the material matures and degrades through drying.

**Key Polish terms**: Reklasyfikacja, Pęknięcia powierzchniowe, Pęknięcia czołowe, System 4 pozycji A/B/C/D, Rozrzut wilgotności, Kod reklasyfikacji „-r", Uczciwość dokumentacyjna.

### Week 4 - Work organization (`organizacja`)

#### Lesson 1 (`m2-w4-l1`): Sawing Work Order - What the Foreman Assigns the Operator

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You read the work order (species, target assortment, priority, quantity), ask questions when unclear, and sign off receipt of the assignment. You know what to do when the raw material doesn't match the order.

**Key Polish terms**: Dyspozycja przetarcia / Sawing work order / Orden de aserrado / Наряд на розпил, Sortyment docelowy / Target assortment / Surtido objetivo / Цільовий сортимент, Priorytet zmianowy A/B/C / Shift priority A/B/C / Prioridad de turno A/B/C / Змінний пріоритет A/B/C, Potwierdzenie odbioru / Acknowledgment of receipt / Confirmación de recepción / Підтвердження прийняття, Niezgodność surowcowa / Raw-material discrepancy / Discrepancia de materia prima / Невідповідність сировини, Eskalacja do brygadzisty / Escalation to the foreman / Escalado al capataz / Ескалація до бригадира, Operator w brygadzie / Operator in the crew / Operario en la brigada / Оператор у бригаді.

#### Lesson 2 (`m2-w4-l2`): Operator's Shift Log and Output Register

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: After the shift you fill in: machine running hours, downtime, bands used, reported faults, volume of raw material sawn. You know why the log goes to the foreman and to the dispatcher.

**Key Polish terms**: Dziennik zmiany operatora / Operator's shift log / Diario de turno del operador / Журнал зміни оператора, Motogodziny / Machine running hours / Horas de funcionamiento / Мотогодини, Wskaźnik wydajności / Output ratio / Índice de rendimiento / Коефіцієнт виходу, Typy przestojów T/M/O/P / Downtime types T/M/O/P / Tipos de parada T/M/O/P / Типи простоїв T/M/O/P, Numer taśmy / Band number / Número de cinta / Номер стрічки, Format trzyczęściowy usterki / Three-part fault format / Formato tripartito de avería / Трискладовий формат несправності, Łańcuch zmian / Shift chain / Cadena de turnos / Ланцюг змін.

#### Lesson 3 (`m2-w4-l3`): PN-EN Standards and FSC/PEFC Certificates in Brief

**Duration**: 120 minutes

**Goal**: You understand that PN-EN 1611 (European standard for visual softwood grading) covers visual sorting, PN-EN 14081 (European standard for strength-graded structural timber) covers strength grading, and FSC/PEFC is a wood supply-chain certification. You know why an export customer asks about these marks.

**Key Polish terms**: PN-EN 1611 / PN-EN 1611 / PN-EN 1611 / PN-EN 1611, PN-EN 14081 / PN-EN 14081 / PN-EN 14081 / PN-EN 14081, Klasa wytrzymałościowa C24 / Strength class C24 / Clase de resistencia C24 / Клас міцності C24, FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) / FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) / FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) / FSC (Forest Stewardship Council), PEFC (Programme for the Endorsement of Forest Certification) / PEFC / PEFC / PEFC, Chain of Custody (CoC) / Chain of Custody (CoC) / Cadena de custodia (CoC) / Ланцюг постачання (CoC), Pole certyfikowane / Certified pile / Pila certificada / Сертифікована ділянка.

#### Lesson 4 (`m2-w4-l4`): Working with the Forklift Operator
