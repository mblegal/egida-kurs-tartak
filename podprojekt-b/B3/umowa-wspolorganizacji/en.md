# FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT ON THE CO-ORGANISATION OF THE PRACTICAL COURSE

**„Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill)**: a vocational course for foreigners legally residing on the territory of the Republic of Poland

hereinafter referred to as the „**Agreement**" or the „**Framework Agreement**"

---

## Preamble

Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (hereinafter referred to as the „Foundation") is a public benefit organisation which, within the framework of its statutory objectives, conducts educational and integration activities for the benefit of foreigners legally residing on the territory of the Republic of Poland. One of the flagship educational projects of the Foundation is the practical course entitled „Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill) (hereinafter referred to as the „Course"), preparing Course Participants to take up legal employment in Polish sawmills and to acquire in-demand qualifications in the area of wood processing for the needs of the construction, joinery and furniture industries.

The implementation of the Course requires cooperation with entrepreneurs operating in the sawmill sector who are able to make actual workstations available under production conditions for the purposes of the main practical component of the Course. This Framework Agreement sets out the rules of cooperation between the Foundation and the Entrepreneur identified below as regards the organisation of successive Cycles of the Course, while preserving the principle that the Foundation directs the Course as a whole as its organiser, and the Entrepreneur organises on the Foundation's behalf the main practical component at its sawmill plant with ongoing support and consultation from the Foundation.

The Parties jointly declare that their cooperation is based on mutual goodwill and on a public-private partnership in the area of the integration of foreigners through professional competences, without mutual financial settlements, and that neither Party derives from its participation in the Course any financial benefits other than indirect benefits resulting from the realisation of its own statutory objectives (the Foundation) or from the possibility of acquiring qualified employees in the future on market terms (the Entrepreneur).

## II. Definitions

The terms used in this Agreement have the following meanings:

1. **Course** – the practical course entitled „Praca w tartaku" (Work in a sawmill), organised by the Foundation in cooperation with the Entrepreneur, consisting of three modules (M1, M2, M3) with a total duration of 316 teaching hours over 12 weeks, conducted in accordance with the programme approved by the Foundation.
2. **Cycle** – a single edition of the Course, covering a specific group of Course Participants, conducted within a defined period (commencement, completion) in accordance with the annex to this Agreement.
3. **Course Participant** – a person admitted by the Foundation to take part in the Course on the basis of a separate tripartite agreement (Foundation – Entrepreneur – Course Participant), referred to as the „Course Agreement".
4. **Course Agreement** – the tripartite agreement concluded separately with each Course Participant, governing the rights and obligations of the Foundation, the Entrepreneur and the Course Participant during that Course Participant's participation in the Course.
5. **Main practical component** – that component of the Course in which the Course Participant performs actual sawmill tasks at the Entrepreneur's plant under the supervision of a person designated in accordance with point IV paragraph 2 letter b.
6. **Theoretical component and workshop component** – components of the Course conducted by the Foundation's teaching staff in the Foundation's training room in Opole or, where permissible, at another location agreed between the Parties in an annex to the Agreement.
7. **Supervisor** – a person designated by the Entrepreneur, holding competences at least at the level corresponding to the competences of a graduate of module M3 of this Course or equivalent (e.g. a foreman, master craftsman, experienced operator with a documented professional record), exercising direct supervision over the Course Participant during the performance of tasks at the sawmill plant.
8. **Classifier** – a member of the Foundation's teaching staff responsible for the assessment of the Course Participant's competences and for issuing an opinion on the Course Participant's readiness to progress to the next module, in particular to module M3 (independent operator).
9. **Coordinator** – a member of the Foundation's staff responsible for the day-to-day organisation of the Course Cycle, contact with the Entrepreneur and documentation handling.
10. **Cycle annex** – an annex to this Agreement, containing the data of a specific Cycle (dates, schedule, list of Course Participants, any detailed arrangements), signed by the Parties before the commencement of each Cycle.

## III. Subject matter of the Agreement

1. The subject matter of the Agreement is the **regulation of the rules of cooperation** between the Parties in organising successive Cycles of the Course, in particular the rules on the division of roles, liability, insurance of the Course Participant, protection of personal data and the procedure for agreeing the details of each Cycle.

2. The Agreement is of a **framework** nature. The specific arrangements relating to a single Cycle of the Course (dates, list of Course Participants, weekly schedule, location of the main practical component at the plant, any deviations) shall be set out by the Parties in a **Cycle annex**, concluded before the commencement of each Cycle in written form on pain of nullity.

3. The conclusion of this Agreement does not oblige either Party to launch a specific Cycle of the Course. The decision to launch a Cycle requires a concurring declaration of the Parties in the form of a Cycle annex.

4. **General division of roles**:

   a) **The Foundation directs the Course** as its formal organiser and as the entity responsible for its programme, certification and compliance with statutory assumptions;

   b) **The Entrepreneur organises** the main practical component of the Course at its sawmill plant, on behalf of the Foundation and in accordance with the Foundation's programme guidelines;

   c) **The Foundation assists and consults** the Entrepreneur in the performance of its organisational tasks at the plant, in particular by making available teaching materials, introductory instructions, model documentation and through ongoing contact between the Foundation's teaching staff and the Supervisor.

5. The hierarchy established in paragraph 4 means that in the event of a discrepancy between the Course programme approved by the Foundation and the organisational practice at the Entrepreneur's plant, the Foundation's programme prevails, and the Parties agree by way of consultation on the manner of adjusting the practice to the programme, and not the other way round.

## IV. Obligations of the Parties

### 1. Obligations of the Foundation

The Foundation undertakes to:

a) provide the teaching staff of the Course (trainers, coordinator, classifiers) with qualifications adequate to the scope of modules M1, M2 and M3, and to maintain that staff for the purposes of successive Cycles;

b) develop, update and make available to the Entrepreneur the programme of the Course, the teaching materials in four language versions (Polish, English, Spanish and Ukrainian), templates of attendance lists, assessment cards, modular and final test protocols, as well as templates of module certificates and the certificate of completion of the Course;

c) conduct the theoretical component and the workshop component of the Course at the location indicated in the Cycle annex (as a rule, in the Foundation's training room in Opole, ul. Ozimska 14-16);

d) recruit Course Participants, assess their initial qualifications, conclude with each of them a Course Agreement and provide the Entrepreneur with a list of Course Participants together with the minimum set of data necessary for the organisation of the main practical component (first name, surname, language of communication, any health restrictions resulting from the OHS medical certificate);

e) **insure the Course Participant against the consequences of accidents (PA insurance, in Polish: NNW)** for the duration of the entire Course, at the Foundation's own cost, whereby this insurance is provided by the Foundation always and irrespective of any group policies of the Entrepreneur applicable at its plant in respect of its own employees; this provision constitutes a clarification of point V paragraph 1 letter e of the Course Agreement to the effect that there is no dispute between the Parties as to the entity obliged to provide PA insurance for the Course Participant;

f) conduct general initial OHS training for each Course Participant before admitting them to the main practical component, and to provide the Entrepreneur with the signed initial training card;

g) issue to the Course Participant module certificates upon completion of modules M1, M2 and M3 (attendance threshold 75%) and a certificate of completion of the entire Course upon passing the final test (threshold 70%, i.e. 76 points out of 108);

h) maintain the overall documentation of the conduct of the Course (attendance lists, assessment cards, test protocols, correspondence with the Entrepreneur and with Course Participants) and to retain it in accordance with the Foundation's data retention policy;

i) **provide ongoing consultation to the Entrepreneur** during the Cycle as regards the manner of implementing the Course programme at the plant, in particular by enabling direct contact between the Supervisor and the trainer conducting the given module and the classifier;

j) **carry out, during the Cycle, at least one visit by the Foundation's staff** (coordinator or classifier) to the Entrepreneur's plant for the purpose of observing the conduct of the main practical component and gathering any comments from the Supervisor;

k) fulfil the obligations of controller of the Course Participants' personal data within the scope arising from the GDPR, on the terms set out in the GDPR information clause constituting an annex to the Course Agreement.

### 2. Obligations of the Entrepreneur

The Entrepreneur undertakes to:

a) make workstations available to Course Participants at the sawmill plant indicated in point I, to the extent and for the period necessary to carry out the main practical component in accordance with the Course programme and the Cycle schedule;

b) designate a **Supervisor** for each group of Course Participants and ensure the Supervisor's availability during the hours when the main practical component is carried out; the Supervisor must hold competences at least at the level corresponding to that of a graduate of module M3 of this Course or equivalent, confirmed by a documented professional record;

c) provide Course Participants with personal protective equipment (PPE) consistent with the occupational risk assessment for the given workstation, on terms analogous to the rules applicable to the Entrepreneur's own employees at the plant;

d) carry out **workstation OHS instruction** for each Course Participant before admitting them to the performance of tasks at a given workstation, and to document such instruction on terms analogous to the rules applicable to the Entrepreneur's own employees;

e) organise the work of Course Participants at the plant in a manner ensuring safe and hygienic conditions, in accordance with the principles applied accordingly on the basis of Article 207 of the Act of 26 June 1974 - Labour Code (consolidated text, as amended), taking into account that the Course Participant is not an employee of the Entrepreneur, but a person directed to the plant under the rules of a practical course, the protection of whose health and life at the plant nevertheless remains fully within the sphere of responsibility of the Entrepreneur as the host of the place and the holder of occupational risk at the plant;

f) refrain from employing the Course Participant in overtime hours during the Course, refrain from issuing instructions to the Course Participant going beyond the didactic scope of the module in which the Course Participant is currently engaged, and refrain from issuing instructions requiring competences higher than those documented by the Course Participant's certificate from the previous module;

g) confirm the attendance of the Course Participant on the attendance list maintained in accordance with the template provided by the Foundation and forward the completed attendance list to the Foundation upon completion of each week of classes in the Cycle;

h) **provide an opinion on the readiness of the Course Participant to progress to the next module**, in particular to module M3 (independent operator), in consultation with the Foundation's classifier; the Supervisor's opinion does not replace the Foundation's classifier's decision on admitting the Course Participant to the next module, but constitutes a material and mandatory input thereto;

i) signal to the Foundation any need for changes in the programme arising from observation of the Course Participant under real conditions, in the manner indicated in point VIII of this Agreement;

j) fulfil the obligations of a separate controller of the Course Participants' personal data within the scope arising from the GDPR and from point VII of this Agreement, in particular as regards data processed for the purposes of OHS instruction, registration of attendance at the plant and supervision of work safety.

## V. Financial settlements

1. The Parties jointly declare that this cooperation is of a **mutually non-remunerative** nature.

2. The Foundation does not collect from the Entrepreneur any remuneration, fee, commission or other financial consideration on account of organising the Course, conducting the theoretical component and the workshop component, providing teaching materials or on any other ground arising from this Agreement.

3. The Entrepreneur does not collect from the Foundation any remuneration, fee, rent or other financial consideration on account of making workstations at the plant available, the working time of the Supervisor, the consumption of operating materials arising in the course of the performance of tasks by the Course Participant or on any other ground arising from this Agreement.

4. Each Party shall bear, within its own scope, its own operating costs arising from the performance of its obligations set out in this Agreement, with no right to claim their reimbursement from the other Party.

5. The provisions of this point do not preclude the possibility of the Parties concluding, in the future and in a separate document, a remunerative agreement relating to a subject matter of cooperation other than the Course (e.g. paid advisory services of the Foundation in the area of integration of foreigners, paid participation of the Entrepreneur's staff in other training events of the Foundation); such agreements do not form part of this Framework Agreement.

6. The Parties declare that the non-remunerative nature of the cooperation corresponds to the statutory objectives of the Foundation as a public benefit organisation, and that neither Party treats the mutual considerations covered by this Agreement as a basis for the recognition of revenue or cost within the meaning of the income tax regulations; any tax consequences of this arrangement are recognised by each Party within its own scope and at its own responsibility.

## VI. Insurance and liability

### 1. Insurance of the Course Participant

a) The Foundation provides the Course Participant with insurance against the consequences of accidents (PA insurance, in Polish: NNW) for the entire duration of the Course, including the period of performance of the main practical component at the Entrepreneur's plant.

b) The policy referred to in letter a covers, as a minimum, events occurring during the Course classes (theoretical, workshop and main practical components) and the journey from the Course Participant's place of residence to the place of classes and back.

c) The Foundation provides the Entrepreneur with confirmation of the conclusion of the PA insurance policy (policy number, insurer, sums insured, period of validity) no later than on the date of commencement of the given Cycle of the Course.

d) The provisions of this paragraph are binding irrespective of whether the Entrepreneur covers its own employees with a group PA insurance policy or other insurance; any policy of the Entrepreneur does not release the Foundation from the obligation to provide PA insurance for the Course Participant, nor does it replace the Foundation's policy.

### 2. Liability for events during the Course

a) **The Entrepreneur alone is liable for the state of occupational health and safety at the sawmill plant at which the main practical component is carried out, and for events taking place at that plant during the performance by the Course Participant of tasks within the framework of the Course**, on principles applied accordingly to the principles of employer liability set out in Article 207 of the Act of 26 June 1974 - Labour Code (consolidated text, as amended).

b) The scope of the Entrepreneur's liability referred to in letter a is not limited by reason of the fact that the Course Participant is not an employee of the Entrepreneur within the meaning of the Labour Code. The Parties jointly accept that the Entrepreneur, as the host of the plant and the holder of occupational risk at the plant, bears full responsibility for the safety of persons present at that plant for the purposes of the Course, in a manner analogous to the responsibility owed to its own employees.

c) **The Foundation is liable for the general initial OHS training** (content, correctness of conduct, documentation), for the correctness of the Course programme in the theoretical and workshop components, and for events occurring in the Foundation's training room in Opole and in the Foundation's workshop facility.

d) The Parties jointly exclude joint and several liability of the Parties for events taking place at the Entrepreneur's plant during the performance by the Course Participant of tasks within the framework of the main practical component. The above exclusion does not limit the Foundation's liability for damage resulting from errors in the Course programme or in the general initial OHS training caused through the Foundation's fault, where the Foundation's error constituted the direct cause of the event at the plant.

e) Each Party is liable for damage caused to the other Party on account of non-performance or improper performance of the obligations arising from this Agreement on the general principles set out in Article 471 et seq. of the Act of 23 April 1964 - Civil Code (consolidated text, as amended).

f) The Parties undertake to **inform each other without delay** of any event during a Cycle of the Course which may give rise to liability of one of the Parties, in particular of accidents involving Course Participants at the plant, of claims by Course Participants or third parties, of inspection proceedings of state authorities concerning the Course, and of any circumstances that may affect the conduct of the Cycle.

## VII. Protection of personal data (GDPR)

1. The Parties jointly declare that, within the framework of the implementation of the Course, they act as **separate controllers of the Course Participants' personal data** within the meaning of Article 4(7) GDPR, each within its own scope and on its own legal bases. The Parties do not enter into a joint controllership arrangement within the meaning of Article 26 GDPR.

2. **The Foundation is the controller of the Course Participants' personal data** as regards:

   a) recruitment to the Course, including the assessment of initial qualifications,

   b) maintaining the overall documentation of the conduct of the Course (attendance lists, assessment cards, test protocols),

   c) issuing module certificates and the certificate of completion of the entire Course,

   d) fulfilment of the statistical and reporting obligations of the Foundation as a public benefit organisation,

   e) insurance of the Course Participant against the consequences of accidents,

   f) conducting correspondence with the Course Participant and with the Entrepreneur on matters arising from the Course Agreement.

3. **The Entrepreneur is a separate controller of the Course Participants' personal data** as regards:

   a) workstation OHS instruction and its documentation,

   b) registration of the Course Participant's attendance at the sawmill plant,

   c) supervision of the Course Participant's work safety at the plant,

   d) fulfilment of the employer's obligations applied accordingly (Article 207 of the Labour Code) within the scope arising from point VI of this Agreement,

   e) any accident documentation, where the event took place at the plant.

4. **Boundary of the scopes of controllership**: data processed for the purposes listed in paragraph 2 fall within the exclusive competence of the Foundation; data processed for the purposes listed in paragraph 3 fall within the exclusive competence of the Entrepreneur. The Parties do not share with each other Course Participants' data going beyond the minimum scope necessary for the purposes of directing Course Participants to the performance of the main practical component (point IV paragraph 1 letter d).

5. Each Party fulfils, within its own scope, the information obligation towards Course Participants arising from Article 13 GDPR; the Foundation does so by means of the GDPR information clause constituting an annex to the Course Agreement, and the Entrepreneur by means of its own internal OHS information clause applied to all persons present at the plant.

6. Each Party is responsible, within its own scope, for the security of the Course Participants' data processed by it and implements appropriate technical and organisational measures in accordance with Article 32 GDPR.

7. The Parties undertake to **inform each other of breaches of the protection of Course Participants' personal data**, where such breach concerns data processed in connection with the Course, within a period not exceeding 24 hours from the discovery of the breach, while preserving each Party's separate obligation to notify the breach to the President of the Personal Data Protection Office in accordance with Article 33 GDPR.

## VIII. Operational cooperation

### 1. Hierarchy of leadership

The Foundation directs the Course as a whole as its organiser. As regards the Course programme, the selection of teaching staff, the rules of assessment and certification, the decisions of the Foundation are binding on the Entrepreneur. The Entrepreneur organises the implementation of the main practical component in accordance with the Foundation's programme guidelines.

### 2. Classifiers and assessment of the Course Participant

a) Classifiers are designated by the Foundation from among its teaching staff.

b) The Classifier assesses the Course Participant's competences on the basis of the modular tests, the Classifier's own observation and the Supervisor's opinion gathered in accordance with letter c.

c) The Supervisor provides the Foundation's Classifier with an opinion on the Course Participant's readiness to progress to the next module, in particular to module M3, no later than 7 days before the planned progression; the opinion shall cover, as a minimum, observations as to the Course Participant's independence, compliance with OHS rules, the quality of the tasks performed and communication with the Supervisor.

d) **The decision on admitting the Course Participant to the next module is taken solely by the Foundation's Classifier**, taking into account the Supervisor's opinion as a material and mandatory element of the input, but not as a decision binding on the Classifier.

e) In the event of a discrepancy between the Supervisor's opinion and the Classifier's preliminary assessment, the Classifier is obliged to consult the Supervisor directly before taking the final decision.

### 3. Substantive consultation by the Foundation with the Entrepreneur

a) The trainer conducting a given module of the Course remains at the disposal of the Supervisor during the Cycle for the purpose of substantive consultation regarding the module programme, the manner of conducting workstation instruction, the interpretation of OHS requirements and other matters connected with the implementation of the module at the plant.

b) Contact between the Supervisor and the trainer takes place on a working basis (telephone, e-mail, possibly a meeting at the plant or in the Foundation's training room), during the working hours of both Parties and without the need to observe any particular form.

c) At least once during a Cycle, the Foundation delegates to the Entrepreneur's plant a coordinator or classifier in order to observe the conduct of the main practical component and to gather the Supervisor's comments. The Parties agree the date of the visit at least 7 days in advance.

### 4. Signalling needs for changes in the programme

a) The Entrepreneur may, during a Cycle or after its completion, signal to the Foundation any needs for changes in the Course programme, in particular as regards technical issues arising in the sawmill sector, changing market requirements or competence gaps observed in Course Participants.

b) The Foundation considers each signal of the Entrepreneur and, where it recognises its merit, introduces a change to the Course programme with effect from the next new Cycle, informing the Entrepreneur in writing (or by e-mail) of the decision within 60 days of receipt of the signal.

c) Signalling the need for programme changes does not give rise to any obligation on the part of the Foundation to introduce a change; the final decision on the Course programme remains within the exclusive competence of the Foundation.

## IX. Legal basis and status of the Agreement

1. The Agreement is implemented within the framework of the **statutory activity of the Foundation** as a public benefit organisation, in particular on the basis of:

   a) Article 3 paragraph 1 in conjunction with Article 4 paragraph 1 points 2, 5a and 8 of the Act of 24 April 2003 on Public Benefit Activity and Volunteerism (consolidated text Dz.U. 2024, item 1491, as amended),

   b) the provisions of the Foundation's statute, made available for inspection on the website: https://fundacjaegida.eu.

2. The Agreement constitutes a **separate co-organisation agreement** referred to in point VI paragraph 3 of the Course Agreement concluded with each Course Participant; it does not form part of the Course Agreement and is not made available to the Course Participant by force of the provisions of the Course Agreement, although the Course Participant is aware of its existence by reference made in the Course Agreement.

3. In matters not regulated by this Agreement, the provisions of the Act of 23 April 1964 - Civil Code (consolidated text, as amended) shall apply, in particular the provisions on liability for non-performance or improper performance of an obligation (Article 471 et seq.), and accordingly the provisions of the Act of 26 June 1974 - Labour Code (consolidated text, as amended) within the scope indicated in point VI of this Agreement.

## X. Duration of the Agreement

1. The Agreement enters into force on the date of its signature by both Parties and is concluded for an **indefinite period**.

2. Either Party may terminate the Agreement upon **three-month notice**, calculated from the end of the calendar month in which the notice was delivered to the other Party. The notice requires the written form on pain of nullity.

3. Termination of the Framework Agreement **does not affect Cycles of the Course already commenced** as at the date of delivery of the notice. Cycles commenced before the date of delivery of the notice end in accordance with the Cycle annex, and the obligations of the Parties arising from them remain in force until the certificate of completion has been issued to the last Course Participants from those Cycles.

4. **A Cycle annex** expires on the date of completion of the Cycle to which it relates, irrespective of the further binding force of the Framework Agreement. Expiry of the annex does not require a separate declaration of will of the Parties.

5. Notwithstanding paragraph 2, either Party may terminate the Agreement with immediate effect in the event of a gross breach by the other Party of the obligations arising from this Agreement or from the Cycle annex, where, despite a written request to remedy the breach within 14 days, that breach has not been remedied. Termination with immediate effect requires the written form, with the reason given, on pain of nullity, and does not affect Cycles already completed.

---

## XI. Final provisions

1. Any amendments to the Agreement require the written form on pain of nullity, with the exception of amendments to Cycle annexes, which may be introduced by an exchange of letters (including an exchange of e-mails with confirmed read-receipt), with an unambiguous indication of the Cycle and of the paragraph of the annex to which the amendment relates.

2. If any provision of the Agreement is held to be invalid or ineffective, the remaining provisions of the Agreement shall remain in force, and the Parties undertake to agree in good faith on a substitute provision as close as possible to the original intent of the invalid or ineffective provision.

3. The Parties shall settle disputes arising from this Agreement amicably. In the absence of agreement within 30 days from the date of delivery to the other Party of a written request for amicable resolution of the dispute, the court competent to hear the case is the common court having jurisdiction over the registered seat of the Foundation.

4. The Agreement has been drawn up in **two identical counterparts**, one for each of the Parties.

5. The annexes to this Framework Agreement are the successive **Cycle annexes** drawn up in accordance with point III paragraph 2, signed before the commencement of each Cycle of the Course.
