# GDPR INFORMATION CLAUSE AND CONSENTS TO THE PROCESSING OF PERSONAL DATA

**Practical course "Praca w tartaku" ("Work in a Sawmill")**: a vocational course for foreigners legally residing in the territory of the Republic of Poland, organised by Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation) in cooperation with an Entrepreneur operating a sawmill establishment.

This document is delivered to the Course Participant **before the signing of the Agreement on the conduct of the practical Course** (hereinafter: the "**Course Agreement**") as its annex and as part of the information requirement under Article 13 of the GDPR. It consists of two parts:

- **Part I**, the information clause on the processing of the Course Participant's personal data by the Foundation and by the Entrepreneur. Familiarisation with Part I is a condition of participation in the Course. Part I does not require the Course Participant's consent, since the processing takes place on legal bases other than consent (performance of the Agreement, legal obligation, legitimate interests of the controller, protection of health within the scope of occupational health and safety).
- **Part II**, optional consents. Granting or withholding the consents in Part II does not affect the Course Participant's ability to take part in the Course. Each consent is separate, voluntary and may be withdrawn at any time.

### 2. Second controller (principal practical part at the sawmill establishment)

With regard to the Course Participant's personal data processed during the principal practical part of the Course performed at the sawmill establishment, the **separate controller** is:

The **Entrepreneur**, that is the entity operating the sawmill establishment with which the Foundation has concluded an agreement on the co-organisation of the Course, identified in the Course Agreement (section I "Parties to the agreement", field "Business name of the Entrepreneur"). The full business name, registered seat, registration data and the person representing the Entrepreneur are entered in the Course Agreement individually for each cycle of the Course, depending on the sawmill at which the principal practical part takes place.

The Entrepreneur processes the Course Participant's personal data **solely** within the scope and for the purpose necessary for the conduct of the principal practical part, in particular for the purposes of occupational health and safety instruction, attendance records at the workstation and supervision of work safety. The Entrepreneur is in this scope responsible independently and separately from the Foundation as a separate controller within the meaning of Article 4(7) of the GDPR. The Entrepreneur is obliged to fulfil its own information obligation towards the Course Participant under Article 13 of the GDPR before the Course Participant is admitted to the performance of tasks at the sawmill establishment.

**This clause neither excludes nor replaces the Entrepreneur's information obligation.** The Course Participant receives a separate information clause from the Entrepreneur before the commencement of the principal practical part.

### 5. Categories of recipients of the data

The Foundation discloses the Course Participant's personal data solely to the following categories of recipients:

a) the **teaching staff of the Foundation** (trainers, the coordinator, classifiers, persons issuing certificates), within the scope necessary for the conduct of classes and for marking;

b) the **Entrepreneur**, within the scope necessary for the conduct of the principal practical part; the Entrepreneur as a separate controller further processes the data on its own responsibility;

c) the **insurer** with which the Course Participant has been covered by accident insurance for the duration of the Course (in accordance with point V paragraph 1 letter e of the Course Agreement);

d) **entities providing auxiliary services to the Foundation** (accounting, legal services, e-mail and IT system hosting), solely on the basis of data processing entrustment agreements within the meaning of Article 28 of the GDPR;

e) **public authorities entitled to obtain the data** on the basis of applicable provisions of law, in particular the President of the Office for Personal Data Protection (UODO), the courts, law enforcement authorities, fiscal control authorities and authorities supervising foundations;

f) solely in the event that consent for the newsletter is granted (consent 1, Part II), the **mailing service system** used by the Foundation, within the scope necessary for the distribution of messages;

g) solely in the event that consent for the use of image is granted (consent 2, Part II), the **recipients of the Foundation's promotional materials**, including persons visiting its website, the social media profiles run by the Foundation and the publishers of the Foundation's publications.

The Foundation does not sell the Course Participant's personal data to any entity and does not disclose them for the purposes of marketing carried out by third parties.

### 7. Rights of the Course Participant

The Course Participant has, vis-à-vis the Foundation, the following rights arising from the GDPR:

a) **the right of access to the data** (Article 15 of the GDPR), including the right to obtain a copy of the data;

b) **the right to rectification of data** that are incorrect or out of date (Article 16 of the GDPR);

c) **the right to erasure of data** ("the right to be forgotten", Article 17 of the GDPR), subject to the limitations arising from Article 17(3) of the GDPR, in particular where the processing is necessary for the Foundation's compliance with legal obligations (for example archiving of certificates);

d) **the right to restriction of processing** (Article 18 of the GDPR);

e) **the right to data portability** in respect of data processed on the basis of consent or contract, in an automated manner (Article 20 of the GDPR);

f) **the right to object** to processing based on Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR (legitimate interests of the controller), on grounds relating to the Course Participant's particular situation (Article 21 of the GDPR);

g) **the right to withdraw consent** at any time, in respect of processing based on consent (newsletter, image, data on ethnic origin), without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out on the basis of consent before its withdrawal (Article 7(3) of the GDPR);

h) **the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority**, which in the Republic of Poland is the **President of the Office for Personal Data Protection (UODO)**, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, website: https://uodo.gov.pl, if the Course Participant considers that the processing of his or her personal data infringes the provisions of the GDPR or of other provisions on the protection of personal data.

In order to exercise any of the rights referred to in letters a) to g), the Course Participant submits a request to the e-mail address or postal address indicated in point 3 of this clause. The Foundation provides a reply without undue delay and not later than within one month of receipt of the request, in accordance with Article 12(3) of the GDPR. In particularly complex cases that period may be extended by a further two months, of which the Foundation informs the Course Participant.

### 8. Requirement to provide the data and consequences of failure to provide them

Provision of the personal data:

a) indicated in section I of the Course Agreement ("Parties to the agreement", field "Course Participant": first name, surname, date of birth, citizenship, identity document, title of residence in the Republic of Poland, postal address, e-mail address and telephone number) is **necessary for the conclusion and performance of the Course Agreement**. Failure to provide such data, or the provision of untrue data, results in the inability to conclude the Course Agreement and to take part in the Course;

b) of the medical certificate confirming the absence of contraindications to work in sawmill conditions is **necessary for the conduct of the principal practical part of the Course**, on grounds of occupational health and safety obligations. Failure to provide the certificate results in the inability to take part in the principal practical part and, consequently, in the inability to complete the Course;

c) for the purposes requiring consent (newsletter, image, ethnic origin or nationality) is **entirely voluntary**. Failure to provide such data or to grant consent in Part II of this document does not affect in any way the Course Participant's ability to take part in the Course or the scope of the certificates issued to the Course Participant.

### 9. Absence of automated decision-making and profiling

The Foundation declares that the Course Participant's personal data **are not processed in an automated manner producing decisions which produce legal effects concerning the Course Participant or similarly significantly affect the Course Participant** (Article 22 of the GDPR). In particular, decisions on the passing of tests, on the issuance of certificates and on admission to subsequent Modules of the Course are taken solely by the teaching staff of the Foundation as natural persons. The Foundation **does not apply profiling** within the meaning of Article 4(4) of the GDPR.

### 10. Absence of transfers of data to third countries and to international organisations

The Foundation declares that the Course Participant's personal data **are not transferred to third countries** (countries outside the European Economic Area) or to **international organisations** within the meaning of Article 4(25) and (26) of the GDPR. Should the Foundation in the future intend to commence such transfers, it will fulfil a separate information obligation towards the Course Participant and, where necessary, will obtain a legal basis for the transfer in accordance with Chapter V of the GDPR (an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses, binding corporate rules or the consent of the data subject).

### 11. Amendments to this information clause

The Foundation may introduce amendments to this information clause in the event of changes to the provisions of law, changes to the methods of data processing or for other important reasons. The Foundation notifies the Course Participant of any such amendment by electronic means at the e-mail address indicated in the Course Agreement, with at least 14 days' notice. A Course Participant who does not accept an amendment to the clause may withdraw from the Course Agreement within 14 days of being notified of the amendment, on the terms set out in point XII paragraph 3 letter d of the Course Regulations.
