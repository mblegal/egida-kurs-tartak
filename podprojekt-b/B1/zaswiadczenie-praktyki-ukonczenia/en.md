## IV. Type of Work Performed during the Apprenticeship

During the apprenticeship the Trainee performed the following type of work, on three increasing competence levels corresponding to the modular structure of the Practical Course „Praca w tartaku":

### 1. Module M1: auxiliary work in a sawmill (4 weeks / 72 teaching hours)

Work consisting in supporting sawmill processes at the Entrepreneur's facility, in particular:

a) recognition of wood raw material in terms of species, quality class and technological suitability;

b) marking and moving of wood material within the sawmill facility;

c) basic handling and warehousing operations (stacking, sorting, preparation for further processing);

d) absorption and application of the OHS regime of the sawmill facility;

e) basics of vocational communication in a Polish-speaking environment.

### 2. Module M2: work as an operator under supervision (4 weeks / 112 teaching hours)

Work consisting in the operation of sawmill machines under the supervision of a competent person, in particular:

a) supervised operation of basic sawmill machines: band saws, circular saws and band saws, planers;

b) control of cutting parameters, accuracy and quality of the material;

c) application of OHS procedures when working with mechanical devices;

d) keeping of workshop documentation (production reports, operating sheets);

e) cooperation within a production team under the direction of a foreman or master.

### 3. Module M3: work as an independent operator (4 weeks / 132 teaching hours)

Work consisting in the independent operation of sawmill machines throughout the full production cycle, in particular:

a) independent operation of sawmill machines throughout the full production cycle;

b) planning and optimization of the cutting process depending on the material and the order;

c) quality control and sorting of sawmill material;

d) diagnostics of simple machine malfunctions and decision to suspend work in the event of technological doubts;

e) supervision of auxiliary workers (Trainees from Module M1) and operators under supervision (Trainees from Module M2);

f) knowledge of sawmill material quality standards applied in Poland.

## V. Skills Acquired during the Apprenticeship

During the apprenticeship the Trainee acquired the following practical skills, confirmed by observation by the supervising person at the Entrepreneur's facility and by meeting the attendance threshold of 75% of the hours completed for each Module of the Course:

### A. Technical skills (hard competences)

a) recognition and classification of wood raw material and sawmill materials (competence developed from Module M1);

b) operation of basic sawmill machines (band saws, saws, planers) initially under supervision (Module M2) and subsequently independently (Module M3);

c) planning of the cutting process and optimization of efficiency in relation to the material and the order;

d) control of cutting parameters, dimensional accuracy and material quality;

e) sorting and classification of finished material;

f) diagnostics of simple malfunctions of sawmill devices and decision-making on their reporting or suspension of work;

g) keeping of basic workshop documentation.

### B. Organisational and safety skills (soft and regulatory competences)

a) application of the OHS regime in the sawmill facility, including the use of personal protective equipment (PPE);

b) observance of evacuation procedures and conduct in the event of an OHS incident;

c) cooperation within a production team under the direction of a foreman, master or experienced operator;

d) vocational communication in Polish in a production environment, including understanding of OHS instructions and workstation instructions;

e) preparation of daily work reports and documentation of the operations performed;

f) decision-making on the suspension of work in situations of doubt from the point of view of safety or product quality.

### C. Competence Profile of the Apprenticeship Graduate

Upon completion of the apprenticeship, the Trainee achieved competences corresponding to the profile of an **independent operator of sawmill machinery** (level M3 of the Course „Praca w tartaku"), capable of making technological decisions throughout the full production cycle and of supervising auxiliary workers. This profile corresponds to the demand of Polish sawmills, wood-processing facilities, production joinery workshops and enterprises of the furniture and construction industry.

## VII. Purpose of the Certificate

This certificate:

a) constitutes the document referred to in **Article 7(1) of the Act of 17 July 2009 on Graduate Apprenticeships**, that is, a written certificate of the host entity concerning the **type of work performed and the skills acquired** by the Trainee during the apprenticeship;

b) confirms the fact of completion of the Trainee's graduate apprenticeship at the Entrepreneur in the period indicated in section III, within the framework of the Foundation's referral;

c) serves the Trainee as **documentation of the practical qualifications obtained** in a form useful for a potential employer in the sawmill, timber and joinery industry as well as in the furniture industry, and also for the authorities competent for the recognition of qualifications obtained outside Poland (Article 93 of the Act of 7 September 1991 on the Education System);

d) **does not replace** the certificate of completion of the Practical Course (B1 #6 of the Course documents package), which separately confirms the completion of the Course as a whole, including the passing of the final test with a threshold of 70%, that is 76 points out of 108. The Trainee may receive both certificates jointly (certificate of completion of the apprenticeship + certificate of completion of the Course) or only one of them, depending on the fulfilment of the requirements of each document;

e) **does not replace** the certificates of completion of the individual Modules of the Course (B1 #3, B1 #4, B1 #5 for M1, M2, M3), issued by the Foundation upon meeting the attendance threshold of 75% of the relevant Module;

f) **does not replace** the graduate apprenticeship agreement concluded between the Trainee and the Entrepreneur in accordance with Article 5(1) of the Act of 17 July 2009, nor the certificate of performance of the graduate apprenticeship (B1 #7a), issued during the apprenticeship for the purpose of presentation to the competent authorities.
