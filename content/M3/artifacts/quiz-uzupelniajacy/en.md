# M3 Supplementary Final Quiz

## Purpose

This quiz is a **supplement to the l8 final test of Module 3** (108 pts, 70% pass threshold = 76 pts, weighting 20% M1 / 30% M2 / 50% M3). It contains **15 integrative questions**: each question **combines a minimum of two M3 lessons** (or lessons from two different modules in the case of meta-arc M1 -> M2 -> M3 questions). The answer key includes justifications referencing specific lessons and legal articles (Polish Civil Code, Polish Labour Code, Polish Technical Inspection Act, Polish Energy Law, PN-EN standards).

Used in two modes:

1. **Self-assessment mode**. The trainee solves it after the l8 test (if passed) to verify deeper integration of knowledge before the solo practical exam (LT70 oak FSC 12 m³ Bouwhout van Doorn, deadline +14 days). No impact on certification.
2. **Focused remedial mode**. A trainee who fails l8 solves selected quiz questions corresponding to areas with gaps (e.g. a trainee weak in block T3 does questions 9-12 integrating supervision + diagnosis + boundary of competence). There is no aggregate pass threshold here: the trainer reconstructs the gap diagnosis.

## Instructions

- **15 questions, 4 pts each = 60 pts maximum total**. Point distribution: 2 pts for the correct A/B/C/D selection + 2 pts for a written 2-4 sentence justification referencing a lesson or a legal provision.
- **13 integrative questions** (combining >= 2 M3 lessons) + **2 meta-arc questions** (M1 -> M2 -> M3 arc).
- Time: **60 minutes**. No consultations, no phone, no notes.
- If you do not know, write "I do not know" (0 pts, information for the trainer about the gap, no penalty).
- Write on paper with a pen. Corrections must be unambiguous (clear strikethrough + marking of the new answer).

## Answer key

### General scoring rubric

Each question is worth 4 pts, distributed as follows:
- **2 pts for the correct A/B/C/D selection** (binary: correct = 2, other = 0)
- **2 pts for the written justification**:
  - 2 pts = full justification, with a citation of the lesson + the legal provision/standard, contains the key fact
  - 1 pt = partial justification, missing the citation of the lesson or provision but the direction is correct
  - 0 pts = no justification, illogical justification, or copying of the question

### Model answers and justifications

**Q1: C**. Bearing knock + 10 Hz vibration in the handle + wavy cut = lower wheel runout > 0.5 mm (l8 T3, Rustam's case 17 June 2026). Hard external boundary: bearing replacement requires a hydraulic press + tamper-evident seal + UDT inspection of the press + Wood-Mizer service warranty. Crossing the boundary = voiding the warranty + OHS liability + KP art. 120 § 1 (damage to the machine). STOP, KKS-001, call to Marek, Marek to Mr. Krzysztof at Wood-Mizer Polska.

**Q2: A**. Scheme A cant sawing + stellite 7/8-A13 + 32 ft/min + 2200 PSI = standard parameters for fresh pine for a construction client (l5 T1 M3 + l1 T3 M3, Rustam's ZLE-077 case). Margin +600-900 zł against the average +/-2800 zł potential of parametric decisions (l6 T3). Scheme B (quarter sawing) is unnecessary for construction rafters (that is for furniture customers), scheme C is extremely risky (1900 PSI is too low for fresh pine), scheme D is excessively expensive.

**Q3: B**. KP art. 120 § 1: "an employee bears material liability within the limits of the actual loss". KP art. 120 § 2: "compensation may not exceed three months' remuneration". Cap at 3 monthly salaries (about 11000 zł). In practice 1/3 of the damage if unintentional = about 1400 zł deducted. Negative margin of 529 zł = sawmill economics, not damage attributable to the operator (l6 T3 M3). Intent under KP art. 122 § 1 = full damage, but there is no intent here (the operator misjudged parameters).

**Q4: B**. The M2 principle "the operator proposes an understanding, does not wait for instructions" (M2 paradigm, l1 T1). ISO 9001 documentation requires 15-min audit availability + completeness of fields. Signing an empty tolerance field = risk of a complaint where PN-EN 336 +/-2 mm is incorrectly applied. The "I see X, I understand Y, is that correct?" pattern protects both parties. (l1 T2 M3 KEP-001 + l1 T4 M3 batch documentation.)

**Q5: B**. Two independent facts: (a) the minus 529 zł margin results from small Ø 32 cm logs + Drew-Sus premium drying (economics, l6 T3); (b) the 174 mm complaint = non-material defect under KC art. 560 § 4 ("the buyer may not withdraw from the contract if the defect is non-material"). PN-EN 336 standard tolerance +/-2 mm, 174 vs 175 = 1 mm = non-material. Refusal of withdrawal, price reduction proposal (KC art. 560 § 1). EGIDA email template variant 1, non-material defect (l2 T4 M3, Dom-Bud case).

**Q6: B**. Two economic dimensions: (a) order margin = sawmill economics (l6 T3: 34% raw material + 19% labor + 17.5% processing + ...); (b) course ROI = personal economics of the operator (l6 T4: investment 3000 zł / 500 zł/month extra + 12-month amortization = ROI 5.7 months). They are independent. The loss on a single order does not block the investment in a course. Logic of 100 orders (average +340 zł/order) vs a one-off investment in competence (linear return 500 zł/month over years).

**Q7: B**. Drew-Sus premium 7-day cycle = customer contract (timeliness) + 50% surcharge = investment in the customer relationship (l6 T3 M3 economics). KEP-001 filled in with Ms. Ania = formal classification (l1 T2 M3). FSC chain of custody = FSC Connect register + labels + intake register (l3 T4 M3 audit). Monitoring the 8-10% final moisture for furniture-grade beech requires consultation with Mr. Henryk (drying-room master, l1-l4 T3 M3). PN-EN 13183-2: MC measurement at 4 positions.

**Q8: B**. EGIDA regulation § 8 sec. 3: "I correct when wrong, not when different from mine" (l7 T3 M3, Adrian case). Three substantive errors to correct during the shift (unsafe feed speed, guides not consistent with KDP parameters, log in pencil legally invalid). The stylistic difference (cutting order) is not subject to correction; Adrian has the right to his own method. KP art. 100 § 2 point 7 "to observe the order established at the workplace" does not impose a personal style. Score 21/25 = full authorization 30 days (l7 T3).

**Q9: C**. Three senses (l8 T3 M3) gave an unambiguous diagnosis: lower wheel runout > 0.5 mm. The EGIDA table of 7 fault types classifies this as service-level. Hard external boundary (l4 T1 M3): bearing replacement = hydraulic press + tamper-evident seal + Wood-Mizer service warranty. Crossing it = voiding the warranty (cost 8-15 thousand zł of repair + loss of warranty on the LT70 machine worth 180 thousand zł). STOP + KKS-001 + 4W to Marek + escalation to Mr. Krzysztof Wieliczko at Wood-Mizer Polska.

**Q10: B**. Prioritization after analysis (l7 T3 supervision + l8 T3 diagnosis): your LT70 is already on STOP (wheel runout), so there is no conflict with "Adrian's waviness". Waviness on a board at Adrian's = probably band dulling (operator-level, replacement) or wavy cut due to low tension (also band replacement). Pedagogical supervision: Adrian replaces it under your observation, he learns. Your entry in his KS-001. Then you return to LT70 and Mr. Krzysztof. You retain the supervisory role + service escalation in parallel.

**Q11: B**. KC art. 556¹ § 4: "a defect is material if it results in unsuitability for ordinary use or for the contractual purpose". KC art. 560 § 1: "the buyer may demand a price reduction or withdraw from the contract". KC art. 560 § 4: "the buyer may not withdraw from the contract if the defect is non-material". Polish Supreme Court ruling I NSNc 152/20 of 17 Feb 2021 confirms: a non-material defect is not a basis for withdrawal. PN-EN 336 standard tolerance +/-2 mm. 1 mm fits within it, defect non-material. Refusal of withdrawal + proposal of a 3-5% reduction (l2 T4 M3, Dom-Bud case).

**Q12: B**. FSC-C112233 chain of custody (l3 T4 M3, NEPCon audit): 8 documentation elements as a single chain. Inconsistency = penalty 150-300 thousand zł + loss of certificate.

**Q13: B**. Investment 3000 zł (UDT course 2500 + SEP 500). Extra salary 500 zł/month (350 zł UDT IIWJO forklift + 150 zł SEP G1-E electrician) per the EGIDA 2026 pay regulations. ROI = 3000/500 + 12-month amortization = 5.7 months simple payback, full financial equivalence in 11-12 months. KP art. 103¹ § 1: "the employer grants the employee training leave with the right to remuneration retained". Polish Technical Inspection Act 2000 (Dz.U. 2000 No. 122 item 1321) art. 12-13: UDT examination and entitlements to operate technical equipment. Polish Energy Law 1997 (Dz.U. 1997 No. 54 item 348) art. 54: SEP entitlements for operation of installations up to 1 kV. (l6 T4 M3 career planning.)

**Q14: B**. Meta-principle 1 from l7 M3: responsibility grows concentrically (body -> batch -> shift), not linearly. M1 = own body + colleagues' bodies within 5 m (3 STOP gestures, IARC group 1 dust, S3 boots, hearing protectors at 85 dB, 30 kg manual transport for a man, log yard 37% of fatalities). M2 = a 1-3 m³ batch under the tool (band 2200-2400 PSI, final MC 12-15% for construction, class A/B/C/D weakest-element rule, 8-field log book, 4W). M3 = an 8-hour shift with 15 people (4 communication channels, KAO-001, EGIDA-PZ-001, KC complaint, margin 5-15 thousand zł per day).

**Q15: B**. Meta-principle 3 from l7 M3: "from receiving instructions to issuing instructions". The oral instruction "stop, replace the band, drop to 62%" + the written KAO-001 = two-layer responsibility: (a) oral with supervisory force (Adrian obeys), (b) written with evidentiary force (KAO-001 in the personal file, 10-year retention, evidence for audit). Rustam went through: M1 receiver (Wahan: "pass the log") -> M2 partner (Damian teaches, Rustam asks) -> M3 sender (Rustam: "stop" + signature).

## For the trainer: how to grade

**Before the test**. Print the questions on double-sided A4 cards (7 cards for 15 questions + a blank page for justifications). Keep the key with the model justifications for yourself only.

**During the test**. 60 min for 15 questions = 4 min per question. Remind after 30 min "halfway", after 50 min "10 min remaining". No consultations, but technical questions are allowed ("do we write the KC article number with the paragraph?" - answer: as the trainee finds convenient, what counts is the content).

**After the test**. Grade per question (15 min per sheet x 15 questions = 3.75 h). Alternatively grade per M3 block (T1/T2/T3/T4/meta-arc) detecting patterns of gaps. Each trainee receives a sheet with the per-block result.

**Feedback**. A trainee with 48/60 receives a different recommendation than a trainee with 40/60 spread evenly. The first one supplements the specific block with the largest gap (e.g. T4 law). The second one needs a comprehensive M3 review. Threshold 40/60 (66%) = recommendation of targeted review before the practical exam. <32/60 (53%) = recommendation to delay the practical exam by 1-2 weeks and additional lessons on the weakest block.

**Correlation with the l8 test**. The supplementary quiz **does not replace** the 108-point test, but **predicts** readiness for the practical exam. A trainee who passes l8 with 78/108 + quiz 52/60 = ready. A trainee who passes l8 with 80/108 + quiz 32/60 = formally passed, but with integrative gaps, recommendation of 1 week of additional theoretical practice before the exam.
