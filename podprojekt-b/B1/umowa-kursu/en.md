## II. Subject matter of the Agreement

1. The Foundation, in cooperation with the Entrepreneur, will conduct the **practical course entitled „Praca w tartaku" (Work at the sawmill)** (hereinafter referred to as the „**Course**") for the benefit of the Course Participant.

2. The aim of the Course is to prepare the Course Participant to perform work at Polish sawmills and to enable the Course Participant to acquire in-demand qualifications on the Polish labour market, in particular in the area of wood processing for the needs of the construction, joinery and furniture industries.

3. The Course is organised in **three modules** of progressively increasing competence complexity:

| Module | Title | Duration | Competence position |
|--------|-------|----------|---------------------|
| **M1** | Auxiliary worker at the sawmill | 4 weeks (72 h) | helper performing tasks under supervision |
| **M2** | Sawmill equipment operator under supervision | 4 weeks (112 h) | operator performing tasks on an assigned machine under supervision |
| **M3** | Independent operator | 4 weeks (132 h) | operator independently making technological decisions and supervising persons at M1 and M2 levels |

In total, **316 didactic hours over 12 weeks** (approximately 3 calendar months), unless the parties agree on a different schedule in an annex to this Agreement.

4. Each module ends with a **separate certificate** of completion of the module, issued upon passing the modular test with a threshold of 70% of correct answers. After completing all three modules and passing the final test (threshold 70%, i.e. 76 points out of 108), the Course Participant receives a **certificate of completion of the Course**.

---

## III. Organisational structure of the Course

The Course consists of three didactic components, the location of which depends on organisational conditions:

| Component | Character | Location |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **Theoretical part** | lectures, discussions, debates, written tests | training room of the Foundation in Opole (ul. Ozimska 14-16); admissibly the workshop of the Foundation or a room at the Entrepreneur's plant, provided that organisational conditions are ensured |
| **Workshop part** | simple practical exercises, demonstrations, instructional sessions | workshop of the Foundation in Opole; admissibly the Entrepreneur's plant, provided that organisational conditions are ensured |
| **Core practical part** | performance of actual sawmill tasks under supervision | **always at the Entrepreneur's sawmill plant** (address as in section I) |

The core practical part performed at a real sawmill is the essence of the Course and cannot be replaced by simulation or workshop exercises. This expresses the principle adopted by the Foundation that the proper integration of foreigners into Polish society requires equipping them with real, verifiable competences to perform work in compliance with the law and consistent with the socio-economic needs of Poland.

## V. Obligations of the parties

### 1. The Foundation undertakes to:

a) provide didactic staff (trainers, coordinator, classifiers) with qualifications adequate to the scope of the Course,

b) make available a training room and workshop in Opole for the purposes of the theoretical and workshop parts,

c) develop and make available didactic materials in four language versions: Polish (PL), English (EN), Spanish (ES) and Ukrainian (UK),

d) conduct the modular tests and the final test, and issue the certificates referred to in section II paragraph 4,

e) insure the Course Participant against the consequences of accidents for the duration of the Course, unless the Entrepreneur covers the Course Participant with its own insurance on the terms applicable at its plant,

f) maintain documentation of the course of the Course (attendance lists, assessment cards, test protocols).

### 2. The Entrepreneur undertakes to:

a) make work stations available to the Course Participant at the sawmill plant indicated in section I, to the extent necessary for the performance of the core practical part,

b) provide the Course Participant with supervision by a person possessing competences at least at the M3 level of this Course or equivalent (e.g. foreman, master craftsman, experienced operator with documented work record),

c) provide the Course Participant with personal protective equipment (PPE / Personal Protective Equipment) consistent with the occupational risk assessment for the given work station,

d) carry out a workplace OSH (occupational safety and health) instruction before allowing the Course Participant to perform tasks,

e) comply with the provisions of the Polish Labour Code and OSH regulations with respect to the Course Participant on terms analogous to those applicable to the Entrepreneur's own employees, with regard to working time, breaks and safety,

f) refrain from employing the Course Participant in overtime hours during the Course and from issuing instructions to the Course Participant that go beyond the didactic scope of the module in which the Course Participant is currently engaged.

### 3. The Course Participant undertakes to:

a) participate in the Course classes systematically and punctually,

b) comply with the Course regulations and with the work regulations applicable at the Entrepreneur's premises,

c) comply with OSH and fire safety regulations and with workplace instructions,

d) use personal protective equipment in accordance with the recommendations,

e) maintain the confidentiality of the commercial and technological information of the Entrepreneur of which the Course Participant becomes aware during the Course,

f) immediately report to the Foundation's coordinator and to the supervising person at the Entrepreneur's premises any situation threatening health or life, any OSH incident, and any organisational or communication difficulty that hinders the performance of the Course.

---

## VI. Gratuitous nature of the Course

1. **The Course is free of charge for the Course Participant.** The Course Participant does not bear any fees for participation in any of the modules or for the issuance of certificates.

2. The Course Participant is not entitled to any remuneration on account of participation in the Course, in particular on account of performing tasks during the core practical part, unless the parties conclude a separate employment contract, contract of mandate or graduate apprenticeship contract.

3. Mutual settlements between the Foundation and the Entrepreneur are governed by a separate **agreement on co-organisation of the practical course**, which does not form part of this Agreement.

---

## VII. Legal basis and status of the Course

The Course is conducted as part of the **statutory activity of the Foundation**, on the basis of the provisions of its statute (the statutory objectives of the Foundation have been made available for inspection on the website: https://fundacjaegida.eu), in particular on the basis of:

- Article 3 paragraph 1 in conjunction with Article 4 paragraph 1 items 2, 5a and 8 of the Polish Act of 24 April 2003 on Public Benefit Activity and Volunteerism (oryg. „ustawa o działalności pożytku publicznego i o wolontariacie"; consolidated text Dz.U. 2024, item 1491, as amended),
- Article 93 of the Polish Act of 7 September 1991 on the Education System (oryg. „ustawa o systemie oświaty"; consolidated text Dz.U. 2024, item 750, as amended), to the extent that the Course prepares for the recognition of qualifications obtained outside Poland,

and on the basis of the agreement on co-organisation of the Course concluded between the Foundation and the Entrepreneur, referred to in section VI paragraph 3.

## VIII. Personal data protection

1. The controller of the Course Participant's personal data within the scope of the performance of this Agreement is Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA, with its registered office in Opole, ul. Ozimska 14-16/314A.

2. The Course Participant's personal data are processed for the purpose of carrying out the Course, issuing certificates and fulfilling the Foundation's statistical and reporting obligations, on the basis of Article 6 paragraph 1 letter b and letter f of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (GDPR).

3. Detailed information on the processing of personal data is contained in the **GDPR information clause**, which constitutes an annex to this Agreement.

4. The Entrepreneur processes the Course Participant's personal data to the extent and for the purpose necessary to perform the core practical part, in particular for the purposes of OSH instruction, attendance records and supervision of work safety, acting as a separate controller in this respect.

## IX. Final provisions

1. This Agreement enters into force on the day of its signature by all three parties.

2. Any amendments to the Agreement require written form, on pain of nullity.

3. In matters not regulated by this Agreement, the provisions of the Polish Civil Code and the provisions of the statutes referred to in section VII shall apply.

4. Disputes arising out of this Agreement shall be settled by the parties amicably, and in the absence of an agreement, before the common court competent for the registered office of the Foundation.

5. The Agreement has been drawn up in three identical counterparts, one for each of the parties.
