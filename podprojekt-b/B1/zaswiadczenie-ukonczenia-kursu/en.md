## II. Data of the Completed Course

**Course Title**: „Praca w tartaku" (Work in a Sawmill), a course preparing participants to perform work in Polish sawmills and to acquire qualifications in deficit in the Polish labor market, in particular in the area of wood processing for the needs of construction, joinery and the furniture industry.

**Total Volume of the Course**:

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Number of modules: | 3 (M1, M2, M3) |
| Total duration: | 12 weeks |
| Total number of teaching hours: | 316 teaching hours |
| Form of participation: | on-site, in the full modular scope M1 + M2 + M3 |

**Graduate competence profile** (achieved jointly after the three modules):

| Module | Vocational profile | Volume | Scope of competences achieved |
|--------|--------------------|--------|--------------------------------|
| **M1** | Auxiliary worker in a sawmill | 4 weeks / 72 teaching hours | recognition of wood raw material, basics of occupational health and safety in the sawmill facility, marking and moving of material, auxiliary handling of simple manipulation and warehousing operations, basics of vocational communication in a Polish-speaking environment |
| **M2** | Operator of sawmill machinery under supervision | 4 weeks / 112 teaching hours | supervised operation of basic sawmill machines (band saws, circular saws, planers), control of cutting parameters and material quality, application of occupational health and safety procedures when working with mechanical devices, keeping of workshop documentation, cooperation within a production team |
| **M3** | Independent operator | 4 weeks / 132 teaching hours | independent operation of sawmill machinery throughout the full production cycle, planning and optimization of the cutting process, quality control and material sorting, diagnostics of simple malfunctions, supervision of auxiliary workers, knowledge of sawmill material quality standards applied in Poland |

This certificate confirms the **completion of the entire Course** in the full modular scope (M1 + M2 + M3) and the successful passing of the final test of the Course.

## VIII. Purpose of the Certificate

This certificate constitutes a **certificate of professional competences** of the Course Participant on the Polish labor market, in particular in the sawmill, timber and related wood-processing industries serving the needs of construction, joinery and the furniture industry, and is issued for the purposes of:

a) presentation to competent public administration authorities, in particular authorities competent in matters of residence and employment of foreigners in the territory of the Republic of Poland,

b) confirmation of completion of the entire Course before employers, labor market institutions and other entities requesting such confirmation, in particular in order to demonstrate competences within the full scope of the vocational profiles M1 + M2 + M3,

c) the internal documentation of the Course kept by the Foundation and the Entrepreneur,

d) the Course Participant's application for employment with entrepreneurs conducting sawmill, timber or joinery activity in the territory of the Republic of Poland.

The certificate does not confirm the acquisition of vocational qualifications within the meaning of separate regulations (in particular within the meaning of the Integrated Qualifications System), but **confirms the attendance and successful passing** of the three-module practical course in the total volume of 316 teaching hours.
