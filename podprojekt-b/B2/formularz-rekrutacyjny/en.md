# CANDIDATE RECRUITMENT FORM

**Practical course "Praca w tartaku" ("Work in a Sawmill")**: a vocational course for foreigners legally residing in the territory of the Republic of Poland, organised free of charge by Fundacja pomocy prawnej EGIDA (EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation).

---

## Preliminary information for the candidate

This form is to be completed by a person who wishes to take part in the practical course "Praca w tartaku" ("Work in a Sawmill") (hereinafter: the "**Course**"). The completion and submission of the form does not yet constitute the conclusion of any contract or any obligation to take part in the Course. The form serves:

a) as an invitation to the candidate to conclude the Course Agreement (Article 71 of the Polish Civil Code), that is, as a preliminary assessment of whether the candidate satisfies the conditions of participation set out in point IV of the Course Regulations and whether the candidacy fits within the limit of places for the given cycle of the Course;

b) the collection of data needed for the subsequent drafting of the Course Agreement, of the GDPR Information Notice and of any certificates (if the candidate is qualified);

c) the proper qualification of the candidate to one of the three Modules of the Course (M1, M2 or M3) on the basis of the declared professional experience.

**The Course is free of charge for the Course Participant.** The submission of the form does not entail any fees. The Foundation's decision on the admission of the candidate will be communicated by the means indicated by the candidate in this form within 30 days of the date of receipt of the complete form by the Foundation.

## Part G. State of health

The Course includes the **principal practical part** performed at a real sawmill establishment, that is, physical work under conditions of noise and dust, in the vicinity of sawmill machinery, with the necessity of using personal protective equipment. In this part of the Course the full OHS regime applies, analogous to that applicable to the workers of the sawmill.

This form **does not require** the candidate to provide detailed information on the state of health or a list of illnesses. It requires only a general declaration of readiness for physical work, on the principle of data minimisation in accordance with Article 5(1)(c) GDPR.

A detailed verification of the medical fitness for work under sawmill conditions takes place at a later stage, on the basis of a separate **medical certificate** of the absence of contraindications to work in the wood industry. The candidate provides this certificate to the Foundation no later than before the commencement of Module M2, at his or her own cost or, if organisational conditions allow, in the manner indicated by the Foundation.

**General declaration of the candidate concerning the state of health**:

> I declare that, to the best of my knowledge, I have no inherent contraindications to performing physical work under sawmill conditions (work in a standing position, lifting and carrying wooden elements, work in noise and dust, use of personal protective equipment). I understand that the final assessment of medical fitness to participate in the principal practical part of the Course will be made on the basis of a medical certificate which I will provide to the Foundation no later than before the commencement of Module M2.
