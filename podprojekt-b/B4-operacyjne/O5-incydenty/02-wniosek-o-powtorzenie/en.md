# REQUEST FOR RETAKE OF A TEST OR MODULE

**Practical Course "Sawmill Work"**: a vocational course for foreign nationals legally residing on the territory of the Republic of Poland, organised free of charge by the EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation.

---

## Introductory note

This request serves the **Course Participant's application for another attempt at an assessment tool** (modular test, final test) or for **repetition of the cycle of a selected Module** in a subsequent edition of the Course. The request is submitted **within 14 days** from the date of the negative test result or the decision finding non-completion of a Module. The coordinator decides on retake matters independently; in matters concerning repetition of a whole Module in a subsequent edition, the decision is taken by the coordinator after consultation with the Foundation's management board.

## Note on the project

The course "Sawmill Work" was created as a result of the potential developed under the project "Building the foundations of support for foreign nationals in the labour market in the Opolskie Voivodeship", carried out by the SMART Association (lead partner) in cooperation with the EGIDA Legal Aid Foundation (partner) in 2026-2027 from the resources of the European Social Fund Plus. The Course itself is not financed from project resources and does not constitute a project activity; it is an independent statutory undertaking of the Foundation, conducted free of charge for the benefit of foreign nationals.
